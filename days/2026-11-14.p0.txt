<section class="block"><p class="en">BBC File on 4 Investigates has discovered that four planned &quot;hyperscale&quot; data centres - including three in the south of England - are already considering using gas-fired power to meet their electricity needs, creating more CO2 than the cities of Manchester, Liverpool and Southampton combined.
