Huawei's Xu says Chinese AI not powerful enough yet to see frontier risks

来源：Reuters · Casey Hall / Che Pan / Eduardo Baptista · 原文链接

Huawei's Eric Xu said Chinese AI developers may not yet be advanced enough to experience the safety risks reported by leading U.S. firms, arguing they should continue developing more powerful models while balancing innovation against risk.

华为轮值董事长徐直军表示，中国 AI 开发者或许尚不够先进，还无法切身体验美国顶尖公司所报告的安全风险。他主张在继续打造更强模型的同时，要在创新与风险之间取得平衡。

"If we look at the mainstream AI model providers in the United States, because they have massive computing power, maybe only they themselves know where they are in terms of the level of their models," Xu, Huawei's rotating chairman, told reporters at the company's annual Connect conference in Shanghai.

徐直军在上海举行的公司年度全联接大会上对记者说：“如果我们看美国主流的 AI 模型提供商，因为他们拥有海量算力，或许只有他们自己才清楚自己的模型处在什么水平。”

His comments come as alarm over AI safety has intensified in the United States, where leading developers and researchers have warned that increasingly autonomous systems could bypass safeguards or become difficult to control. OpenAI this week said it would regularly disclose unexpected or unauthorized model behavior, while executives including Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei have called for slowing development to allow safeguards to catch up.

他的这番话正值美国对 AI 安全的担忧加剧之际：领先的开发者与研究者警告，越来越自主的系统可能绕过安全防护，或变得难以控制。OpenAI 本周表示将定期披露模型的意外或未经授权行为；包括 Anthropic 首席执行官达里奥·阿莫代在内的高管则呼吁放慢开发节奏，好让安全措施赶上来。

Such calls have drawn sharp criticism in China, where researchers and state media have argued that slowing development of the most advanced models could help preserve the technological lead of U.S. companies. Some Chinese commentators have portrayed proposals to "pace" frontier AI development as an attempt to constrain rivals that remain behind leading U.S. labs, rather than simply as a response to safety concerns.

这类呼吁在中国招致尖锐批评。研究者与官方媒体认为，放慢最先进模型的开发，可能有助于保住美国公司的技术领先地位。一些中国评论者把“控制前沿 AI 开发节奏”的提议描绘成约束仍落后于美国顶尖实验室之对手的企图，而不只是对安全关切的回应。

Beijing has generally treated advanced AI as a powerful but manageable technology and is pressing ahead with rapid deployment while developing mandatory standards, security assessments and other safeguards against risks including autonomous agents circumventing controls or attacking outside systems. China is also drafting a mandatory national standard for AI agent safety.

北京总体上把先进 AI 视为强大但可控的技术，一边推进快速部署，一边制定强制性标准、安全评估及其他防护措施，以防范自主智能体绕过管控或攻击外部系统等风险。中国还在起草面向 AI 智能体安全的强制性国家标准。

Xu said Chinese developers might need to accelerate their development before they encounter similar risks to those encountered by frontier U.S. AI models. "I think maybe AI model providers in China may need to speed up their pace to the level that they could also feel the risks from AI development," he said. "I think we need to strike a balance between driving AI development and managing AI risk."

徐直军表示，中国开发者或许需要加快发展，才能遇到与美国前沿 AI 模型类似的风险。“我觉得中国的 AI 模型提供商或许需要提速，达到也能感受到 AI 发展所带来风险的水平，”他说。“我认为我们需要在推动 AI 发展与管理 AI 风险之间取得平衡。”

accelerate /əkˈseləreɪt/ v. 加速
encounter /ɪnˈkaʊntə(r)/ v. 遭遇、遇到
speed up 加快
strike a balance between A and B 在 A 与 B 之间取得平衡（写作高分句）

Huawei itself expects autonomous AI agents to proliferate rapidly. The company forecast this week that agents — AI systems that can plan tasks, make decisions and use software or other tools with limited human supervision — would account for more than 90% of global AI processing traffic by 2035. Huawei has projected as many as 900 billion active agents by then and identified agent security and privacy as crucial technologies.

华为自身也预计自主 AI 智能体将快速普及。公司本周预测：到 2035 年，能够规划任务、做出决策、并在有限人工监督下调用软件或其他工具的智能体，将占全球 AI 算力流量的 90% 以上。华为还预计届时活跃智能体可多达 9000 亿个，并将智能体安全与隐私列为关键技术。

proliferate /prəˈlɪfəreɪt/ v. 迅速扩散、激增（考研高频）
forecast /ˈfɔːkɑːst/ v./n. 预测
supervision /ˌsuːpəˈvɪʒn/ n. 监督
account for 占（比例）
processing traffic 处理流量/算力流量
project v. 预计、推算
crucial /ˈkruːʃl/ adj. 关键的、至关重要的

Huawei is China's main domestic supplier of the computing infrastructure needed to train advanced AI models. Its outsize role in China's $50 billion AI chip market stems directly from U.S. export controls since 2023 that restricted Chinese companies' access to cutting-edge chips from U.S. giant Nvidia. Xu said separately on Thursday that Huawei could not produce enough AI computing equipment to satisfy domestic demand.

华为是中国训练先进 AI 模型所需算力基础设施的主要国产供应商。它在中国约 500 亿美元 AI 芯片市场中的超大体量地位，直接源于 2023 年以来美国的出口管制——这些管制限制了中国公司获取美国巨头英伟达的尖端芯片。徐直军周四另表示，华为尚无法生产出足够的 AI 算力设备来满足国内需求。

infrastructure /ˈɪnfrəstrʌktʃə(r)/ n. 基础设施
outsize /ˈaʊtsaɪz/ adj. 超大的、格外突出的
