<section class="block"><p class="en">The success of DeepSeek turned its CEO Liang Wenfeng into a national hero and &quot;is worth 10 billion yuan of advertising for [China&#x27;s] AI industry,&quot; he added. &quot;It has let the public know that AI is not just a concept, that it can indeed change people&#x27;s lives. It has inspired public curiosity.&quot; Six homegrown AI firms, including DeepSeek, have now been nicknamed China&#x27;s six little dragons by the internet – the others are Unitree Robotics, Deep Robotics, BrainCo, Game Science, and Manycore Tech.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">DeepSeek 的成功使其 CEO 梁文锋成为民族英雄，并「值上百亿元为中国 AI 产业做广告」，他补充道。「它让公众知道 AI 不只是概念，确实能改变人们的生活，激发了公众好奇心。」包括 DeepSeek 在内的六家本土 AI 公司，现被网民戏称为中国「六小龙」——其余是宇树科技、云深处科技、强脑科技、游戏科学与美宅科技（Manycore）。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>turn ... into</strong> 把……变成</div><div class="item"><strong>national hero</strong> 民族英雄</div><div class="item"><strong>be worth</strong> 值……、相当于</div><div class="item"><strong>advertising</strong> n. 广告（活动）</div><div class="item"><strong>concept</strong> n. 概念</div><div class="item"><strong>indeed</strong> ad. 确实、的确</div><div class="item"><strong>inspire</strong> v. 激发、鼓舞</div><div class="item"><strong>curiosity</strong> n. 好奇心</div><div class="item"><strong>homegrown</strong> a. 本土的、国产的</div><div class="item"><strong>nickname</strong> v. 给……起绰号；n. 绰号</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">AI is hungry for data - the more it gets, the smarter it makes itself and, with around a billion mobile phone users compared to just over 400 million in the US, Beijing has a real advantage. The West and many experts believe that data gathered by Chinese apps such as DeepSeek, RedNote or TikTok can be accessed by the Chinese Communist Party. Some point to the country&#x27;s National Intelligence Law as evidence. Chinese firms, including ByteDance, say the law allows for the protection of private companies and personal data. Still, suspicion drove Washington&#x27;s decision to ban TikTok; South Korea banned new downloads of DeepSeek, while Taiwan and Australia have barred the app from government-issued devices.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">AI 渴望数据——得到越多，把自己变得越聪明；中国约有十亿手机用户，对比美国刚过四亿，北京确有优势。西方与许多专家认为，DeepSeek、小红书或 TikTok 等中国应用采集的数据可被中国共产党获取；有人援引《国家情报法》为证。包括字节跳动在内的中国企业则称，该法允许保护民营企业与个人数据。尽管如此，疑虑推动华盛顿决定封禁 TikTok；韩国禁止新下载 DeepSeek，台湾与澳大利亚则禁止政府配发设备安装该应用。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>hungry for</strong> 渴望、渴求</div><div class="item"><strong>the more ... the smarter</strong> 越……越聪明</div><div class="item"><strong>advantage</strong> n. 优势</div><div class="item"><strong>gather</strong> v. 采集、收集</div><div class="item"><strong>access</strong> v. 获取、接入；n. 通道</div><div class="item"><strong>point to</strong> 指向、援引……为证</div><div class="item"><strong>evidence</strong> n. 证据</div><div class="item"><strong>allow for</strong> 容许、考虑到；使……成为可能</div><div class="item"><strong>suspicion</strong> n. 怀疑、疑虑</div><div class="item"><strong>drive</strong> v. 推动、驱使；驾驶</div><div class="item"><strong>ban</strong> v./n. 禁止、禁令</div><div class="item"><strong>bar ... from</strong> 禁止……接触/使用</div><div class="item"><strong>government-issued</strong> a. 政府配发的</div><div class="item"><strong>device</strong> n. 设备、装置</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">Chinese companies are aware of these sensitivities. Beijing also realises that this will be a challenge in its bid to be a global leader in AI. But China&#x27;s AI firms believe thrifty innovation will win them an undeniable advantage – because it was DeepSeek&#x27;s claim that it could rival ChatGPT for a fraction of the cost that shocked the AI industry. So the engineering challenge is how to make more, for less. Xi has repeatedly declared &quot;technological self-reliance&quot; a key goal. The Beijing Daily recently warned that the DeepSeek moment was not a time for &quot;AI triumphalism&quot; because China was still in &quot;catch-up mode&quot;. President Xi is investing heavily in artificial intelligence, robots and advanced tech in preparation for a marathon that he hopes China will eventually win.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">中国企业清楚这些敏感点。北京也意识到，这会成为其争夺全球 AI 领导地位路上的挑战。但中国 AI 公司相信，精打细算的创新会为它们赢得无可否认的优势——正是 DeepSeek 声称能以远低于 ChatGPT 的成本与之匹敌，震惊了 AI 业界。因此工程挑战在于：如何用更少投入做出更多。习近平反复将「科技自立自强」宣示为关键目标。《北京日报》近日警告，DeepSeek 时刻并非「AI 必胜论」之时，因中国仍处「追赶模式」。习近平正大举投资人工智能、机器人与先进科技，为他希望中国最终跑赢的马拉松做准备。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>aware of</strong> 意识到、清楚</div><div class="item"><strong>sensitivity</strong> n. 敏感点、敏感性</div><div class="item"><strong>realise</strong> v. 意识到（美式 realize）</div><div class="item"><strong>challenge</strong> n. 挑战；v. 质疑</div><div class="item"><strong>global leader</strong> 全球领导者</div><div class="item"><strong>thrifty</strong> a. 节俭的、精打细算的</div><div class="item"><strong>innovation</strong> n. 创新</div><div class="item"><strong>undeniable</strong> a. 无可否认的</div><div class="item"><strong>rival</strong> v. 与……匹敌；n. 对手</div><div class="item"><strong>a fraction of</strong> ……的一小部分</div><div class="item"><strong>self-reliance</strong> n. 自立、自力更生</div><div class="item"><strong>triumphalism</strong> n. 必胜论、必胜心态</div><div class="item"><strong>catch-up mode</strong> 追赶模式</div><div class="item"><strong>invest heavily in</strong> 大举投资于</div><div class="item"><strong>in preparation for</strong> 为……做准备</div><div class="item"><strong>marathon</strong> n. 马拉松；喻：持久战</div><div class="item"><strong>eventually</strong> ad. 最终、终于</div></div></div></div></section>
