<section class="block"><p class="en">In early 2024, a young man in the city of Huangshi, south-eastern China, reportedly posted a message on the Chinese version of TikTok, Douyin. The caption read: &quot;Farewell to this world.&quot; Within minutes, the suicide prevention team at Douyin kicked into gear. The team, which is staffed 24 hours a day, shared the man&#x27;s posts with five police stations in Huangshi. After geolocating one of his videos to a local park, police found him in emotional distress and intervened before he was able to take his own life.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">2024 年初，中国东南部黄石市一名年轻男子据称在抖音上发帖，配文是：「告别这个世界。」几分钟内，抖音的自杀干预团队便行动起来。该团队全天候值班，把他的帖子分享给黄石的五个派出所。在定位到其中一条视频来自当地公园后，警方发现他情绪崩溃，并在他采取极端行为前介入。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>reportedly</strong> ad. 据称、据说</div><div class="item"><strong>caption</strong> n. 配文、说明文字</div><div class="item"><strong>kick into gear</strong> 开始运转、立即行动起来</div><div class="item"><strong>staffed</strong> a. 配备人员的；v. 为……配备人手</div><div class="item"><strong>geolocate</strong> v. 进行地理定位</div><div class="item"><strong>emotional distress</strong> 情绪困扰、心理痛苦</div><div class="item"><strong>intervene</strong> v. 介入、干预</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">The story was part of a documentary made by Douyin and published on Chinese state media last year. It claimed that Douyin&#x27;s suicide prevention team had successfully intervened in 400 cases of attempted suicide in the first half of 2025 alone. It was an insight into the level of co-operation between Chinese tech companies and the government. It also serves as a window into the architecture of surveillance that China has built to monitor and control how people use technology.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">这个故事出自抖音制作、去年在中国官方媒体发布的纪录片。片中称，仅 2025 年上半年，抖音自杀干预团队就成功介入了 400 起自杀未遂案例。它揭示了中国科技公司与政府之间的合作程度，也打开了一扇窗，让人看到中国为监控并管控人们如何使用技术而搭建的监控架构。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>documentary</strong> n. 纪录片</div><div class="item"><strong>state media</strong> 官方媒体、国家媒体</div><div class="item"><strong>attempted suicide</strong> 自杀未遂</div><div class="item"><strong>insight into</strong> 对……的洞见/深入了解</div><div class="item"><strong>co-operation</strong> n. 合作（美式 cooperation）</div><div class="item"><strong>architecture</strong> n. 架构、结构；建筑学</div><div class="item"><strong>surveillance</strong> n. 监控、监视</div><div class="item"><strong>monitor</strong> v. 监控；n. 显示器</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">On 15 July this year, that control was extended to the realm of relationships and artificial intelligence (AI) when China brought in the most far-reaching regulations in the world on &quot;human-like&quot; AI chatbots. China&#x27;s new regulation puts strict limitations on any AI that can have a &quot;sustained emotional interaction&quot; with a human.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">今年 7 月 15 日，这种管控延伸到了人际关系与人工智能（AI）领域：中国出台了全球针对「类人」AI 聊天机器人最为深远的监管规定。新规对任何能与人进行「持续情感互动」的 AI 施加严格限制。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>extend to</strong> 延伸到、扩展至</div><div class="item"><strong>realm</strong> n. 领域、范围</div><div class="item"><strong>bring in</strong> 出台、引入（法规等）</div><div class="item"><strong>far-reaching</strong> a. 影响深远的</div><div class="item"><strong>regulation</strong> n. 法规、监管规定</div><div class="item"><strong>human-like</strong> a. 类人的、像人的</div><div class="item"><strong>limitation</strong> n. 限制</div><div class="item"><strong>sustained</strong> a. 持续的、持久的</div><div class="item"><strong>emotional interaction</strong> 情感互动</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">US President Donald Trump and his Chinese counterpart Xi Jinping met last week amid dire warnings that AI could lead to human extinction. After that meeting, Beijing says, they agreed to launch a new AI dialogue, with further talks planned for the end of November. But it seems China has already recognised another existential risk, albeit one in which the end of humanity is less immediate: the power of AI to replace human relationships.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">美国总统唐纳德·特朗普与中国国家主席习近平上周会晤，其间正值「AI 可能导致人类灭绝」的严厉警告。北京称，会后双方同意开启新的 AI 对话，并计划于 11 月底继续谈。但中国似乎已意识到另一种生存性风险——虽不那么「立即终结人类」，却同样重大：AI 取代人际关系的力量。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>counterpart</strong> n. 对等人物、对应方</div><div class="item"><strong>amid</strong> prep. 在……之中</div><div class="item"><strong>dire</strong> a. 可怕的、严峻的</div><div class="item"><strong>extinction</strong> n. 灭绝</div><div class="item"><strong>launch</strong> v. 启动、发起</div><div class="item"><strong>dialogue</strong> n. 对话</div><div class="item"><strong>existential risk</strong> 生存性风险</div><div class="item"><strong>albeit</strong> conj. 尽管、虽然</div><div class="item"><strong>immediate</strong> a. 立即的；直接的</div><div class="item"><strong>replace</strong> v. 取代、替换</div></div></div></div></section>
