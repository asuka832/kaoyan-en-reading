Alibaba deepens AI push with new chip, bigger model; shares jump 5%

阿里巴巴加码 AI：发布新芯片、规划更大模型，股价涨约 5%

来源：Reuters · 2026-09-22 · 原文链接

Alibaba Group said on Tuesday it was developing an AI model up to four times larger than the company's flagship model and unveiled a next-generation AI chip it called China's most powerful, sending shares up 5%.

阿里巴巴集团周二表示，正在开发一款规模可达其旗舰模型四倍的人工智能模型，并发布了其称为中国最强的新一代 AI 芯片，股价随之上涨约 5%。

flagship adj./n. 旗舰的；旗舰产品
unveil v. 发布、揭晓
next-generation adj. 新一代的
sending shares up 推动股价上涨

The launch comes as Chinese technology companies race to develop domestic alternatives to Nvidia's AI processors amid tightening US export curbs.

这一发布正值中国科技企业竞相开发英伟达人工智能处理器的国产替代方案，同时美国出口限制持续收紧。

race to 竞相、争相去做
domestic alternative 国产替代方案
processor n. 处理器
amid prep. 在……之中/背景下
export curb 出口限制

The announcements at Alibaba Cloud's annual Apsara conference in Hangzhou underscore the Chinese technology giant's push to build out every layer of its AI business, from foundation models and semiconductors to data centres needed to train and deploy increasingly powerful AI systems.

在杭州举行的阿里云年度云栖大会上，这些公告凸显出这家中国科技巨头正着力打造 AI 业务的每一层——从基础模型、半导体，到训练与部署日益强大的 AI 系统所需的数据中心。

underscore v. 强调、突显
build out 全面铺开、扩建
foundation model 基础模型
semiconductor n. 半导体
deploy v. 部署

Wu said Alibaba's Qwen team plans to train a new model with 5 trillion to 10 trillion parameters, to tackle "more complex, longer-horizon tasks" as it pursues artificial superintelligence — one that surpasses human capabilities. Alibaba's current flagship, Qwen 3.8 Max, has 2.4 trillion parameters.

吴泳铭表示，阿里通义千问（Qwen）团队计划训练参数量达 5 万亿至 10 万亿的新模型，以应对“更复杂、更长周期的任务”，并追求超越人类能力的人工超级智能。当前旗舰模型 Qwen 3.8 Max 参数量为 2.4 万亿。

parameter n. 参数（衡量模型规模）
tackle v. 处理、应对
longer-horizon 更长周期/更长远视距的
pursue v. 追求
surpass v. 超过、超越

Alibaba also unveiled the Zhenwu V900, a new AI chip developed by its T-Head semiconductor unit. Wu said the processor delivers three times the performance of its predecessor, the M890, and can be linked in clusters of up to 500,000 chips to train and run the largest AI models.

阿里还发布了由其平头哥半导体部门研发的新 AI 芯片“镇武 V900”。吴泳铭称，该处理器性能为其前代 M890 的三倍，并可组成多达 50 万颗芯片的集群，用于训练和运行最大规模的 AI 模型。

unit n. 部门、单元
deliver v. 提供、实现（性能）
predecessor n. 前代产品、前任
cluster n. 集群
up to 多达、最多

The chip is set for mass production and commercial release in the first quarter of 2027, and Wu said the company expected "significant growth" in annual AI chip shipments.

该芯片计划于 2027 年第一季度量产并商业发布；吴泳铭表示，公司预期年度 AI 芯片出货量将有“显著增长”。

be set for 计划在……进行/定于
mass production 大规模量产
commercial release 商业发布
shipment n. 出货、发货量

Wu also set a target for Alibaba Cloud's global data centre capacity to surpass 20 gigawatts by 2032. Customer demand for AI was "exceptionally robust" and was accelerating Alibaba Cloud's revenue growth, he added, though supply chain constraints limit the pace of expansion.

吴泳铭还为阿里云设定目标：到 2032 年全球数据中心容量超过 20 吉瓦。他补充说，客户对 AI 的需求“异常强劲”，正在加速阿里云营收增长，但供应链约束会限制扩张速度。

capacity n. 容量、产能
gigawatt n. 吉瓦（功率单位）
exceptionally robust 异常强劲的
revenue growth 营收增长
constraint n. 约束、限制因素
pace of expansion 扩张节奏/速度
