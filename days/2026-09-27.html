China builds national computing network as key pillar of new infrastructure

中国加速建设全国算力网：数字时代新基建的关键支柱

来源：Xinhua · 2026-09-21 · 原文链接

BEIJING, Sept. 21 (Xinhua) -- As artificial intelligence (AI) and large-scale data processing reshape the global tech landscape, China is accelerating the construction of a nationwide computing power network as a cornerstone of the country's future infrastructure. The computing power network has been incorporated into the national "six networks" infrastructure framework as the 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-2030) enters its first year, marking the industry's shift toward high-quality development, said Wang Jinping, president of the China Association of Information, at an industry conference.

新华社北京 9 月 21 日电——随着人工智能与大规模数据处理重塑全球科技格局，中国正加快建设全国性算力网络，将其作为未来基础设施的基石。中国信息协会会长王金平在一次行业会议上表示，随着“十五五”规划（2026—2030）进入开局之年，算力网已被纳入国家“六网”基础设施框架，标志着该产业正转向高质量发展。

Formalized by a top-level decision in April 2026, the computing power network -- alongside the networks of water, next-generation power grids, next-generation telecommunications, underground urban pipelines and logistics -- has become as indispensable to the digital age as water and electricity. It is expected to unlock a new wave of strategic development opportunities.

经 2026 年 4 月一项高层决策正式确立，算力网与水网、新型电网、新一代电信网、地下管网及物流网并列，已成为数字时代如同水电一般不可或缺的基础设施。预计它将释放新一轮战略发展机遇。

As of June 2026, the country's intelligent computing capacity reached 2,185 EFLOPS -- each unit representing 1 quintillion floating-point operations per second -- up 177 percent year over year. More than 70 computing power transmission corridors linking major computing hubs and key regions have been built, and 17 national computing interconnection regional nodes have been approved. Building on this momentum, China is now accelerating the construction of an integrated national computing power network, forming an "8+10+3" spatial layout centered on eight national computing hubs, 10 national computing clusters and three computing-electricity coordination zones.

截至 2026 年 6 月，中国智能算力规模达 2185 EFLOPS（每单位代表每秒 100 京次浮点运算），同比增长 177%。已建成连接主要算力枢纽与重点区域的算力传输通道逾 70 条，并批复 17 个国家算力互联区域节点。在此基础上，中国正加快建设一体化全国算力网，形成以八大国家算力枢纽、十大国家算力集群和三大算电协同区为核心的“8+10+3”空间布局。

To centrally pool the nation's computing resources, a national computing service platform is accelerating its expansion, steadily connecting platforms across 31 provincial-level regions. Its marketplace has attracted more than 10,000 enterprise users, over 200 service providers, more than 2,000 computing products and over 300 large models, while offering developers computing access services and accumulating billions of monitoring data records.

为统筹汇聚全国算力资源，国家算力服务平台正加快扩容，稳步联通 31 个省级行政区的平台。其市场已吸引逾 1 万家企业用户、200 多家服务商、2000 余款算力产品与 300 多个大模型，同时为开发者提供算力接入服务，并积累了数十亿条监测数据。

Beyond interconnecting existing facilities, the computing power network is matching supply in resource-rich regions with surging demand in the fast-growing east through "computing-electricity coordination." Regions such as Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region and Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region are converting abundant wind and solar power into on-site computing capacity, supporting the east's growing needs for large-scale data processing and AI training.

算力网不仅联通既有设施，还通过“算电协同”，把资源富集地区的供给与东部增长迅猛的需求对接起来。内蒙古、新疆等地区正把丰富的风电、光伏就地转化为算力，支撑东部日益增长的大规模数据处理与人工智能训练需求。

Inner Mongolia offers a telling example. With green electricity priced at around 0.35 yuan (about 5 U.S. cents) per kilowatt-hour, a cool climate that allows natural cooling for nearly 10 months a year and network latency of no more than 5 milliseconds to Beijing, the region has emerged as a preferred destination for large-scale computing projects. As of the first half of 2026, 20 of China's 30 leading private computing companies had established a presence in Inner Mongolia, with combined investment exceeding 200 billion yuan, and nine of the country's top 10 computing service providers had set up operations in the region.

内蒙古是一个很能说明问题的例子。当地绿电约 0.35 元/千瓦时（约 5 美分），气候凉爽，近 10 个月可自然冷却，到北京的网络时延不超过 5 毫秒，因而成为大型算力项目的首选地。截至 2026 年上半年，中国 30 家领先民营算力企业中已有 20 家落户内蒙古，合计投资超 2000 亿元；全国前十大算力服务商中有 9 家在当地布局。

The network's benefits are also extending beyond China's borders. As of early August, Chinese large models had ranked first in call volume for 15 consecutive weeks on model aggregation platforms that mainly serve overseas users, according to data from OpenRouter. More overseas developers are choosing to train AI models in China or directly tap its mature computing resources. According to Xiao Hongwei, a researcher at the State Information Center, the computing power network, functioning as an "intelligent foundation" for economic and social operations, can meet computing demands across diverse scenarios including large model training, film rendering, autonomous driving and smart finance, and drive upgrades across the entire industrial chain. "The computing power network will fuel the development of new quality productive forces," Xiao said.

算力网的效益也在向境外延伸。据 OpenRouter 数据，截至 8 月初，中国大模型在主要服务海外用户的模型聚合平台上，连续 15 周占据调用量第一。越来越多海外开发者选择在中国训练模型，或直接调用其成熟算力资源。国家信息中心研究员肖红伟表示，算力网作为经济社会运行的“智能底座”，既能满足大模型训练、影视渲染、自动驾驶与智慧金融等多元场景需求，也能带动人工智能、大数据与工业互联网整条产业链升级。“算力网将为新质生产力发展注入动力，”肖红伟说。
