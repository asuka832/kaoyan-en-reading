Chinese 'robot brain' startup sees ChatGPT-style breakthrough as soon as next year

BEIJING, Sept 18 (Reuters) — Humanoid robots could complete most general-purpose tasks from verbal instructions as soon as next year, but putting them to useful work in ordinary homes will take much longer, according to Gao Yang, co-founder and chief scientist of Chinese startup Spirit AI.

路透社北京 9 月 18 日电 —— 中国初创公司灵心巧手（Spirit AI）联合创始人兼首席科学家高阳表示，人形机器人最早明年就能根据口头指令完成多数通用任务；但要真正在普通家庭里派上用场，仍需更长时间。

humanoid /ˈhjuːmənɔɪd/ adj. 类人的；人形的
general-purpose 通用的、多用途的
verbal instructions 口头指令
co-founder 联合创始人
chief scientist 首席科学家

Gao, who is also an assistant professor at Tsinghua University, said the industry has made striking hardware advances — robots that can sprint, dance and even perform backflips. Attention is now shifting to software intelligence, often called "embodied AI".

高阳同时是清华大学助理教授。他指出，行业在硬件上已取得显著进展——机器人能冲刺、跳舞甚至后空翻。眼下重心正转向软件智能，常被称为「具身智能」（embodied AI）。

assistant professor 助理教授
striking /ˈstraɪkɪŋ/ adj. 显著的、引人注目的
sprint /sprɪnt/ v. 冲刺、短跑
backflip 后空翻
embodied AI 具身人工智能（智能嵌入物理身体）

"The brain is indeed the weakest link in the complete robotics stack," Gao said. Firms are hunting for a "ChatGPT moment" — a leap that suddenly makes robot intelligence feel transformative.

「在整套机器人技术栈里，大脑确实是最薄弱的一环，」高阳说。各家公司都在寻找「ChatGPT 时刻」——那种让机器人智能一下子显得颠覆性的跃迁。

weakest link 最薄弱环节
stack n. （技术）栈、整套体系
hunt for 寻找、追寻
transformative /trænsˈfɔːmətɪv/ adj. 变革性的、颠覆性的

He anticipates a GPT-3.0-like milestone by mid-2027: users speak in natural language, and the robot carries out a series of reasonable physical actions. Fine motor skills — such as unscrewing a bottle cap — and tasks never seen in training remain hard.

他预计到 2027 年中将出现类似 GPT-3.0 的里程碑：用户用自然语言说话，机器人就能执行一连串合理的肢体动作。精细动作——比如拧开瓶盖——以及训练中未见过的任务，仍是难点。

anticipate /ænˈtɪsɪpeɪt/ v. 预期、预料
milestone /ˈmaɪlstəʊn/ n. 里程碑
carry out 执行、完成
fine motor skills 精细动作技能
unscrew /ˌʌnˈskruː/ v. 拧开、旋松

Spirit AI employs about 1,000 contractors nationwide who wear data-collection gear at home and in factories. At its training centre, people are filmed opening fridges, unlocking safes and cutting vegetables — everyday motions used to teach robots.

Spirit AI 在全国约有 1000 名承包人员，在家中和工厂里佩戴数据采集装备。训练中心里，人们被拍摄打开冰箱、开保险柜、切菜等日常动作，用来教机器人。

contractor /kənˈtræktə(r)/ n. 承包商；外包人员
nationwide 全国范围内的
gear /ɡɪə(r)/ n. 装备、器材
safe n. 保险箱、保险柜

In structured living-room environments, the company reports about a 90% success rate on simple tasks. Progress has moved from one isolated action — pouring water or folding clothes — to larger spaces and continuous, complex workflows.

在结构化的客厅环境中，公司称简单任务成功率约达 90%。进展已从单一孤立动作——倒水或叠衣服——扩展到更大空间与连续、复杂的工作流程。

structured 结构化的、有序安排的
success rate 成功率
isolated /ˈaɪsəleɪtɪd/ adj. 孤立的、单独的
workflow /ˈwɜːkfləʊ/ n. 工作流程

Over the next one to two years, Gao expects initial industrial applications; commercial services for simpler tasks may follow in about two years. Homes remain far harder because clutter, pets and unpredictable people create messy conditions.

未来一到两年，高阳预计会先出现工业场景的初步应用；约两年后，面向较简单任务的商业服务或将跟进。家庭仍难得多——杂物、宠物和难以预测的人会让环境变得混乱。

initial /ɪˈnɪʃl/ adj. 最初的、初步的
application n. 应用（场景）
commercial /kəˈmɜːʃl/ adj. 商业的
clutter /ˈklʌtə(r)/ n. 杂乱物品
unpredictable /ˌʌnprɪˈdɪktəbl/ adj. 难以预测的

Tens of the firm's Moz1 wheeled humanoids are already working at battery maker CATL and e-commerce giant JD.com, which is also an investor. Founded in 2024, the 300-person startup has raised more than $670 million and is valued at about 20 billion yuan ($2.9 billion).

数十台该公司的 Moz1 轮式人形机器人已在电池制造商宁德时代（CATL）以及电商巨头京东（同时也是投资方）投入使用。这家 2024 年成立、约 300 人的初创公司已融资逾 6.7 亿美元，估值约 200 亿元人民币（约 29 亿美元）。

wheeled 带轮的、轮式的

investor /ɪnˈvestə(r)/ n. 投资者

be valued at 估值为

Gao prefers real-world data over simulators. Simulators still struggle with flexible, deformable objects such as cables. He argued that "dirty data" with diverse motions sped learning more than repeating one clean motion fifty times or more.

高阳更青睐真实世界数据而非模拟器。模拟器在处理线缆等柔性、可变形物体时仍力不从心。他认为，带有多样动作的「脏数据」比把一个干净动作重复五十次以上更能加速学习。

simulator /ˈsɪmjuleɪtə(r)/ n. 模拟器
flexible /ˈfleksəbl/ adj. 柔韧的、灵活的
deformable /dɪˈfɔːməbl/ adj. 可变形的
dirty data 脏数据（含噪声、不完美的真实数据）
diverse /daɪˈvɜːs/ adj. 多样的

Concerns about rogue AI feel less pressing for robots today because the models are still immature, Gao said. The company uses whole-body force control with emergency braking. More advanced alignment, he added, will become more actionable once models reach a mature GPT-4.0-like era.

高阳称，对「失控 AI」的担忧目前对机器人没那么紧迫，因为模型仍不成熟。公司采用全身力控并配备紧急制动。他补充说，等到模型进入类似成熟 GPT-4.0 的时代，更先进的对齐技术才会更有可操作性。

rogue /rəʊɡ/ adj. 失控的、流氓式的
pressing /ˈpresɪŋ/ adj. 紧迫的
immature /ˌɪməˈtjʊə(r)/ adj. 不成熟的
force control 力控（力反馈控制）
emergency braking 紧急制动
alignment /əˈlaɪnmənt/ n. （AI）对齐；使行为符合人类意图
actionable /ˈækʃənəbl/ adj. 可付诸行动的、可落地的
