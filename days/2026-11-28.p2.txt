<section class="block"><p class="en">The second approach – reverse engineering – involves working backwards from a finished game to understand how it was created. One way of doing this is decompilation, which attempts to turn the game&#x27;s final version into something closer to its original source code. Brown compares the process to "taking a cake and somehow turning it back into the original recipe, with the exact quantities of flour, eggs, and milk used, so anyone can remake the same cake". A related technique, recompilation, can help adapt a game that has been reverse-engineered to run on different hardware.
