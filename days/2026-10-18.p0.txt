<section class="block"><p class="en">AI companies should be regulated. They keep saying so themselves. US carmakers have been legally required to install seatbelts in all models since 1968. If your seatbelt fails or unlatches during a crash, the carmaker is legally liable. If you&#x27;re hacked by an AI system gone rogue – is the company that created it legally liable?</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">AI 公司应当受到监管——这话它们自己就一直在说。自 1968 年起，美国法律要求汽车制造商在所有车型上安装安全带。如果安全带在车祸中失灵或松脱，车企要承担法律责任。那么，如果你遭到一个“失控”AI 系统的黑客攻击，开发它的公司要不要担法律责任？</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>regulate</strong> v. 管理、监管（regulation n. 规章、监管）</div><div class="item"><strong>legally</strong> ad. 法律上、合法地</div><div class="item"><strong>require</strong> v. 要求（be required to do 被要求做……）</div><div class="item"><strong>install</strong> v. 安装</div><div class="item"><strong>unlatch</strong> v. 松开、打开（门闩、扣件）</div><div class="item"><strong>liable</strong> a. 负有法律责任的（be liable for）；易于……的（be liable to do）</div><div class="item"><strong>rogue</strong> a. 失控的、不受约束的</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">Gabriel Weil, Senior Fellow at the Institute for Law on AI: &#x27;So the answer is maybe, and it depends. The legal regime that clearly applies is negligence – negligence requires a plaintiff, someone harmed, to prove that the defendant, the AI company, failed to exercise reasonable care – in the legal terminology we say &quot;breached their duty of reasonable care&quot; – and that had they exercised reasonable care, that would have prevented the plaintiff&#x27;s injury.&#x27;</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">AI 法律研究所高级研究员加布里埃尔·韦尔说：“答案是‘也许’，要视情况而定。明确适用的法律制度是过失责任——过失责任要求原告，也就是受害者，证明被告即 AI 公司未尽到合理注意义务（法律术语叫‘违反了合理注意义务’），并且证明假如对方尽到了合理注意，原告就不会受到伤害。”</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>regime</strong> n. 制度、体制；政权</div><div class="item"><strong>apply</strong> v. 适用；申请；应用</div><div class="item"><strong>negligence</strong> n. 疏忽、过失（negligent a.）</div><div class="item"><strong>plaintiff</strong> n. 原告</div><div class="item"><strong>defendant</strong> n. 被告</div><div class="item"><strong>exercise</strong> v. 行使、运用（exercise care 尽到注意）；n. 练习</div><div class="item"><strong>terminology</strong> n. 术语</div><div class="item"><strong>breach</strong> v. 违反、违背；n. 违约</div><div class="item"><strong>had they exercised ...</strong> 虚拟语气倒装 = if they had exercised ...</div></div></div></div></section>
