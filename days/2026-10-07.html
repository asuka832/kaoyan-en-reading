Why neutrinos matter as Francis Halzen wins 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics?

弗朗西斯·哈尔岑获 2026 年诺贝尔物理学奖：中微子为何如此重要？

来源：Xinhua（新华社）· 2026-10-07 · 原文链接

STOCKHOLM, Oct. 6 (Xinhua) -- Buried deep beneath the Antarctic ice, thousands of light sensors wait for flashes left by neutrinos, elusive particles that can travel across the universe almost undisturbed.

新华社斯德哥尔摩10月6日电——在南极冰层深处，数以千计的光传感器静静等待着中微子留下的闪光。中微子是一种难以捕捉的粒子，几乎可以不受干扰地穿越整个宇宙。

buried adj./过去分词 被埋藏的（Buried ... 过去分词短语作状语，逻辑主语是 sensors）
beneath prep. 在……之下
Antarctic adj. 南极的
sensor n. 传感器
neutrino n. 中微子
elusive adj. 难以捉摸的、难以捕捉的（考研高频）
undisturbed adj. 未受干扰的
elusive particles that ... 同位语，解释 neutrinos

By turning a cubic kilometre of South Pole ice into a giant detector, physicist Francis Halzen helped open a new window on the cosmos.

通过把南极一立方千米的冰变成一台巨型探测器，物理学家弗朗西斯·哈尔岑帮助人类打开了一扇观测宇宙的新窗口。

by doing 通过做……（方式状语）
turn A into B 把 A 变成 B
cubic kilometre 立方千米（英式拼写 kilometre）
detector n. 探测器
open a new window on 为……打开一扇新窗口（比喻：提供新的观察途径）
cosmos n. 宇宙（= universe）

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awarded Halzen the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics on Tuesday "for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin."

瑞典皇家科学院周二将 2026 年诺贝尔物理学奖授予哈尔岑，以表彰他“对冰立方中微子天文台的决定性贡献，以及发现源自天体物理过程的高能中微子”。

award sb. sth. 授予某人某物（双宾语）
decisive adj. 决定性的
contribution to 对……的贡献（to 是介词）
observatory n. 天文台、观测站
astrophysical adj. 天体物理的
of ... origin 起源于……的（of astrophysical origin 来自天体物理过程）

What are neutrinos, and why do they matter?

Neutrinos are among the smallest known particles. They carry no electric charge, have extremely little mass, and interact so weakly with matter that most pass straight through the Earth without leaving a trace.

中微子是已知最小的粒子之一。它们不带电荷，质量极小，与物质的相互作用极其微弱，以至于大多数中微子会径直穿过地球而不留下任何痕迹。

among the + 最高级 ……之一（= one of the ...）
electric charge 电荷
mass n. 质量
interact with 与……相互作用
so ... that ... 如此……以至于……（结果状语从句）
matter n. 物质；v. 要紧、重要（标题 why do they matter）
trace n. 痕迹；v. 追溯（后文 trace back）

Olle Eriksson, a member of the Nobel Committee for Physics and professor at Uppsala University, told Xinhua that detecting neutrinos is extremely challenging: "Through my thumbnail, there are 65 billion neutrinos that go through every second, and I don't feel it." But once detected, they can reveal information about events occurring far out in the universe.

诺贝尔物理学委员会委员、乌普萨拉大学教授奥勒·埃里克松对新华社表示，探测中微子极具挑战性：“每一秒都有 650 亿个中微子穿过我的拇指指甲，而我毫无感觉。”但中微子一旦被探测到，就能揭示宇宙遥远之处所发生事件的信息。

committee n. 委员会
challenging adj. 有挑战性的、困难的
thumbnail n. 拇指指甲
once detected once (they are) detected 省略结构：一旦被探测到
reveal v. 揭示、透露
events occurring ... 现在分词作后置定语 = events that occur ...

Scientists have long known that the cosmos contains natural particle accelerators capable of producing particles with energies far beyond those achievable in laboratories on Earth. The origins of the highest-energy cosmic particles, however, have remained difficult to trace.

科学家早就知道，宇宙中存在天然的粒子加速器，能够产生能量远超地球实验室所能达到水平的粒子。然而，最高能宇宙粒子的来源一直难以追溯。

accelerator n. 加速器
capable of doing 能够做……（形容词短语作后置定语修饰 accelerators）
far beyond 远远超出
those achievable in ... those 指代 energies；achievable 可达到的，后置修饰
origin n. 起源、来源
remain + adj. 保持……状态（系动词）
however 插入语，表转折，阅读题注意转折后为重点

Cosmic rays carry electric charge and are deflected by magnetic fields as they travel through space. Neutrinos, unlike other particles, are electrically neutral and travel essentially in a straight line, allowing scientists to trace their direction back toward the violent environments where they were produced.

宇宙射线带有电荷，在太空中穿行时会被磁场偏转。与其他粒子不同，中微子呈电中性，基本沿直线传播，这使科学家能够沿其方向回溯，找到产生它们的剧烈天体环境。

cosmic ray 宇宙射线
deflect v. 使偏转、使转向
magnetic field 磁场
unlike prep. 与……不同（对比结构，阅读常考）
neutral adj. 中性的；中立的
essentially adv. 基本上、本质上
allowing ... 现在分词作结果状语：从而使……
trace ... back toward 把……追溯到
violent adj. 剧烈的、猛烈的

How did Halzen turn South Pole ice into a telescope?

In 1988, Halzen, the Belgian-born scientist, presented the idea of installing light sensors deep in Antarctic ice. When one of the rare neutrinos collides with an atomic nucleus, it can produce a charged particle that generates a faint flash of blue light as it moves through the transparent ice. Sensors can capture that light and help scientists reconstruct where the neutrino came from.

1988 年，出生于比利时的哈尔岑提出了在南极冰层深处安装光传感器的设想。当某个罕见的中微子与原子核碰撞时，会产生一个带电粒子，它在透明的冰中穿行时会发出微弱的蓝光。传感器能捕捉到这道光，帮助科学家重建中微子的来源方向。

Belgian-born adj. 出生于比利时的（X-born 构词）
present the idea of 提出……的想法
install v. 安装
collide with 与……碰撞
atomic nucleus 原子核（复数 nuclei）
generate v. 产生
faint adj. 微弱的、模糊的
transparent adj. 透明的
reconstruct v. 重建、重现
where the neutrino came from 宾语从句作 reconstruct 的宾语

The idea eventually became IceCube, a detector using an entire cubic kilometre of Antarctic ice. Completed in 2011, it contains 5,160 light sensors, mostly between about 1,450 and 2,450 metres below the surface.

这一设想最终成为“冰立方”——一台利用整整一立方千米南极冰体的探测器。它于 2011 年建成，装有 5160 个光传感器，大多位于冰面以下约 1450 米至 2450 米之间。

eventually adv. 最终
entire adj. 整个的、全部的
Completed in 2011 过去分词短语作状语 = It was completed in 2011
contain v. 包含、容纳
surface n. 表面

The depth matters. "There, the ice is very clear, no air bubbles, no dirt, no dust," said Eriksson, adding that this condition allows faint flashes to travel hundreds of metres before being absorbed.

深度至关重要。“那里的冰非常清澈，没有气泡，没有污垢，没有灰尘，”埃里克松说。他补充道，这样的条件使微弱的闪光在被吸收之前能传播数百米。

depth n. 深度（deep 的名词）
bubble n. 气泡
dirt n. 污垢、泥土
adding that ... 现在分词作伴随状语：并补充说……
allow sb./sth. to do 使……能够做
before being absorbed before + 被动动名词
absorb v. 吸收

The effort paid off. In 2013, IceCube researchers reported the first strong evidence for high-energy neutrinos arriving from distant space. With more data, they were able to establish that some of the particles originated far outside the solar system.

努力终获回报。2013 年，冰立方研究人员报告了来自遥远太空的高能中微子的首个有力证据。随着数据增多，他们得以证实其中一些粒子起源于太阳系之外的遥远区域。

pay off 取得成功、得到回报
evidence for ……的证据（不可数）
distant adj. 遥远的
establish that ... 证实、确定……
originate v. 起源于（originate in/from）
solar system 太阳系

Eriksson said the achievement helps humanity better understand its surroundings and its place in the universe by providing information about extreme cosmic events that cannot easily be obtained in other ways.

埃里克松说，这项成就提供了关于极端宇宙事件的信息——这些信息很难通过其他方式获得——从而帮助人类更好地了解自身所处的环境及其在宇宙中的位置。

achievement n. 成就
humanity n. 人类
help sb. (to) do 帮助某人做（to 可省）
surroundings n. 环境、周围事物（常用复数）
extreme adj. 极端的
obtain v. 获得
that cannot ... 从语义看修饰 information（信息难以获得），而非紧邻的 events，注意先行词判断

The immediate significance lies mainly in expanding fundamental knowledge, he said, adding that major scientific discoveries can also lead to practical applications that were impossible to foresee when the research began.

他说，这项成果的直接意义主要在于拓展基础知识；他补充道，重大科学发现也可能催生研究之初无法预见的实际应用。

immediate adj. 直接的、当前的
significance n. 意义、重要性
lie in 在于（lie-lay-lain）
fundamental adj. 基础的、根本的
lead to 导致、带来
practical application 实际应用
foresee v. 预见、预知

Why Halzen, and what comes next?

IceCube is the work of a large international collaboration involving hundreds of scientists and engineers. "Even though it is a large collaboration, we could still identify one person who was the pivotal, most important contributor to that experimental buildup and the experimental discovery," said Eriksson.

冰立方是一项由数百名科学家和工程师参与的大型国际合作的成果。“尽管这是一项大规模合作，我们仍然可以找出一个人，他是这一实验建设和实验发现中关键的、最重要的贡献者，”埃里克松说。

collaboration n. 合作、协作（项目）
involving ... 现在分词作后置定语：涉及……的
even though 尽管、即使（让步状语从句）
identify v. 识别、确认
pivotal adj. 关键的、核心的（考研高频）
contributor n. 贡献者
buildup n. 建设、积累

"It was a long journey, but ... it finally worked," Halzen recalled the early phases at Tuesday's Nobel press conference, describing how the success was not guaranteed nearly 40 years ago but made possible by critical contributions from many people.

“那是一段漫长的旅程，但……它终于成功了。”哈尔岑在周二的诺贝尔奖新闻发布会上回忆起早期阶段时说。他讲述道，近 40 年前这项成功并无保证，是众多人的关键贡献才使之成为可能。

recall v. 回忆、回想
phase n. 阶段
press conference 新闻发布会
describing how ... 现在分词作伴随状语，how 引导宾语从句
guarantee v. 保证、担保
not ... but ... 不是……而是……（并列 was not guaranteed / (was) made possible）
critical adj. 关键的；批判的

The journey is continuing. Scientists are planning IceCube-Gen2, which is expected to expand the instrumented volume at the South Pole to about eight cubic kilometres, while other neutrino observatories are being developed around the world.

这段旅程仍在继续。科学家正在规划“第二代冰立方”，预计将把南极的探测体积扩大到约八立方千米；与此同时，世界各地也在建设其他中微子观测站。

Gen2 second generation 第二代
be expected to 预计、有望
expand ... to ... 把……扩大到……
instrumented adj. 装有仪器的（instrumented volume 探测体积）
volume n. 体积；卷；音量
while conj. 与此同时、而（表对比/同时）
are being developed 现在进行时被动语态

Halzen said opening a new window on the universe often leads to unexpected discoveries. For neutrino astronomy, that window may only have begun to open.

哈尔岑说，打开一扇观测宇宙的新窗口，往往会带来意想不到的发现。对中微子天文学而言，这扇窗或许才刚刚开启。

opening a new window ... 动名词短语作宾语从句的主语
unexpected adj. 意想不到的
astronomy n. 天文学
may only have begun may have done 对过去/现在完成情况的推测：可能才刚刚开始
