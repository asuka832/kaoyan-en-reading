<section class="block"><p class="en">OpenAI in September claimed that employees using a non-public version of its AI tools were able to solve part of a notoriously difficult problem in just 88 hours. The problem was part of the Navier-Stokes equations, which concern how fluids move. More than 10,000 AI agents, or computer programmed bots that operate somewhat autonomously, were put to the task as an AI training exercise. The proof that OpenAI released on Navier-Stokes has yet to be confirmed as accurate, and it set off criticism within academic and higher maths communities and organisations about the use of AI to solve maths problems.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">OpenAI 在九月宣称，其员工使用该公司尚未公开的人工智能工具，仅用 88 小时就解决了一道极为棘手问题的一部分。该问题属于纳维-斯托克斯方程，涉及流体如何运动。超过 1 万个人工智能智能体——即在一定程度上自主运行的计算机程序机器人——被投入这项任务，作为一次人工智能训练演习。OpenAI 公布的纳维-斯托克斯证明尚未被确认为准确，并在学术和高等数学社群及机构中引发了关于用人工智能解决数学问题的批评。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>claim that</strong> 宣称</div><div class="item"><strong>notoriously difficult</strong> 极为棘手的、出了名地困难的</div><div class="item"><strong>in just 88 hours</strong> 仅用 88 小时</div><div class="item"><strong>equation</strong> n. 方程</div><div class="item"><strong>concern</strong> v. 涉及、关于（熟词僻义）</div><div class="item"><strong>fluid</strong> n. 流体</div><div class="item"><strong>agent</strong> n. 智能体、代理人（熟词僻义）</div><div class="item"><strong>autonomously</strong> adv. 自主地</div><div class="item"><strong>be put to the task</strong> 被投入该项任务</div><div class="item"><strong>training exercise</strong> 训练演习</div><div class="item"><strong>yet to be confirmed</strong> 尚未被确认</div><div class="item"><strong>set off criticism</strong> 引发批评</div><div class="item"><strong>academic community</strong> 学术界</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">Anthropic has also claimed to have made other progress and solutions in maths and physics. A group of maths scholars said AI companies boasting of major solutions to show how sophisticated their commercial products had become was &quot;detrimental to the science of mathematics&quot;. &quot;We are witnessing a general threat to intellectual work, with misalignment between the outcome of the use of AI and its initial purpose,&quot; the scholars said in a group letter. A month later, OpenAI released what it claimed were solutions, proofs, or major progress on 372 more outstanding higher maths problems. It will take months, if not longer, for those claims to be reviewed by experts to prove their validity.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">Anthropic 也声称在数学和物理领域取得了其他进展与解法。一群数学学者表示，人工智能公司为展示其商业产品有多么精巧而鼓吹重大解法，这“有损于数学这门科学”。“我们正在目睹对智力工作的普遍威胁，人工智能使用的结果与其最初目的之间出现了错位，”这些学者在一封联名信中写道。一个月后，OpenAI 又发布了据称是对另外 372 个未解高等数学问题的解法、证明或重大进展。要由专家审阅这些主张并证明其有效性，将需要数月乃至更长时间。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>make progress</strong> 取得进展</div><div class="item"><strong>boast of</strong> 吹嘘、鼓吹</div><div class="item"><strong>sophisticated</strong> adj. 精密的、先进的</div><div class="item"><strong>commercial products</strong> 商业产品</div><div class="item"><strong>detrimental to</strong> 对……有害的</div><div class="item"><strong>witness</strong> v. 目睹、见证</div><div class="item"><strong>threat to</strong> 对……的威胁</div><div class="item"><strong>intellectual work</strong> 智力工作</div><div class="item"><strong>misalignment</strong> n. 错位、不一致</div><div class="item"><strong>outcome</strong> n. 结果</div><div class="item"><strong>initial purpose</strong> 最初目的</div><div class="item"><strong>group letter</strong> 联名信</div><div class="item"><strong>outstanding</strong> adj. 未解决的（熟词僻义，非“杰出的”）</div><div class="item"><strong>validity</strong> n. 有效性、正确性</div><div class="item"><strong>if not longer</strong> 如果不是更久的话</div></div></div></div></section>

<h2>复盘</h2>
<p><strong>主旨：</strong>Anthropic 数学家阿尔珀盖声称，他用该公司尚未公开的 Claude 版本，在约六周内完成了黎曼猜想的详尽证明；若成立，这将是历史上最重大的数学成果之一。陶哲轩等指出核验尚需时日。文章同时把此事放在人工智能公司密集宣称攻克数学难题、学界批评其“作秀且有损科学”的背景下，核心对照是“重大宣称 vs 尚未被独立证实”。</p>
<p><strong>结构：</strong>开篇抛出证明黎曼猜想的声称，并点出时点就在公司上市次日 → 若成立的历史意义，并引出 OpenAI 等近期密集宣布解法 → 说明使用未公开版 Claude，介绍黎曼猜想与素数 → 以黎曼命名，以及素数作为高等数学基石所具有的广泛影响 → 向首席执行官请示公布方式，作者否认“借上市炒作”的冷嘲解读 → Lean 语言逾 25 万页证明，陶哲轩称核验尚早，但此次至少有人类作者可沟通 → OpenAI 用逾万智能体声称部分解决纳维-斯托克斯方程，证明未获确认并引发批评 → Anthropic 的其他进展、学者联名信批评“有损数学科学”，以及 OpenAI 后又宣布 372 项进展、审阅需数月。</p>
<p><strong>考研词汇与搭配：</strong>claim to have、stock market debut、over the course of、if found to be correct、among the most significant、describe ... as、scores of、elude、put forward、non-public version、deal with、be widely considered to be、in large part due to、name after、bedrock、broad impact、precise timing、refer to、unilaterally、cynical view、to do with、verification、some way off、in this case、assist with、notoriously difficult、concern、autonomously、be put to the task、yet to be confirmed、set off criticism、boast of、detrimental to、threat to、intellectual work、misalignment、initial purpose、outstanding、validity、if not longer。熟词僻义：concern（涉及）、agent（智能体）、outstanding（尚未解决的）、view（看法）。</p>
<p><strong>阅读技巧：</strong>标题用 may have 表示此事尚未被证实，阅读时应始终抓住“声称 vs 核验”这一限定。关键对程度与不确定性的表述包括：If found to be correct、verification is still some way off、has yet to be confirmed、It will take months, if not longer。第 5 段关于 timing 的自我辩护（an obvious cynical view... but it is false）易出态度与推理题，需分清“外界可能怎么看”和“作者/当事人怎么辩”。第 6 段陶哲轩将“此次有人类作者可沟通”与今年早些时候“显然没有这样的人类”作对比，是平衡写法，不宜把全文简化为对人工智能证明的全盘否定。末段学者联名信态度明确（detrimental、threat、misalignment），与公司密集宣称形成对照。专有名词需对应：Riemann hypothesis、Claude、Lean、Navier-Stokes、Dario Amodei、Terrance Tao（陶哲轩）。注意 outstanding 在 outstanding higher maths problems 中是“尚未解决的”，不是“杰出的”。</p>
