<section class="block"><p class="en">Chinese AI developers have showed &quot;remarkable resilience and innovation&quot; in spite of the shortage of advanced American chips, analyst Lian Jye Su from tech research firm Omdia said. But US officials and AI firms have also alleged that Chinese developers closed the gap by using distillation - a technique that enables models to learn from more advanced platforms. China&#x27;s foreign ministry said in response that AI development was &quot;fuelled&quot; by the country&#x27;s &quot;vision of extensive consultation and joint contribution for shared benefit&quot;.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">科技研究公司 Omdia 分析师 Lian Jye Su 说，尽管先进美国芯片短缺，中国 AI 开发者仍展现出「显著的韧性与创新」。但美方官员与 AI 公司也指控，中国开发者通过「蒸馏」——一种让模型向更先进平台学习的技术——缩小了差距。中国外交部回应称，AI 发展由本国「共商共建共享」愿景所「推动」。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>remarkable</strong> a. 显著的、非凡的</div><div class="item"><strong>resilience</strong> n. 韧性、恢复力</div><div class="item"><strong>in spite of</strong> 尽管</div><div class="item"><strong>shortage</strong> n. 短缺</div><div class="item"><strong>allege</strong> v. 声称、指控（常未证实）</div><div class="item"><strong>close the gap</strong> 缩小差距</div><div class="item"><strong>distillation</strong> n. 蒸馏（此处：模型蒸馏）</div><div class="item"><strong>technique</strong> n. 技术、手法</div><div class="item"><strong>enable</strong> v. 使能够</div><div class="item"><strong>in response</strong> 作为回应</div><div class="item"><strong>fuel</strong> v. 推动、助长；n. 燃料</div><div class="item"><strong>consultation</strong> n. 协商、磋商</div><div class="item"><strong>joint contribution</strong> 共同贡献、共建</div><div class="item"><strong>shared benefit</strong> 共享利益</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">China and the US may be in fierce competition, but their governments&#x27; approaches to safety concerns are markedly different. While some in the industry have recently warned that AI could pose an existential risk to humanity, Chinese researchers seem less concerned about that and are more worried about potential misuse of the technology and cyber war. Responding to concerns over AI, Trump has said the US already has sufficient rules in place and that the only guardrails AI needs are a &quot;high IQ&quot; president.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">中美或许竞争激烈，但两国政府对安全关切的处理方式明显不同。业内近来有人警告 AI 可能对人类构成生存性风险，中国研究者似乎对此不那么忧虑，更担心技术被滥用与网络战。回应 AI 关切时，特朗普称美国已有足够规则，AI 唯一需要的护栏是一位「高智商」总统。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>fierce</strong> a. 激烈的、猛烈的</div><div class="item"><strong>markedly</strong> ad. 明显地、显著地</div><div class="item"><strong>pose a risk</strong> 构成风险</div><div class="item"><strong>existential</strong> a. 生存的、存在主义的</div><div class="item"><strong>humanity</strong> n. 人类；人道</div><div class="item"><strong>misuse</strong> n./v. 滥用</div><div class="item"><strong>cyber war</strong> 网络战</div><div class="item"><strong>sufficient</strong> a. 足够的</div><div class="item"><strong>in place</strong> 到位的、已实施的</div><div class="item"><strong>guardrail</strong> n. 护栏、安全约束</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">Beijing has taken a different strategy to the US hands-off approach. Xi urged the country&#x27;s leaders to keep a close eye on AI&#x27;s risks and ensure the technology was always &quot;under human control&quot;. &quot;China has taken a relatively cautious and centrally-coordinated approach,&quot; introducing guides on data usage and algorithms before many other countries, helping to address many underlying concerns, said information security officer Jayant Dave. The use of guidelines, though not legally binding, sets expectations while giving developers room to innovate, said National University of Singapore AI ethics lecturer Jonathan Sim.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">北京采取了有别于美方「放手」路径的策略。习近平敦促领导人密切关注 AI 风险，确保技术始终「处于人类控制之下」。「中国采取了相对谨慎、中央协调的路径，」在许多国家之前就出台数据使用与算法指南，有助于回应诸多深层关切，信息安全官员 Jayant Dave 说。新加坡国立大学 AI 伦理讲师 Jonathan Sim 称，指南虽无法力约束，却设定了预期，同时留给开发者创新空间。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>hands-off</strong> a. 不干涉的、放手的</div><div class="item"><strong>urge</strong> v. 敦促、力劝</div><div class="item"><strong>keep a close eye on</strong> 密切关注</div><div class="item"><strong>cautious</strong> a. 谨慎的</div><div class="item"><strong>centrally-coordinated</strong> a. 中央协调的</div><div class="item"><strong>algorithm</strong> n. 算法</div><div class="item"><strong>address</strong> v. 处理、回应；致辞</div><div class="item"><strong>underlying</strong> a. 深层的、潜在的</div><div class="item"><strong>legally binding</strong> 具有法律约束力的</div><div class="item"><strong>set expectations</strong> 设定预期</div><div class="item"><strong>room to innovate</strong> 创新空间</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">Despite calls for international cooperation, it remains unclear how the AI superpowers plan to address the technology&#x27;s potential dangers - if at all. &quot;Washington worries about capability escaping control. Beijing worries about capability threatening domestic stability,&quot; Dave said. Trump has dismissed proposals for an industry-wide slowdown, arguing that it could allow Chinese developers to close the gap. Ahead of the Trump-Xi summit, officials discussed establishing a notification mechanism for serious AI incidents. What we don&#x27;t want, policy analyst Leia Wang added, was for an &quot;unhealthy version of the race narrative to perpetuate&quot;, potentially escalating an already fierce rivalry in AI.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">尽管有国际合作呼声，AI 超级大国究竟打算如何——若真打算——应对这项技术的潜在危险，仍不清楚。「华盛顿担心能力失控；北京担心能力威胁国内稳定，」Dave 说。特朗普否定全行业放缓提议，称那可能让中国开发者缩小差距。峰会前，官员们讨论建立严重 AI 事件通报机制。政策分析师 Leia Wang 补充说，我们不想要的是「不健康版本的竞赛叙事持续发酵」，从而可能加剧本已激烈的 AI 对抗。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>it remains unclear</strong> 仍不清楚</div><div class="item"><strong>if at all</strong> 即便有的话、哪怕一点点</div><div class="item"><strong>capability</strong> n. 能力</div><div class="item"><strong>escape control</strong> 失控</div><div class="item"><strong>domestic stability</strong> 国内稳定</div><div class="item"><strong>dismiss</strong> v. 否定、不予考虑；解雇</div><div class="item"><strong>proposal</strong> n. 提议、提案</div><div class="item"><strong>industry-wide</strong> a. 全行业的</div><div class="item"><strong>slowdown</strong> n. 放缓、减速</div><div class="item"><strong>notification mechanism</strong> 通报/通知机制</div><div class="item"><strong>incident</strong> n. 事件、事故</div><div class="item"><strong>perpetuate</strong> v. 使持续、使永久化</div><div class="item"><strong>escalate</strong> v. 升级、加剧</div><div class="item"><strong>rivalry</strong> n. 对抗、竞争</div></div></div></div></section>
