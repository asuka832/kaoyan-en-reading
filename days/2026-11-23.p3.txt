<section class="block"><p class="en">But some factors suggest that the impact of a crash in San Francisco may be softer than some fear. For all the investment they have attracted, AI companies are employing relatively few workers in the city compared to previous tech booms. So there may not be that many jobs to lose in a downturn. Ted Egan, the chief economist for the City and County of San Francisco, adds that &quot;because of AI, it is a good time for tech again in the city, but we are not seeing tonnes of overall tech job growth&quot;. Despite the expansion of the AI sector, the city remains about 35,000 jobs shy of its pre-pandemic peak, estimates Enrico Moretti, an economics professor at the University of California, Berkeley. Instead, much of the spending by AI firms in the US is going towards data centres and computing power, relatively little of which are located in the Bay Area. San Francisco might be the brains of the AI boom, but it isn&#x27;t the brawn.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p <section class="block"><p class="en">Nonetheless, a significant correction could still be &quot;very painful&quot; for San Francisco, warns Moretti. Less AI hiring and potential layoffs would ripple through the local service sector, likely to affect jobs in everything from restaurants, to retail, to personal services like dog walking. But AI isn&#x27;t the only force driving San Francisco&#x27;s recovery. Healthcare and tourism are also growing, which may help cushion against a possible AI downturn. <h2>复盘</h2>
<p><strong>主旨：</strong>文章写旧金山正因人工智能热潮迎来复苏：OpenAI、Anthropic 等汇聚于此，租金房价上涨、写字楼空置下降，连遛狗等服务业也受益。但国际货币基金组织等已警告可能出现股价向下修正。热潮高度集中于旧金山本市，冲击或大于硅谷；不过因当地雇佣人数相对有限、算力支出多在湾区以外，影响也许比预想温和。莫雷蒂仍警告服务业外溢会“非常痛苦”，伊根则将其视为可预期的“小小顿挫”，而非经济轨迹的重大转向。</p>
<p><strong>结构：</strong>开篇以遛狗公司说明热潮惠及其他行业，城市自疫情外流后因人工智能复苏 → OpenAI、Anthropic 及大量初创公司落户，热潮迹象体现在租金、空置与广告 → 以“倘若向下修正会怎样”转折，引出基金组织警告与英伟达相关波动，同时强调其仍高利润 → 匿名从业者称“纸牌屋”将倒塌，质疑美国公司巨额开支能否被收入证明，尤其面对来自中国的更廉价模型 → 另一从业者认为市场修正完全可能，但圈子里更担心生存风险 → 热潮集中于旧金山本市，60% 风投流向此地，冲击或大于硅谷 → 雇佣偏少、就业仍低于疫情前峰值，旧金山是“大脑”而非“肌肉” → 服务业外溢之痛，医疗与旅游可作缓冲，伊根称为“hiccup”，戈尔博斯主张“趁它还在先做起来”。</p>

<p><strong>阅读技巧：</strong>标题中的 boom 与 worries about a crash 提示对照，阅读时应同时抓住复苏迹象与崩盘忧虑，不宜把全文读成对崩盘的断言。开篇遛狗公司与文末服务业外溢、build while it is here 形成首尾呼应。注意几组隐喻与对照：house of cards（纸牌屋，喻不稳固的繁荣）；brains vs brawn（大脑与肌肉，喻创新中心与算力基建）；hiccup vs major course correction（小小顿挫与重大转向）；screaming vs grind to a halt（高歌猛进与陷入停滞）。第 3 段修辞问句 what if there is a significant downward correction 引出转折，后文既有“certain that the house of cards will come down”，也有 softer than some fear 与 hiccup rather than a major course correction，属于平衡写法，态度题需抓住这一层次。注意 for all 在 For all the investment they have attracted 中表示“尽管”，不是“为了全部”；shy of 表示“不及”；course correction 与前文 market/downward correction 形成词汇呼应，但含义从“股价修正”转到“经济航向修正”。专有名词需对应：OpenAI、Anthropic、Claude、Nasdaq、International Monetary Fund、Jensen Huang、Nvidia、Silicon Valley、San Francisco Bay Area、Ted Egan、Enrico Moretti、University of California, Berkeley。</p>
