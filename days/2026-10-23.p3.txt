<section class="block"><p class="en">Startups have flocked to open source models like DeepSeek. For a technology or AI developer, using DeepSeek in their work costs a fraction of what a closed model like Google&#x27;s Gemini, Anthropic&#x27;s Claude or OpenAI&#x27;s Codex would.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">初创公司纷纷涌向像 DeepSeek 这样的开源模型。对技术或 AI 开发者而言，在工作中使用 DeepSeek 的成本，只是谷歌 Gemini、Anthropic Claude 或 OpenAI Codex 等闭源模型的一小部分。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>startup</strong> n. 初创公司</div><div class="item"><strong>flock to</strong> 蜂拥而至、纷纷涌向</div><div class="item"><strong>developer</strong> n. 开发者</div><div class="item"><strong>cost</strong> v. 花费；n. 成本</div><div class="item"><strong>a fraction of</strong> ……的一小部分</div><div class="item"><strong>closed model</strong> 闭源模型</div><div class="item"><strong>would</strong> 情态动词，表虚拟/对比省略（省略 cost）</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">Similarly, the expense borne by DeepSeek to develop its models has been astronomically less than what it has cost US AI firms to develop their own. Last year, DeepSeek said it took about $6m (£4.5m) to train the debut version of its model, while the costs associated with OpenAI&#x27;s GPT-4 were roughly $100m. Such revelations sent tech stocks tumbling.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">同样，DeepSeek 开发模型所承担的费用，也远低于美国 AI 公司开发自有模型的成本。去年，DeepSeek 称训练其首发版本模型约花费 600 万美元（约 450 万英镑），而与 OpenAI GPT-4 相关的成本大约为 1 亿美元。此类披露令科技股大跌。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>similarly</strong> ad. 同样地、类似地</div><div class="item"><strong>expense</strong> n. 费用、开支</div><div class="item"><strong>borne by</strong> 由……承担（bear 的过去分词）</div><div class="item"><strong>astronomically</strong> ad. 天文数字地、极其（大/小）</div><div class="item"><strong>train</strong> v. 训练（模型）；培训</div><div class="item"><strong>debut</strong> n./a. 首次亮相、首发的</div><div class="item"><strong>associated with</strong> 与……相关的</div><div class="item"><strong>roughly</strong> ad. 大约、粗略地</div><div class="item"><strong>revelation</strong> n. 披露、揭露的事实</div><div class="item"><strong>send ... tumbling</strong> 使……暴跌/下跌</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">The most recent version of DeepSeek was released last week. Users of the model have praised it online, saying it beats even the most advanced AI tools out of OpenAI on common tasks for AI tools like computer software coding.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">DeepSeek 的最新版本于上周发布。模型用户在网上称赞它，称其在软件编程等 AI 工具常见任务上甚至胜过 OpenAI 最先进的工具。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>version</strong> n. 版本</div><div class="item"><strong>release</strong> v. 发布、发行；n. 发布</div><div class="item"><strong>praise</strong> v./n. 称赞、赞扬</div><div class="item"><strong>beat</strong> v. 击败、胜过（熟词僻义）</div><div class="item"><strong>advanced</strong> a. 先进的、高级的</div><div class="item"><strong>out of</strong> 出自……、来自……</div><div class="item"><strong>common task</strong> 常见任务</div><div class="item"><strong>coding</strong> n. 编程、写代码</div></div></div></div></section>
