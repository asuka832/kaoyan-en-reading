Economic Watch: China's five-year plan to elevate electronic information manufacturing sector

经济观察：中国“十五五”电子信息制造业提升规划

来源：Xinhua · 2026-09-20 · 原文链接

BEIJING, Sept. 20 (Xinhua) -- As artificial intelligence (AI) plays an increasingly significant role in reshaping the global industrial landscape, China has recently unveiled a plan to advance its electronic information manufacturing sector, a critical foundation for AI development, over the next five years.

新华社北京 9 月 20 日电——随着人工智能在重塑全球产业格局中扮演愈发重要的角色，中国近日公布一项规划，拟在未来五年推进电子信息制造业发展——该产业是人工智能发展的关键基础。

reshape v. 重塑、改塑
industrial landscape 产业格局/工业版图
unveil v. 公布、揭晓

electronic information manufacturing 电子信息制造业
critical foundation 关键基础

The development plan for the electronic information manufacturing industry during the 15th Five-Year Plan period (2026-2030), jointly issued by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) and the National Development and Reform Commission, aims to further consolidate the pillar role of the industry by 2030.

由工业和信息化部、国家发展改革委联合印发的《电子信息制造业“十五五”（2026—2030）发展规划》提出，到 2030 年进一步巩固该产业的支柱地位。

Five-Year Plan 五年规划
jointly issued 联合印发/联合发布
MIIT 工业和信息化部
National Development and Reform Commission 国家发展改革委（发改委）
consolidate v. 巩固、加强
pillar role 支柱地位/支柱作用

Industrial firms in the sector with an annual main business revenue of at least 20 million yuan (about 2.96 million U.S. dollars) are expected to see their combined revenue surpass 30 trillion yuan, and R&D investment intensity of the sector will rise to 3.5 percent, according to the plan.

规划提出，该行业年主营业务收入达到 2000 万元及以上的工业企业合计营收有望突破 30 万亿元，全行业研发投入强度将提升至 3.5%。

main business revenue 主营业务收入
combined revenue 合计营收
surpass v. 超过、突破
R&D investment intensity 研发投入强度（研发支出占营收比重）
trillion n. 万亿

By the end of 2025, China's electronic information manufacturing industry had led the country's 41 major industrial sectors in terms of revenue for 13 consecutive years. Building on that foundation, the plan presents a systemic framework to strengthen the industrial base, spanning integrated circuits, electronic components and materials, intelligent sensors, specialized electronic equipment and measuring instruments, and the photonics industry.

截至 2025 年底，中国电子信息制造业已连续 13 年在全国 41 个工业大类中营收领先。在此基础上，规划提出系统性夯实产业基础的框架，覆盖集成电路、电子元器件与材料、智能传感器、专用电子设备与测量仪器，以及光子产业等。

The framework aims to tackle bottlenecks in key areas and build an independent and controllable industrial base, said Liu Bin, an official with the MIIT. As a representative sector, China's integrated circuit industry is entering a new phase of rapid growth, driven by the explosive global demand for high-end computing and memory chips brought about by the AI revolution. Customs data shows that in the first seven months, the country's integrated circuit exports reached 216 billion U.S. dollars, up 99.5 percent year on year, already surpassing the full-year total of 2025.

工信部官员刘斌表示，该框架旨在破解关键领域瓶颈，打造自主可控的产业基础。作为代表性领域，中国集成电路产业正进入新一轮高速增长，驱动力来自 AI 革命带来的高端算力与存储芯片全球需求爆发。海关数据显示，前七个月中国集成电路出口达 2160 亿美元，同比增 99.5%，已超过 2025 年全年总量。

While strengthening the industrial base, the plan also sets out strategies to boost the competitiveness of whole devices and systems. This includes enhancing industrial chain resilience in key areas, developing a robust computing ecosystem, and promoting innovation in consumer electronics. Terminals form the basic foundation of industrial scale and serve as the gateway through which smart technologies reach hundreds of millions of users, said Liang Linjun, a researcher at China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT).

在夯实产业基础的同时，规划还提出提升整机与系统竞争力的策略，包括增强关键领域产业链韧性、发展稳健的算力生态、推动消费电子创新等。中国信通院研究员梁林俊指出，终端构成产业规模的基本盘，也是智能技术触达数亿用户的入口。

The accelerating evolution of a new generation of AI is bringing new demand, new product forms, and new ways of development to the electronic information manufacturing industry, while raising higher requirements for basic hardware supply and system coordination, said Liang. The plan also addresses rising concerns over cutthroat competition in the industry. It calls for timely revisions to industry norms, coordinated planning and early-warning systems for capacity across subsectors, and a comprehensive effort to curb cutthroat competition.

梁林俊表示，新一代 AI 加速演进正给电子信息制造业带来新需求、新产品形态与新发展方式，同时也对基础硬件供给与系统协同提出更高要求。规划还回应了行业中加剧的“内卷式”竞争担忧，要求及时修订行业规范，统筹规划并建立分领域产能预警机制，综合治理恶性竞争。

evolution n. 演进、演化
coordination n. 协同、协调
cutthroat competition 恶性竞争、割喉式竞争（常对应“内卷式竞争”）
revision n. 修订
early-warning system 预警机制
curb v. 遏制、约束

Turning scale into systemic strength, as well as moving from leading in volume to winning on quality, will be a major task for China's electronic information manufacturing industry during the 15th Five-Year Plan period, said Ao Li, vice president of the CAICT. Such a collective push is expected to put the sector on firmer footing, with a more complete chain and more active innovation, and to lay a solid base for the country's goals of building itself into a manufacturing powerhouse and a cyber powerhouse.

中国信通院副院长敖立表示，“十五五”期间，把规模优势转化为体系化能力、从“量的领先”迈向“质的胜出”，将是电子信息制造业的重大任务。业内预期，各方合力将使该行业根基更牢、链条更完整、创新更活跃，并为制造强国与网络强国目标夯实基础。

systemic strength 体系化能力/系统性实力
leading in volume 在规模/产量上领先
winning on quality 以质量取胜
on firmer footing 根基更稳、站稳脚跟
manufacturing powerhouse 制造强国
cyber powerhouse 网络强国
