<section class="block"><p class="en">That shift could create a difficult entry point for graduates. Junior roles have traditionally allowed new employees to develop technical expertise through supervised work, gradually taking on more complex responsibilities. If companies reduce the number of such positions, aspiring engineers may find fewer opportunities to build the experience employers expect.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">这种转变可能让毕业生的入行门槛变得艰难。传统上，初级岗位让新员工通过在指导下工作来培养技术专长，并逐步承担更复杂的职责。如果公司减少此类岗位，有志成为工程师的人可能会发现，积累雇主所期待的经验的机会变少了。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>entry point</strong> 切入点、入口（此处指进入职场的起点）</div><div class="item"><strong>expertise</strong> n. 专长、专门知识（expert n. 专家）</div><div class="item"><strong>supervised</strong> a. 有人指导/监督的（supervise v.）</div><div class="item"><strong>gradually</strong> ad. 逐渐地</div><div class="item"><strong>take on</strong> 承担（责任、工作）；呈现</div><div class="item"><strong>complex</strong> a. 复杂的</div><div class="item"><strong>position</strong> n. 职位、岗位；位置；立场</div><div class="item"><strong>aspiring</strong> a. 有抱负的、想成为……的（aspire to 渴望）</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">Anthropic&#x27;s own workforce growth offers an important qualification: the company has continued to increase its technical hiring in absolute terms, despite the fall in the proportion of junior recruits. The research therefore describes a change in the composition of recruitment, rather than a complete halt to hiring.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">Anthropic 自身员工规模的增长提供了一个重要的限定条件：尽管初级新员工的比例下降，公司技术岗招聘的绝对人数仍在持续增加。因此，这项研究描述的是招聘结构的变化，而不是招聘的全面停止。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>qualification</strong> n. 限定条件、保留意见；资格（此处为“限定、修正”义，考研高频熟词僻义）</div><div class="item"><strong>in absolute terms</strong> 就绝对数量而言（in relative terms 相对而言）</div><div class="item"><strong>despite</strong> prep. 尽管（后接名词，不接从句）</div><div class="item"><strong>proportion</strong> n. 比例、部分</div><div class="item"><strong>therefore</strong> ad. 因此</div><div class="item"><strong>composition</strong> n. 构成、组成；作文；作品</div><div class="item"><strong>halt</strong> n./v. 停止、中止（come to a halt）</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">Nevertheless, Anthropic identifies a potential gap in existing employment protections. Policies designed to protect people who already have jobs may do little for graduates who never secure their first role. For early-career workers, the central challenge may be that the tasks once used to learn a profession are also among those employers are increasingly willing to automate.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">尽管如此，Anthropic 指出了现有就业保护中的一个潜在空白：旨在保护已有工作者的政策，对那些始终没能找到第一份工作的毕业生可能帮助甚微。对于职场新人来说，核心挑战或许在于：那些曾经用来学习一门职业的任务，恰恰也是雇主越来越愿意交给自动化去做的任务。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>nevertheless</strong> ad. 然而、尽管如此（转折）</div><div class="item"><strong>potential</strong> a. 潜在的；n. 潜力</div><div class="item"><strong>gap</strong> n. 缺口、空白；差距</div><div class="item"><strong>existing</strong> a. 现有的、现行的</div><div class="item"><strong>designed to do</strong> 旨在做……、为……而设计</div><div class="item"><strong>do little for</strong> 对……几乎没有帮助</div><div class="item"><strong>secure</strong> v. 获得、争取到；a. 安全的（熟词僻义：secure a job 找到工作）</div><div class="item"><strong>profession</strong> n. 职业、行业（尤指专业性职业）</div><div class="item"><strong>among those (that) ...</strong> 属于……之列（those 后省略关系代词 that）</div><div class="item"><strong>automate</strong> v. 使自动化</div></div></div></div></section>

<aside class="tip"><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示复盘" data-hide="隐藏复盘">显示复盘</button><div class="panel-body tip-body"><h2>复盘</h2>
<p><strong>文章结构：</strong>现象引入（AI 改变“谁被录用”，初级技术招聘占比 27%→9%）→ 信息来源（Anthropic 研究院报告：内部数据 + 17 人访谈）→ 原因分析（编码、找 bug 等原本锻炼新人的任务可交给 AI；部分经理承认因此少招人）→ 背景数据（AI 智能体工时是人类的 6 倍）→ 招聘标准变化（更看重判断力、适应力、担当）→ 影响（毕业生入行门槛变高）→ 让步限定（qualification：招聘绝对数仍在增长，是结构变化而非停招）→ 结论与建议（现有就业保护有空白；学习职业的任务正是最先被自动化的任务）。</p>
<p><strong>考研词汇与搭配：</strong>recruit / recruitment、adoption、draw on、adapt to、delegate、in response、coincide with、substantial、place weight on、replicate、assess、candidate、rely on、expertise、take on、aspiring、in absolute terms、proportion、composition、halt、nevertheless、secure、profession。注意熟词僻义：qualification（限定条件，而非“资格”）、secure（v. 获得）、volume（量）、direct（v. 指挥）、position（职位）、composition（构成）。</p>
<p><strong>阅读技巧：</strong>首段 not just ... but also ... 把重点放在后半句“谁被雇用”，这就是全文主旨；第三段 where 引导的非限制性定语从句修饰两个领域，长难句要先找主干；第五段倍数表达 six times as many hours as ... 是数字题常考点；第六段 rather than 前后对比，体现“看重什么 / 不再看重什么”，常对应细节题；第八段 qualification 与 despite 是作者的“让步”，提醒不要把结论绝对化——考研题中“AI 导致公司停止招聘”这类选项就是过度推断；末段 Nevertheless 后是作者真正想强调的观点，态度题和主旨题多在这里出。</p>
