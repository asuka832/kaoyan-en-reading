China's Huawei sets 2027 launch for new AI chips as it targets Nvidia

来源：Reuters · Casey Hall / Pan Che · 原文链接
读法：先自己啃英文 → 卡住再点「显示中文」→ 生词看蓝底注释。中文默认隐藏，避免一眼瞄到答案。

Huawei Technologies will launch two new artificial-intelligence semiconductors in 2027, rotating chairman David Wang said on Thursday, as the Chinese tech giant builds up its AI computing business and seeks to challenge U.S. chipmaker Nvidia.

华为轮值董事长汪涛周四表示，公司将在 2027 年推出两款新的人工智能半导体。这家中国科技巨头正加码 AI 算力业务，并寻求挑战美国芯片厂商英伟达。

semiconductor /ˌsemikənˈdʌktə(r)/ n. 半导体
rotating chairman 轮值董事长（轮流担任主席）
build up 逐步加强、壮大
chipmaker 芯片制造商

Huawei plans to launch the 960DT in the first quarter of 2027 and the Ascend 960PR in the third quarter, Wang said.

汪涛称，华为计划在 2027 年第一季度推出 960DT，第三季度推出昇腾 Ascend 960PR。

first / third quarter 第一/第三季度（财报与商业新闻高频）
Ascend 华为昇腾系列 AI 芯片品牌名

The company is also working on ways to connect very large numbers of AI chips, allowing them to work together as one bigger computing system. Most advanced AI programmes require more processing power than a single chip can provide.

公司还在研究如何把大量 AI 芯片互联起来，使它们像一台更大的计算系统协同工作。多数先进 AI 程序所需算力，已超出单颗芯片的能力。

processing power 处理能力；算力
programme (BrE) = program 程序、项目

Wang said in a speech at a company event in Shanghai that Huawei's UnifiedBus technology would be a key link in the company's next generation of large AI systems. The technology is intended to help chips share information and work together more efficiently.

汪涛在上海的一场公司活动上表示，华为的 UnifiedBus 技术将是其下一代大型 AI 系统的关键环节。该技术旨在帮助芯片更高效地共享信息、协同工作。

key link 关键环节/纽带
next generation 下一代
be intended to 旨在、目的是
efficiently /ɪˈfɪʃntli/ adv. 高效地

Huawei has developed 11 semiconductors based on UnifiedBus technology for use in its large systems, Wang said. Its largest linked systems, which the company calls superclusters, can support up to 1 million AI processors, he added.

汪涛说，华为已基于 UnifiedBus 技术开发了 11 款用于大型系统的半导体。其最大的互联系统被称作“超集群”（superclusters），最多可支持多达 100 万颗 AI 处理器。

based on 基于
supercluster 超集群（大量节点互联的超大规模系统）
up to 多达、至多
processor 处理器

Huawei has shipped more than 1,000 smaller linked systems, called supernodes, to more than 370 customers, Wang said. A supernode is a system that brings together many AI chips to tackle the same task.

汪涛表示，华为已向逾 370 家客户交付了 1000 多套规模较小的互联系统，称为“超节点”（supernodes）。超节点是把多颗 AI 芯片聚合在一起、共同完成同一任务的系统。

ship v. 发货、出货（商业新闻常用）
bring together 汇集、聚合
tackle /ˈtækl/ v. 处理、应对（难题）

The United States and China are competing to develop the chips, data centres and software that underpin artificial intelligence.

美中两国正在竞相发展支撑人工智能的芯片、数据中心与软件。

compete to do 竞相做某事
data centre (BrE) 数据中心
underpin /ˌʌndəˈpɪn/ v. 支撑、构成…的基础（考研高频）

Washington has imposed export controls that restrict China's access to certain advanced computing chips and semiconductor manufacturing equipment, while Chinese companies and policymakers have placed greater emphasis on building domestic alternatives.

华盛顿实施了出口管制，限制中国获取部分先进计算芯片与半导体制造设备；与此同时，中国企业与决策者更加重视打造国产替代方案。

impose /ɪmˈpəʊz/ v. 实施、强加（管制/制裁）
export controls 出口管制
restrict access to 限制对…的获取
place emphasis on 强调、重视
domestic alternatives 国内替代品/国产方案

That has made the ability to connect chips increasingly important for Chinese technology companies. If they cannot freely buy the most advanced foreign chips, they can seek more computing power by joining larger numbers of locally available chips in one system. But that approach only works well if the chips can exchange information quickly enough.

这也使“把芯片连起来”的能力对中国科技公司愈发重要。若无法自由采购最先进的外国芯片，它们可以通过把更多本地可得芯片接入同一系统来换取算力。但前提是：芯片之间能足够快地交换信息。

increasingly 日益、越来越
locally available 本地可得的
approach n. 方法、路径
exchange information 交换信息

U.S. export controls have helped Huawei emerge as a prominent Chinese supplier of AI chips. Still, it faces a well-established market leader in Nvidia, whose hardware and software tools are widely used by developers around the world.

美国出口管制在客观上帮助华为成为中国突出的 AI 芯片供应商。不过它仍要面对英伟达这位根基深厚的市场领导者——其软硬件工具已被全球开发者广泛使用。

emerge as 崛起为、成为
prominent /ˈprɒmɪnənt/ adj. 突出的、显著的
well-established 根基稳固的、老牌的
market leader 市场领导者

A strong developer base will be important to Huawei's effort to make its hardware easier to use. Wang said Huawei's AI chip ecosystem had 5,270 monthly active developers.

强大的开发者基础，对华为让硬件更易用的努力至关重要。汪涛表示，华为 AI 芯片生态已有 5270 名月活开发者。

developer base 开发者群体/基础
ecosystem /ˈiːkəʊsɪstəm/ n. 生态系统
monthly active 月活跃（MAU 语境）
