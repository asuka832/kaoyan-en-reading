<section class="block"><p class="en">&quot;In theory, machines can work 24 hours a day, which is certainly an advantage compared with human workers. But machines also require maintenance. Every day we need time for that, and each month the production line also requires inspection and repair,&quot; says Cao Li, vice-president of EV maker Leapmotor. Will there be a day when they are no longer needed? &quot;It&#x27;s entirely possible,&quot; Cao says. &quot;And it may come relatively quickly. In workshops, including stamping, welding, and painting, we&#x27;ve basically achieved 100% automation.&quot;</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">「理论上，机器可以一天工作 24 小时，这相对人工肯定是优势。但机器也需要维护。我们每天都要为此留出时间，每月产线还要检查与维修，」电动车制造商零跑汽车副总裁曹力说。会不会有一天他们也不再被需要？「完全有可能，」曹说。「而且可能来得相对快。在冲压、焊接、喷涂等车间，我们基本上已实现 100% 自动化。」</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>in theory</strong> 理论上</div><div class="item"><strong>advantage</strong> n. 优势、有利条件</div><div class="item"><strong>compared with</strong> 与……相比</div><div class="item"><strong>maintenance</strong> n. 维护、保养</div><div class="item"><strong>production line</strong> 生产线</div><div class="item"><strong>inspection</strong> n. 检查、检验</div><div class="item"><strong>vice-president</strong> 副总裁、副会长</div><div class="item"><strong>EV</strong> 电动车（electric vehicle）</div><div class="item"><strong>entirely possible</strong> 完全有可能</div><div class="item"><strong>workshop</strong> n. 车间、工场；研讨班</div><div class="item"><strong>stamping</strong> n. 冲压</div><div class="item"><strong>painting</strong> n. 喷涂、喷漆；绘画</div><div class="item"><strong>achieve</strong> v. 实现、达成</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">China has the edge over the US due to its enormous manufacturing base. Motors, batteries, electronics, gears, sensors - everything needed to build the body of a robot is all at hand. &quot;China also has an idea. It has a plan. They know where they want to be in five years,&quot; says Dr Susanne Bieller, General Secretary of the International Federation of Robotics. However, she acknowledges that while China is good at building the body, the US is still leading at building the &quot;brain&quot;, which includes developing the AI to programme robots. Driven by an &quot;open-source&quot; approach, Chinese innovation has been able to catch up faster than expected, despite US export controls over critical advanced chips. Analysts now believe that the next leg of this race will be determined not by who has the best language model, but who can put this intelligence into millions of machines.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">凭借庞大的制造底座，中国对美国拥有优势。电机、电池、电子件、齿轮、传感器——造机器人「身体」所需的一切都近在手边。「中国还有想法，有规划。他们知道五年后想站到哪里，」国际机器人联合会秘书长 Susanne Bieller 博士说。不过她也承认：中国擅于造「身体」，美国仍在造「大脑」——包括开发给机器人编程的 AI——方面领先。在「开源」路径驱动下，尽管美方对关键先进芯片实施出口管制，中国创新仍能比预期更快追赶。分析人士认为，这场竞赛的下一程将不再取决于谁拥有最好的语言模型，而取决于谁能把智能装进数百万台机器。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>have the edge over</strong> 对……拥有优势</div><div class="item"><strong>enormous</strong> a. 巨大的、庞大的</div><div class="item"><strong>manufacturing base</strong> 制造业基地/底座</div><div class="item"><strong>at hand</strong> 近在手边、即将到来</div><div class="item"><strong>acknowledge</strong> v. 承认、致谢</div><div class="item"><strong>lead at</strong> 在……方面领先</div><div class="item"><strong>open-source</strong> a. 开源的</div><div class="item"><strong>catch up</strong> 赶上、追赶</div><div class="item"><strong>despite</strong> prep. 尽管</div><div class="item"><strong>export control</strong> 出口管制</div><div class="item"><strong>critical</strong> a. 关键的；批评的</div><div class="item"><strong>the next leg</strong> 下一程、下一阶段（leg 熟词僻义：赛段）</div><div class="item"><strong>determine</strong> v. 决定、确定</div><div class="item"><strong>language model</strong> 语言模型</div><div class="item"><strong>intelligence</strong> n. 智能、情报</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">At the Hangzhou Technician Institute, for example, children can start from the age of 15. Some see this as a solution to China&#x27;s stubbornly high youth unemployment rate, with one in five struggling to find a job. &quot;Our students are in demand and can even be pre-booked by companies. They don&#x27;t need to worry about employment at all,&quot; Chen, the young teacher, says. &quot;My students ask me what will happen in the future,&quot; Chen says. &quot;I say I don&#x27;t know. Technology is changing so fast. But we can&#x27;t just stand still and sleep here. We have to move forward to see what we are capable of doing.&quot;</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">以杭州技师学院为例，学生最早可从 15 岁入学。有人视此为中国居高不下的青年失业率的一种解方——五人中就有一人难找工作。「我们的学生供不应求，甚至可被企业预订。他们完全不必担心就业，」年轻教师陈说。「学生问我未来会怎样，」陈说。「我说我不知道。技术变得太快。但我们不能原地站着睡觉。我们必须往前走，看看自己还能做成什么。」</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>for example</strong> 例如</div><div class="item"><strong>stubbornly</strong> ad. 顽固地、居高不下地</div><div class="item"><strong>youth unemployment rate</strong> 青年失业率</div><div class="item"><strong>one in five</strong> 五人中有一人、五分之一</div><div class="item"><strong>struggle to</strong> 努力去做却困难、难以……</div><div class="item"><strong>in demand</strong> 有需求的、抢手的</div><div class="item"><strong>pre-booked</strong> a. 被预先预订的</div><div class="item"><strong>employment</strong> n. 就业、雇用</div><div class="item"><strong>stand still</strong> 原地不动、停滞不前</div><div class="item"><strong>move forward</strong> 向前推进</div><div class="item"><strong>be capable of</strong> 有能力做……</div></div></div></div></section>
