Holiday experiences highlight the practical power of China's AI innovation

假日体验彰显中国 AI 创新的实用力量

来源：CGTN Opinion（作者 Qian Haoqi，复旦大学）· 2026-10-04 · 原文链接
先自己啃英文。中文译文、生词注释、文末复盘默认折叠，点一下才展开。

Artificial intelligence (AI) has quietly and pervasively found its way into ordinary daily consumption scenarios, reshaping public life in subtle yet profound ways. The 2026 National Day holiday, China's influential and large-scale national consumption event, is an ideal window to observe this silent technological upgrade. The widespread smart services across tourism, cultural consumption and daily life embody the concrete implementation of China's industrial development strategies this year.

人工智能（AI）已悄然而广泛地走进日常消费场景，以细微却深刻的方式重塑公共生活。2026 年国庆假期是中国影响广泛、规模庞大的全国性消费节点，正是观察这场“静默技术升级”的理想窗口。遍布文旅、文化消费与日常生活的智能服务，正是今年中国产业发展战略落地的具体体现。

embody v. 体现、具体表现
implementation n. 实施、落实

These strategies advance manufacturing intelligence, green development and integrated growth, and empower the service industry through demand-driven reform and technological innovation. They show how China's smart manufacturing stretches from industrial production lines to people's daily-life scenarios.

这些战略推进制造业智能化、绿色化与融合化发展，并通过需求牵引的改革与技术创新赋能服务业。它们展示了中国智能制造如何从工业生产线延伸到百姓日常生活场景。

Smart experiences empower cultural tourism

As cultural tourism consumption booms during the holiday, museums have become top travel destinations, with popular venues often fully booked. AI has fundamentally upgraded people's cultural experience. Tourists can pull up information about cultural relics, including their historical backgrounds and artistic values, simply by snapping a photo via mobile-based AI assistants.

【智能体验赋能文旅】随着假期文旅消费火热，博物馆成为热门目的地，热门场馆常常一票难求。AI 从根本上升级了人们的文化体验：游客只需用手机端 AI 助手拍张照，就能调出文物的历史背景、艺术价值等信息。

boom v. 繁荣、激增
destination n. 目的地
venue n. 场馆、场所
fully booked （票/位）全部订满
pull up 调出（信息）
cultural relic 文物
snap a photo 随手拍照

Museums are now offering a more advanced form of intelligent service to enhance visitors' on-site viewing experience. More and more provincial-level museums in China are rolling out smart-glass guided-tour services. Affordable and user-friendly, these AI-powered devices transform passive viewing into immersive, in-depth cultural exploration, enabling ordinary visitors to enjoy professional and refined smart tourism experiences. This booming AI-enabled cultural service perfectly interprets how technological empowerment is optimizing the service industry and meeting the diversified consumption demands of the public.

博物馆如今还提供更高级的智能服务，提升观众的现场观展体验。越来越多的中国省级博物馆推出智能眼镜导览服务。这些 AI 设备价格亲民、上手容易，把被动观看变成沉浸式、深度的文化探索，让普通游客也能享受专业而精致的智慧旅游体验。这类蓬勃发展的 AI 文化服务，生动诠释了技术赋能如何优化服务业、满足公众多样化的消费需求。

enhance v. 提升、增强
on-site adj. 现场的
roll out 推出、上线
affordable adj. 负担得起的、价格亲民的
transform A into B 把 A 转变为 B
immersive adj. 沉浸式的
refined adj. 精致的、讲究的
interpret v. 诠释、阐释
diversified adj. 多样化的

Inclusive AI breaks barriers

Mountain-view sightseeing ranks among the most popular holiday activities across China, attracting visitors of all age groups. Many look forward to hiking up famous mountains during their holiday trips, wishing to enjoy panoramic scenery from the summits. But the steep, winding mountain paths demand considerable physical strength and in the past, limited mobility could restrict the physically disadvantaged. Now, however, thanks to the iterative upgrading of China's smart manufacturing and AI algorithms, lightweight and intelligent wearable exoskeleton devices that fit over the body to boost strength, support posture, or assist with movement, are turning this wish into reality.

【普惠 AI 打破壁垒】登山观景是全国最受欢迎的假日活动之一，吸引着各年龄段的游客。许多人期待假期里登上名山，在山顶饱览全景。但陡峭蜿蜒的山路需要相当的体力，过去行动不便会把身体条件欠佳的人挡在门外。而如今，得益于中国智能制造与 AI 算法的迭代升级，轻量化、智能化的可穿戴外骨骼设备——穿在身上即可增强力量、支撑姿态或辅助行动——正把这一愿望变为现实。

inclusive adj. 普惠的、包容的
rank among 位列……之中
panoramic adj. 全景的
summit n. 山顶、顶峰
steep, winding 陡峭的、蜿蜒的
considerable adj. 相当大的
mobility n. 行动能力、移动性
the physically disadvantaged 身体条件欠佳者（the + adj. 表一类人）
iterative adj. 迭代的
exoskeleton n. 外骨骼
posture n. 姿势、姿态

Optimized by AI-powered gait recognition and adaptive power adjustment technologies, modern exoskeletons are light and adaptable to ordinary tourists' needs. Since 2025, major scenic spots have introduced such devices at affordable rental prices. For example, at Mount Tai, a peak with historical and cultural significance in Shandong Province, east China, more than 60% of exoskeleton renters are tourists aged 60 and above. With technological support, many more visitors can now reach mountain summits, demonstrating how smart-manufactured products enrich travel possibilities.

在 AI 步态识别与自适应助力调节技术的优化下，现代外骨骼轻便、能适应普通游客的需求。自 2025 年起，各大景区以亲民的租金引入这类设备。例如，在山东这座具有历史文化意义的泰山，超过六成外骨骼租用者是 60 岁及以上的游客。有了技术加持，更多游客得以登顶，这说明智能制造产品正在拓宽出行的可能。

gait recognition 步态识别
adaptive adj. 自适应的
adaptable to 能适应……的
scenic spot 景区、景点
rental adj./n. 租赁（的）
significance n. 意义、重要性
aged 60 and above 60 岁及以上
enrich v. 丰富、充实

Full industrial chain underpins AI adoption

The large-scale popularization and inclusive application of AI services in holiday consumption scenarios stems not from single technological breakthroughs, but from China's complete and mature intelligent manufacturing industrial chain. In recent years, China has developed a complete industrial ecosystem for AI, spanning upstream research on AI algorithms and core chips, mass production of smart hardware in the middle-stream, and downstream efforts to turn technical ideas into usable real-world products and business solutions.

【完整产业链支撑 AI 落地】AI 服务在假日消费场景中大规模普及、普惠应用，并非源于单点技术突破，而是源于中国完整、成熟的智能制造产业链。近年来，中国已形成完整的 AI 产业生态：上游是 AI 算法与核心芯片研发，中游是智能硬件规模化生产，下游则把技术构想转化为可用的现实产品与商业方案。

underpin v. 支撑、构成……的基础
popularization n. 普及

not from A, but from B 不是源于 A，而是源于 B（考研常见对比结构）
ecosystem n. 生态系统
span v. 跨越、涵盖
upstream / downstream 上游 / 下游
mass production 大规模生产、量产

This robust supply-chain infrastructure is helping emerging tech firms such as Huawei, Xiaomi and iFlytek build rich product lines for the consumer market. It is also enabling established home appliance brands to advance their technological transformation toward AI-powered offerings.

这套强健的供应链基础设施，正帮助华为、小米、科大讯飞等新兴科技企业为消费市场打造丰富的产品线，也让老牌家电品牌得以推进向 AI 产品的技术转型。

robust adj. 强健的、稳固的
infrastructure n. 基础设施
emerging adj. 新兴的
product line 产品线
established adj. 老牌的、地位稳固的
home appliance 家用电器
offering n. （企业提供的）产品或服务

China's super-large consumer market provides massive real-world scenario data for technological iteration, forming a positive cycle where market demands drive manufacturing upgrading, and optimized manufacturing further empowers consumer services.

中国超大规模的消费市场为技术迭代提供了海量真实场景数据，形成良性循环：市场需求推动制造升级，优化后的制造又进一步赋能消费服务。

massive adj. 海量的、巨大的
iteration n. 迭代
positive cycle 良性循环（= virtuous cycle）
where ... 关系副词引导定语从句，修饰 cycle
drive v. 推动、驱动

People-centered AI innovation fuels future breakthroughs

The prevalence of inclusive AI services during the National Day holiday vividly reflects the core philosophy of China's smart manufacturing development: Technological progress ultimately serves people's well-being. It pursues demand-driven, tech-empowered growth in the service sector, alongside intelligent, green and integrated upgrading within manufacturing. Drawing on its full industrial system and huge-scale domestic market, China keeps fostering real-world application scenarios and new business models for AI.

【以人为本的 AI 创新催生未来突破】国庆假期普惠 AI 服务的普及，生动体现了中国智能制造发展的核心理念：技术进步最终服务于人民福祉。它追求服务业需求牵引、技术赋能的增长，同时推进制造业智能化、绿色化、融合化升级。依托完整的工业体系和超大规模国内市场，中国持续培育 AI 的现实应用场景与新商业模式。

people-centered adj. 以人为本的
fuel v. 推动、助长

philosophy n. 理念、原则
well-being n. 福祉、幸福
alongside prep. 与……一起、同时

foster v. 培育、促进

Ultimately, just like the breakthrough debut of AI research DeepSeek in early 2025, which reshaped the industry with innovative technologies and inclusive application models, China will witness more such "DeepSeek moments" in the future. These technological and industrial breakthroughs will fuel China's high-quality industrial transformation. They will also offer valuable application scenarios, proven industrial experiences and inclusive development opportunities for global-level AI innovation and smart-manufacturing advancement.

归根结底，正如 2025 年初 DeepSeek 突破性亮相、以创新技术和普惠应用模式重塑了整个行业，中国未来还将迎来更多这样的“DeepSeek 时刻”。这些技术与产业突破将推动中国产业高质量转型，也将为全球 AI 创新与智能制造进步提供宝贵的应用场景、经过验证的产业经验和普惠的发展机遇。
