<section class="block"><p class="en">Until this regulation came in, the reach of AI companions in China was vast. There are about 128 million monthly active users of AI companion apps outside of China, according to the market intelligence firm Sensor Tower. Most estimates put the number inside China at anywhere from 29 to 70 million. China&#x27;s new rules cover apps designed as AI friends, boyfriends or girlfriends, but they also apply to any large language model that imitates the way a human interacts.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">在这项规定出台前，AI 陪伴产品在中国的覆盖面很大。市场情报公司 Sensor Tower 称，中国以外的 AI 陪伴应用月活约 1.28 亿；对中国国内的估计大多在 2900 万到 7000 万之间。新规覆盖被设计成 AI 朋友、男友或女友的应用，也适用于任何模仿人类互动方式的大语言模型。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>companion</strong> n. 同伴、陪伴者</div><div class="item"><strong>vast</strong> a. 巨大的、广阔的</div><div class="item"><strong>monthly active users</strong> 月活跃用户（MAU）</div><div class="item"><strong>estimate</strong> n./v. 估计</div><div class="item"><strong>anywhere from ... to ...</strong> 大约在……到……之间</div><div class="item"><strong>apply to</strong> 适用于</div><div class="item"><strong>large language model</strong> 大语言模型（LLM）</div><div class="item"><strong>imitate</strong> v. 模仿</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">The wording of the regulation is vague. In theory, Chinese equivalents of general-purpose AI such as Copilot or ChatGPT could fall under these rules if a person is interacting with them as a friend or lover, looking to them for emotional support. But it seems the real target is character chatbots — services specifically designed as a pseudo-relationship. The rules don&#x27;t apply to customer service bots or AI for education as long as they don&#x27;t provide &quot;care, companionship, support and other emotional services&quot;.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">规定措辞含糊。理论上，若有人把类似 Copilot 或 ChatGPT 的通用 AI 当朋友或恋人、向其寻求情感支持，中国对应产品也可能落入这些规则。但真正目标似乎是角色聊天机器人——专门设计成「伪关系」的服务。客服机器人或教育类 AI 只要不提供「关怀、陪伴、支持及其他情感服务」，就不适用这些规则。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>wording</strong> n. 措辞、用词</div><div class="item"><strong>vague</strong> a. 含糊的、模糊的</div><div class="item"><strong>in theory</strong> 理论上</div><div class="item"><strong>equivalent</strong> n. 对等物；a. 等价的</div><div class="item"><strong>general-purpose</strong> a. 通用的</div><div class="item"><strong>fall under</strong> 属于……范畴、受……管辖</div><div class="item"><strong>emotional support</strong> 情感支持</div><div class="item"><strong>character chatbot</strong> 角色聊天机器人</div><div class="item"><strong>pseudo-</strong> 前缀：伪的、假的</div><div class="item"><strong>companionship</strong> n. 陪伴、友谊</div></div></div></div></section>
