<section class="block"><p class="en">Preliminary research into AI&#x27;s effects on children&#x27;s learning also offers mixed signals, with some studies suggesting short-term gains while raising questions about longer-term intellectual development. A recent study examining nearly 27,000 Chinese teenagers found AI adoption was associated with an 18% increase in homework scores but a 20% decline in monthly examination scores within six months, with the biggest declines in social science subjects. The findings do not establish causation but reinforce teacher concerns about cognitive outsourcing, where children increasingly delegate mental tasks to technology rather than develop those skills themselves.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">关于人工智能对儿童学习影响的初步研究给出的信号也好坏参半：一些研究显示短期成绩有所提高，同时却对更长期的心智发展提出了疑问。一项近期研究考察了近 2.7 万名中国青少年，发现采用人工智能与作业成绩提高 18% 相关，但在六个月内月考成绩下降了 20%，其中社会科学学科降幅最大。这些发现并不能确立因果关系，但加深了教师对“认知外包”的担忧——孩子们越来越多地把脑力任务交给技术，而不是自己去发展这些能力。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>preliminary</strong> adj. 初步的</div><div class="item"><strong>mixed signals</strong> 好坏参半的信号、矛盾信号</div><div class="item"><strong>short-term gain</strong> 短期收益、短期提高</div><div class="item"><strong>intellectual</strong> adj. 心智的、智力的</div><div class="item"><strong>adoption</strong> n. 采用、采纳</div><div class="item"><strong>be associated with</strong> 与……相关</div><div class="item"><strong>decline</strong> n. 下降</div><div class="item"><strong>subject</strong> n. 学科（熟词僻义）</div><div class="item"><strong>establish</strong> v. 确立、证实</div><div class="item"><strong>causation</strong> n. 因果关系</div><div class="item"><strong>reinforce</strong> v. 加强、加深</div><div class="item"><strong>cognitive outsourcing</strong> 认知外包</div><div class="item"><strong>delegate</strong> v. 委派、把……交给</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">Some educators and parents are already experimenting with safeguards to keep AI from replacing critical thinking. At Beijing New Talent Academy, Peng said he tries to solve coding problems himself before asking AI for hints rather than complete answers. &quot;I think AI is a tool that helps me, not something I depend on.&quot; For Beijing parent Rita Luan, whose 11-year-old daughter attends a leading public school in the capital, the challenge is ensuring that AI strengthens rather than shortcuts learning. Her rule: spend 15 minutes trying to solve a difficult maths problem before turning to AI. Days later, Luan asks her to solve the same problem without help. &quot;The point is to make sure she actually digested the solution,&quot; she said.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">一些教育工作者和家长已经在尝试设立防护措施，以免人工智能取代批判性思维。在北京新英才学校，彭高迪说，他会先自己解决编程问题，再向人工智能求提示，而不是直接要完整答案。“我觉得人工智能是帮我的工具，不是让我依赖的东西。”对北京家长 Rita Luan 来说，她 11 岁的女儿在首都一所重点公办学校就读，难处在于确保人工智能强化学习，而不是让学习走捷径。她的规矩是：遇到难题先自己思考 15 分钟，再求助于人工智能。几天后，她会让女儿在没有帮助的情况下再解同一道题。“关键是要确保她真正消化了解题方法，”她说。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>experiment with</strong> 尝试、试验</div><div class="item"><strong>safeguard</strong> n. 防护措施</div><div class="item"><strong>keep ... from doing</strong> 防止……做某事</div><div class="item"><strong>critical thinking</strong> 批判性思维</div><div class="item"><strong>hint</strong> n. 提示</div><div class="item"><strong>depend on</strong> 依赖</div><div class="item"><strong>attend</strong> v. 就读、参加</div><div class="item"><strong>leading</strong> adj. 一流的、领先的</div><div class="item"><strong>ensure</strong> v. 确保</div><div class="item"><strong>strengthen</strong> v. 强化</div><div class="item"><strong>shortcut</strong> v. 走捷径、简化</div><div class="item"><strong>turn to</strong> 求助于</div><div class="item"><strong>digest</strong> v. 消化、真正理解（熟词僻义）</div><div class="item"><strong>solution</strong> n. 解法、解决方案</div></div></div></div></section>

<h2>复盘</h2>
<p><strong>主旨：</strong>中国正大力把人工智能纳入中小学教育，目标是到 2030 年实现学龄儿童人工智能素养普及。路透社调查显示，这一推进规模庞大但不均衡：既有精英学校的创造式应用，也有作业分升、月考分降和“认知外包”的隐忧。学生与家长则在探索如何让人工智能强化、而非取代思考。</p>
<p><strong>结构：</strong>北京私立名校个案（学生用 AI 做应用）→ 全国语境（近 2 亿中小学生、2030 年普及目标）→ 路透调查结论（规模大但不均衡、缺乏共识）→ 动机与中西对比（科技竞争、政府主导 vs 美国分散且非强制）→ 地方蓝图（课时、示范校、纳入升学考试）→ 研究警示（作业分升、月考分降、相关不等于因果、认知外包）→ 应对（先自己思考再求助 AI、事后复测）。</p>
<p><strong>考研词汇与搭配：</strong>pitch、weave into、curriculum、incorporate、the sharp end、undertake、literacy、universal、procurement、sweeping、uneven、consensus、rivalry、exposure、nurture、homegrown、outpace、fundamental、decentralized、nonbinding、dedicated、blueprint、mandate、binding、underscore、high-stakes、preliminary、mixed signals、associated with、causation、cognitive outsourcing、delegate、safeguard、critical thinking、shortcut、digest。熟词僻义：push（推进）、serve（在校规模）、examination（调查）、exposure（接触）、dedicated（专项的）、subject（学科）、digest（消化、理解）。</p>
<p><strong>阅读技巧：</strong>抓住“大力推进 vs 效果不均/存在风险”这条主对比。第 5 段 By contrast 对照中美路径（state-led 对 decentralized, nonbinding）。第 7 段数据对比（homework scores 升 18%，但 examination scores 降 20%）是典型细节题和推理题出题点；紧随其后的 do not establish causation 提醒不要把相关当成因果。第 8 段用个人规则回应前文“认知外包”，属于问题解决型收尾。注意 the sharp end、literacy、high-stakes 等搭配，以及第 4 段长句的“依据 + paints a picture + sweeping but uneven + little consensus”层次。</p>
