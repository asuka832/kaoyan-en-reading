<section class="block"><p class="en">The OfS said it had heard of cases where students were completing entire assessments within minutes using AI and receiving similar grades to those who had not. Some staff, meanwhile, were receiving lots of assignments that looked the same, and did not think their institution&#x27;s assessments were &quot;fit for purpose&quot;. Other staff described a &quot;polarised atmosphere&quot; between students who used AI and other &quot;conscientious objectors&quot; who avoided it on environmental and other grounds.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">OfS 称，它听说过一些案例：学生用 AI 在几分钟内完成整份考核，拿到的分数却与没用 AI 的学生相近。与此同时，一些教职员工收到大量看起来雷同的作业，认为本校的考核“不合要求”。另一些员工则描述了一种“两极分化的氛围”：一边是使用 AI 的学生，另一边是出于环保等理由拒绝使用 AI 的“良心拒绝者”。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>entire</strong> adj. 整个的、全部的</div><div class="item"><strong>assignment</strong> n. 作业、任务</div><div class="item"><strong>fit for purpose</strong> 适合其用途的、合格的</div><div class="item"><strong>polarised</strong> adj. 两极分化的</div><div class="item"><strong>conscientious objector</strong> （出于良心的）拒绝者；原指拒服兵役者</div><div class="item"><strong>on ... grounds</strong> 基于……理由</div></div></div></div></section>
