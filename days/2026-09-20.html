China's CXMT says new memory-chip platform enters mass production

BEIJING, Sept 20 (Reuters) — Chinese DRAM chipmaker CXMT (688825.SS) said on Sunday its fifth-generation technology platform had entered mass production, a claimed breakthrough that could help China build a stronger competitor to Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix and Micron Technology in the global market for memory chips.

路透社北京 9 月 20 日电 —— 中国 DRAM 芯片制造商长鑫存储（CXMT，688825.SS）周日表示，其第五代技术平台已进入量产。这一自称的突破，或有助于中国在全球存储芯片市场里，对三星电子、SK 海力士与美光科技形成更有力的竞争。

DRAM, or dynamic random-access memory, is the short-term working memory that phones, computers and other devices use to run apps and programs.

DRAM，即动态随机存取存储器，是手机、电脑等设备用来运行应用与程序的短期工作内存。

dynamic /daɪˈnæmɪk/ adj. 动态的

The new platform is designed to make more powerful memory chips at lower cost and with less power use. CXMT, which listed on Shanghai's STAR Market this year, said it would give electronics makers an additional source of supply for chips used in smartphones and other devices.

新平台旨在以更低成本、更低功耗制造更强的存储芯片。今年已在上海科创板上市的长鑫存储称，这将为电子厂商提供智能手机等设备所用芯片的额外供应来源。

STAR Market 科创板（上海证券交易所科技板）

CXMT, China's leading producer of DRAM, said the new platform packs the tiny structures that store data closer together, allowing more memory to fit on each chip and more individual chips to be made from each silicon wafer.

作为中国领先的 DRAM 生产商，长鑫存储表示，新平台把存储数据的微细结构排布得更紧密，从而使每颗芯片装下更多内存，也让每片硅晶圆能切出更多独立芯片。

structure /ˈstrʌktʃə(r)/ n. 结构

The Hefei-based memory-chip maker said it had reduced the spacing of key features in the part of the chip that stores data to 11.95 nanometres, or about 12 billionths of a metre.

这家总部位于合肥的存储芯片厂商称，已将芯片存储区关键特征的间距缩小至 11.95 纳米，约等于一米的一百二十亿分之一。

Hefei-based 总部位于合肥的

It achieved this using "quadruple patterning," a process that repeats several manufacturing steps to produce finer circuit patterns. "Our process capability is now on par with the most advanced mass-produced nodes out there in the industry," Luo Xiaodong, CXMT's vice president and head of its marketing centre, said at the 2026 World Manufacturing Convention in Hefei.

公司借助「四重曝光」（quadruple patterning）实现这一目标——该工艺通过重复多道制造步骤，以做出更精细的电路图形。长鑫存储副总裁兼市场中心负责人罗晓东在合肥举行的 2026 世界制造业大会上说：「我们的工艺能力现已与业界最先进的量产节点相当。」

CXMT also unveiled two 24-gigabit LPDDR5X products made on the new platform. LPDDR5X is a power-saving type of DRAM used mainly in smartphones and other portable electronics. The products each hold 50% more data than CXMT's previous equivalent products and are already in mass production, the company said.

长鑫存储还发布了基于新平台的两款 24 吉比特（Gb）LPDDR5X 产品。LPDDR5X 是一种省电型 DRAM，主要用于智能手机及其他便携电子产品。公司称，这两款产品各自的数据容量比此前同类产品高出 50%，且已进入量产。

CXMT said the platform can produce at least 50% more gross chip dies per wafer than its fourth-generation platform, using an 8-gigabit-chip baseline. That measures the potential number of chips made from a wafer before defective units are excluded, rather than the proportion that pass final testing.

长鑫存储称，以 8 吉比特芯片为基准，该平台每片晶圆可产出的毛晶粒（gross dies）数量至少比第四代平台多 50%。这一指标衡量的是剔除缺陷品之前、可从晶圆上切出的芯片数量，而非最终测试通过的比例。

gross /ɡrəʊs/ adj. 毛的、未扣除的

baseline /ˈbeɪslaɪn/ n. 基准线、参照标准
defective /dɪˈfektɪv/ adj. 有缺陷的
exclude /ɪkˈskluːd/ v. 排除、剔除
proportion /prəˈpɔːʃn/ n. 比例

CXMT said it developed the platform using computer simulations and joint work with Chinese chip-equipment makers on critical production steps. The advance comes as Beijing seeks to reduce reliance on foreign semiconductor technology and US export controls since 2022 have restricted China's access to certain advanced chipmaking equipment and related software.

长鑫存储称，该平台依托计算机仿真，并与中国芯片设备厂商在关键生产环节开展合作而开发。这一进展正值北京寻求降低对外国半导体技术的依赖；同时，自 2022 年以来，美国出口管制已限制中国获取部分先进芯片制造设备及相关软件。

chip-equipment maker 芯片设备制造商
