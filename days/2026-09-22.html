OpenAI offers AI for chip design, touts cost advantage over open-source, CFO says

来源：Reuters · 原文链接

OpenAI is pushing its AI into specialized industries and undercutting open-source rivals on cost, Chief Financial Officer Sarah Friar said on Monday, as the ChatGPT maker sees accelerating enterprise growth.

OpenAI 首席财务官莎拉·弗赖尔周一表示，该公司正把人工智能推向专业行业，并以更低成本压制开源对手，同时 ChatGPT 母公司看到企业端增长在加速。

specialized adj. 专业的、专用的
undercut v. 削价竞争、削弱
rival n. 对手
accelerating adj./v. 加速的；加速
enterprise n. 企业；企业级业务

Speaking at Goldman Sachs' Communacopia + Technology Conference in San Francisco, Friar said OpenAI is focusing on sectors including chip design, life sciences and financial services, as businesses increasingly seek AI systems tailored to specific tasks. It also is experimenting with pricing based on business outcomes rather than use, as more enterprises demand return on investment in AI.

在旧金山高盛 Communacopia + Technology 大会上，弗赖尔称 OpenAI 正聚焦芯片设计、生命科学和金融服务等领域，因为企业越来越需要针对具体任务定制的 AI 系统。公司也在试验按业务结果而非按用量定价，因更多企业要求看到 AI 投入的回报。

sector n. 行业、部门
life sciences 生命科学
tailored to 为……量身定制
outcome n. 结果、成效
return on investment 投资回报（ROI）

The push into industry-specific AI and aggressive pricing reflects the growing competition OpenAI faces from Chinese open-weight models and rivals such as Anthropic, as enterprise customers demand more measurable returns from AI spending.

进军行业专用 AI 并采取激进定价，反映出 OpenAI 正面临来自中国开源权重模型以及 Anthropic 等对手的加剧竞争，同时企业客户要求从 AI 支出中获得更可衡量的回报。

industry-specific adj. 行业专用的
aggressive adj. 激进的、进取的
open-weight 开源权重（权重可公开获取）的
measurable adj. 可衡量的
spending n. 支出

Friar said OpenAI had a positive experience of its own, using its own models in developing its Jalapeno chip, which was "taped out", a stage when a chip design is finalized and sent to a factory for production, within nine months.

弗赖尔说，OpenAI 自己也有正面体验：用自家模型开发命名为 Jalapeno 的芯片，并在九个月内完成了“流片”——即芯片设计定稿并送交工厂量产的阶段。

positive experience 正面体验
tape out 流片（设计定稿并送厂）
finalize v. 敲定、使最终确定
within nine months 在九个月内

Open-source and open-weight models are widely seen as a cheaper alternative to frontier models from OpenAI and Anthropic. Friar said OpenAI recently cut the price of its lower-cost Luna model by 80%, helping drive a roughly 10-fold increase in usage. OpenAI's Codex, its coding tool, is attracting customers and has 25 million users, she said.

开源与开源权重模型普遍被视为 OpenAI、Anthropic 等前沿模型的更便宜替代品。弗赖尔称，OpenAI 近期将其低成本 Luna 模型降价 80%，推动用量约增至十倍；其编程工具 Codex 也在吸引客户，已有 2500 万用户。

alternative n. 替代方案
frontier model 前沿大模型
10-fold 十倍的
usage n. 使用量
coding tool 编程工具

Friar said enterprise revenue increased 32% from June to July, compared with 20% growth in overall annualized revenue during the period. Enterprise and consumer businesses had reached roughly an even split by the middle of the year, ahead of OpenAI's target of reaching that balance by year-end, she said.

弗赖尔表示，企业收入从 6 月到 7 月增长了 32%，同期整体年化收入增长为 20%。到年中，企业业务与消费业务大致已各占一半，早于 OpenAI 原定年底实现这一平衡的目标。

revenue n. 收入、营收
annualized adj. 年化的
even split 对半分、大致各半
ahead of 早于、领先于
year-end 年底

Deploying OpenAI's lower-cost model could be cheaper than running Chinese open-source alternatives through cloud providers, she added. "If you're deploying Luna and compare that to (Z.ai's) GLM 5.3, for example, on a cloud layer, we are cheaper," Friar said.

她还补充说，部署 OpenAI 的低成本模型，可能比通过云厂商运行中国开源替代方案更便宜。“例如，如果你部署 Luna，再和（Z.ai 的）GLM 5.3 在云层上比，我们更便宜，”弗赖尔说。

deploy v. 部署
cloud provider 云服务商
cloud layer 云层（云基础设施层面）
compare ... to 把……与……相比
