<section class="block"><p class="en">The school has built a distinctive disciplinary system for rare and endangered fields, covering more than 10 ancient ethnic scripts, including Turkic, Tangut, Khitan, Jurchen, Manchu, and Tibetan. It has also set up teaching and research departments for oracle bone and bamboo and silk manuscript studies, ancient scripts of northern ethnic groups, ancient scripts of southwestern ethnic groups, and Chinese and Western classical studies.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">该学院已构建起一套针对冷门绝学的特色学科体系，覆盖突厥文、西夏文、契丹文、女真文、满文和藏文等 10 余种古代民族文字。学院还设立了甲骨与简帛研究、北方民族古文字、西南民族古文字以及中西古典学等教研机构。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>disciplinary system</strong> 学科体系</div><div class="item"><strong>covering</strong> 覆盖</div><div class="item"><strong>Turkic</strong> 突厥文</div><div class="item"><strong>Tangut</strong> 西夏文</div><div class="item"><strong>Khitan</strong> 契丹文</div><div class="item"><strong>Jurchen</strong> 女真文</div><div class="item"><strong>Manchu</strong> 满文</div><div class="item"><strong>Tibetan</strong> 藏文</div><div class="item"><strong>set up</strong> 设立</div><div class="item"><strong>teaching and research department</strong> 教研机构 / 教研部</div><div class="item"><strong>northern ethnic groups</strong> 北方民族</div><div class="item"><strong>southwestern ethnic groups</strong> 西南民族</div></div></div></div></section>
