<section class="block"><p class="en">Oliver Hayes, head of campaigns at environmental charity Global Action Plan, said: &quot;If we&#x27;re powering by gas that means local communities are going to have huge gas turbines on their doorstep, and they are going to be generating a huge amount of local air pollution and a huge amount of noise and they will be using a huge amount of water from the local water supply.&quot; Buckinghamshire councillor Ed Gemmell warned that, if allowed, it &quot;sets a precedent&quot; that would make it extremely difficult to hit environmental and net zero targets.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">环保慈善机构“全球行动计划”的运动负责人奥利弗·海斯说：“如果用天然气供电，就意味着当地社区家门口会出现巨大的燃气轮机，它们会产生大量的本地空气污染和噪音，还会消耗当地供水系统中的大量水资源。”白金汉郡议员埃德·杰梅尔警告说，一旦获准，这将“开创先例”，使实现环境和净零目标变得极其困难。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>campaign</strong> n. 运动、宣传活动</div><div class="item"><strong>charity</strong> n. 慈善机构</div><div class="item"><strong>turbine</strong> n. 涡轮机</div><div class="item"><strong>on one&#x27;s doorstep</strong> 就在家门口、离得很近</div><div class="item"><strong>councillor</strong> n. （地方）议员</div><div class="item"><strong>set a precedent</strong> 开创先例</div><div class="item"><strong>hit a target</strong> 实现目标</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">The government said its priority was connecting data centres to the grid so they could benefit from the UK&#x27;s rapidly decarbonising power sector. It said any behind-the-meter power generation would be tightly controlled, time-limited and allowed only for exceptionally strategic projects. &quot;Pausing development would send jobs, investment and data overseas and leave us more reliant on other countries for critical digital infrastructure,&quot; a government spokesperson added. &quot;That&#x27;s why we&#x27;re backing their growth while making sure it&#x27;s done responsibly.&quot;</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">政府表示，其首要任务是让数据中心接入电网，从而受益于英国快速脱碳的电力行业。政府称，任何“表后”（自发自用）发电都将受到严格控制、有时间限制，并且只允许用于极具战略意义的项目。一名政府发言人补充说：“暂停开发会把就业、投资和数据推向海外，让我们在关键数字基础设施上更加依赖其他国家。这就是为什么我们一边支持其发展，一边确保以负责任的方式进行。”</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>decarbonise</strong> v. 脱碳、减少碳排放</div><div class="item"><strong>sector</strong> n. 行业、部门</div><div class="item"><strong>behind-the-meter</strong> adj. 表后的（用户侧自发自用的）</div><div class="item"><strong>exceptionally</strong> adv. 特别地、格外地</div><div class="item"><strong>strategic</strong> adj. 战略的</div><div class="item"><strong>reliant on</strong> 依赖……的</div><div class="item"><strong>critical</strong> adj. 关键的；批评的（一词多义）</div><div class="item"><strong>infrastructure</strong> n. 基础设施</div><div class="item"><strong>back</strong> v. 支持（熟词僻义）</div></div></div></div></section>

<h2>复盘</h2>
<p><strong>主旨：</strong>BBC 调查发现，英国政府为吸引 AI 数据中心、允许其在等待电网接入时现场自建发电，已有四个超大规模项目考虑用燃气发电，排放可能超过三座大城市之和。文章呈现了“经济增长 / 数字基础设施”与“净零减排 / 社区环境”之间的张力。</p>
<p><strong>结构：</strong>核心发现（政策可能削弱净零目标）→ 议员批评 → 背景：什么是数据中心、新一代为何更耗电 → 数据对比（72 GW 申请 vs 45 GW 峰值）与电网瓶颈 → 政策细节与 BBC 测算 → 环保组织与地方议员的担忧 → 政府回应：优先并网、严格限制、发展不可停。</p>
<p><strong>考研词汇与搭配：</strong>undermine、ambition、substantial、a vast range of、come on stream、cope with、sophisticated、considerably、predecessor、struggle to、at capacity、in return for、permit、set a precedent、hit a target、reliant on、infrastructure。熟词僻义：landscape（格局）、back（支持）、due to（预计将）、oversight（监督）、critical（关键的）。</p>
<p><strong>阅读技巧：</strong>第 5 段 “Such is the demand ..., (that) some developers have been told ...” 是 such 引导的倒装结构，表“如此……以至于”，长难句题常考。这类“政策 + 多方回应”的报道，做态度题时要区分：BBC 调查与议员、环保组织偏批评，政府则强调“必要且受控”；作者本身以客观陈述为主。数字对比（72 vs 45 吉瓦、4.1 百万吨）常被用作细节题干扰项。</p>
