Generative AI users in China exceed 700 million

中国生成式人工智能用户规模突破 7 亿

BEIJING, Sept. 29 (Xinhua) -- Users of generative artificial intelligence (AI) in China had surpassed 700 million in number by the end of June 2026, according to a report released on Tuesday.

新华社北京 9 月 29 日电——据周二发布的一份报告，截至 2026 年 6 月底，中国生成式人工智能（AI）用户规模已突破 7 亿。

generative AI 生成式人工智能
surpass v. 超过、超越
according to 根据……

The penetration rate of generative AI had exceeded 50 percent, the report by the China Internet Network Information Center noted. There had been a diversified range of application scenarios for this technology, said the report, adding that the main scenario is to use generative AI as a conversation tool. A total of 76 percent of users employed it to answer questions, it revealed.

中国互联网络信息中心的报告指出，生成式 AI 渗透率已超过 50%。报告称，该技术的应用场景日趋多元，而主要场景是将其作为对话工具；共有 76% 的用户用它来回答问题。

penetration rate 渗透率
diversified adj. 多样化的
application scenario 应用场景
employ v. 使用、运用
reveal v. 透露、显示

Noting that the potential of consumption regarding intelligent technology, with purchases of smart devices as a representative, had been unleashed at a faster speed, the report said that 38.7 percent of internet users bought smart devices online during the past six months.

报告指出，以智能设备购买为代表的智能技术消费潜力正以更快速度释放：过去六个月，有 38.7% 的网民在线购买了智能设备。

potential n. 潜力
regarding prep. 关于
representative n. 代表（此处：以……为代表）
unleash v. 释放、激发
smart device 智能设备

China's first homegrown AI supercluster boasting 100,000 graphics processing units had been put into use, said the report, adding that this underpinned the country's independent innovation regarding AI.

报告称，中国首个拥有 10 万块图形处理器（GPU）的国产 AI 超算集群已投入使用，这为国内 AI 自主创新提供了支撑。

homegrown adj. 国产的、本土的
supercluster n. 超算集群 / 超级集群
boast v. （自豪地）拥有
graphics processing unit (GPU) 图形处理器
put into use 投入使用
underpin v. 支撑、奠定基础
independent innovation 自主创新

Generative AI had been empowering in-depth industrial transformation and upgrading, making various sectors smarter, said the report. It also said that embodied AI had represented one of the most active areas in AI development. With increasingly improved capabilities for performing sophisticated tasks, embodied AI robots had been applied in many sectors like industrial manufacturing, surgical operations and everyday life services.

报告称，生成式 AI 正赋能产业深度转型升级，推动各行业更智能。报告还指出，具身智能是当前 AI 发展最活跃的领域之一；随着完成复杂任务的能力不断提升，具身智能机器人已应用于工业制造、外科手术与日常生活服务等多个领域。

empower v. 赋能、使能够
in-depth adj. 深入的
transformation and upgrading 转型升级
embodied AI 具身智能
sophisticated adj. 复杂的、精密的
surgical adj. 外科的、手术的

The report was made public at the 2026 China Internet Infrastructure Resources Conference in Beijing.

该报告在北京举行的 2026 中国互联网基础资源大会上公开发布。

make public 公开发布 / 公布
infrastructure n. 基础设施
conference n. 会议、大会
