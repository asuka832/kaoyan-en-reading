S. Korea's exports hit record high in September on record-breaking AI chip demand

SEOUL, Oct. 1 (Xinhua) -- South Korea's exports hit a record high in September, topping 120 billion U.S. dollars for the first time thanks to a record-breaking global demand for artificial intelligence (AI) semiconductors, government data showed Thursday.

新华社首尔 10 月 1 日电——政府数据周四显示，得益于人工智能（AI）半导体全球需求破纪录，韩国 9 月出口创历史新高，首次突破 1200 亿美元。

hit a record high 创历史新高
top / topping v. 超过、突破（金额上限）
record-breaking adj. 破纪录的
semiconductor n. 半导体
government data showed 政府数据显示（新闻导语常用）

The outbound shipments, which account for about half of the export-led economy, soared 83.5 percent from a year earlier to 120.94 billion dollars in September, according to the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources.

据产业通商资源部数据，出口约占韩国出口导向型经济的一半；9 月出货额同比飙升 83.5%，达 1209.4 亿美元。

outbound shipments 对外出货/出口发货额
account for 占……（比例）
export-led economy 出口导向型经济
soar v. 飙升、猛增
from a year earlier 同比（与去年同期相比）
Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources （韩国）产业通商资源部

For the first nine months of this year, cumulative exports reached 814.5 billion dollars, overtaking last year's record-setting annual performance of 709.3 billion dollars. The daily average exports spiked 105 percent to 5.63 billion dollars, surpassing 5 billion dollars for the first time. Imports grew 26.0 percent to 71.09 billion dollars last month, sending the trade surplus to an all-time high of 49.85 billion dollars. Of the country's 20 major export items, 11 products saw growth in outbound shipments.

今年前九个月累计出口达 8145 亿美元，已超过去年创纪录的全年 7093 亿美元。日均出口同比激增 105%，至 56.3 亿美元，首次突破 50 亿美元。上月进口增长 26.0%，至 710.9 亿美元，贸易顺差随之升至历史新高 498.5 亿美元。在 20 大主要出口品类中，有 11 类出货增长。

cumulative adj. 累计的、累积的
overtake v. 超过、赶超
record-setting adj. 创纪录的

Semiconductor exports skyrocketed 262.8 percent to 60.30 billion dollars, crossing 60 billion dollars for the first time. The milestone was attributed to the continuous expansion of AI infrastructure investments that led to price hikes in memory chips. Computer exports surged 435.3 percent to 7.00 billion dollars, boosted by solid demand for solid-state drives (SSDs) used in AI infrastructure. Mobile phone shipments mounted 23.4 percent to 2.14 billion dollars, extending its growth streak to 11 months due to strong demand for premium smartphones, while display panel exports retreated in single digits.

半导体出口暴增 262.8%，至 603.0 亿美元，首次跨过 600 亿美元关口。这一里程碑归因于 AI 基础设施投资持续扩张推高存储芯片价格。电脑出口飙升 435.3%，至 70.0 亿美元，受 AI 基建所用固态硬盘（SSD）需求坚挺带动。手机出货增长 23.4%，至 21.4 亿美元，因高端智能手机需求旺盛，连续增长月数拉长至 11 个月；显示面板出口则个位数回落。

memory chip 存储芯片

Automotive exports declined 5.5 percent to 6.05 billion dollars, impacted by the shift of Chuseok holidays, the South Korean version of Thanksgiving Day, while auto parts shipments fell in single figures. Exports for ships jumped 29.9 percent to 3.76 billion dollars, driven by higher deliveries of high-value ships including liquefied natural gas (LNG) tankers and very large gas carriers (VLGCs). Shipments of oil products soared 72.0 percent to 7.22 billion dollars, and petrochemicals exports rose 5.1 percent to 3.95 billion dollars as geopolitical risks in the Red Sea and the Strait of Hormuz pushed up global oil prices despite a drop in shipment volumes.

汽车出口下降 5.5%，至 60.5 亿美元，受中秋（韩国版感恩节）假期挪移影响，汽车零部件出货亦个位数下滑。船舶出口跳升 29.9%，至 37.6 亿美元，主因液化天然气（LNG）船、超大型气体运输船（VLGC）等高价值船舶交付增加。石油产品出货飙升 72.0%，至 72.2 亿美元；石化产品出口升 5.1%，至 39.5 亿美元——尽管出货量下降，红海与霍尔木兹海峡地缘风险推高了全球油价。

Rechargeable battery exports advanced 14.0 percent to 720 million dollars, marking five straight months of gains on the back of higher mineral prices and a booming energy storage system (ESS) market. Steel products shipments swelled 3.3 percent to 2.24 billion dollars on strong U.S. demand for steel sheets used for AI data center construction, but general machinery exports shrank 3.2 percent to 4.07 billion dollars owing to fewer working days and rising logistics costs.

充电电池出口增长 14.0%，至 7.2 亿美元，在矿产价格走高与储能系统（ESS）市场火热支撑下，连续五个月增长。钢铁产品出货增加 3.3%，至 22.4 亿美元，受美国对 AI 数据中心用钢板需求强劲拉动；但通用机械出口因工作日减少、物流成本上升而收缩 3.2%，至 40.7 亿美元。

Exports to the United States shot up 137.0 percent to 24.33 billion dollars as a result of robust demand for chips, oil products, machinery and new vehicle models. Shipments to the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) jumped 92.6 percent to 21.29 billion dollars, while exports to the European Union (EU) picked up 20.9 percent to 8.65 billion dollars.

对美出口猛增 137.0%，至 243.3 亿美元，得益于芯片、石油产品、机械与新车型需求强劲。对东盟（ASEAN）出货跳升 92.6%，至 212.9 亿美元；对欧盟（EU）出口回升 20.9%，至 86.5 亿美元。

ASEAN 东南亚国家联盟（东盟）
pick up （经济/出口）回升、好转
European Union (EU) 欧盟

Regarding import items, the import of three major energy sources, including crude oil, natural gas and coal, gained 38.0 percent to 12.96 billion dollars last month. Non-energy imports mounted 23.6 percent to 58.13 billion dollars on strong demand for semiconductor equipment and mobile devices.

进口方面，原油、天然气与煤炭三大能源进口上月增长 38.0%，至 129.6 亿美元。非能源进口因半导体设备与移动设备需求强劲而增加 23.6%，至 581.3 亿美元。
