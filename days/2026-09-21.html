Scientists worried about impact of AI on deep thinking as study shows early effects on learning

来源：ABC News · 原文链接

There are early signs the use of artificial intelligence is impacting learning, with a study finding use of the technology lifted homework results, while exam scores fell by 20 per cent. Some young people are boycotting the technology, while neuroscientists are worried deep thinking being increasingly offloaded to AI could impact the development of neural connections in the brain.

有早期迹象显示，使用人工智能正在影响学习：一项研究发现，使用该技术提高了作业成绩，但考试分数下降了20%。一些年轻人正在抵制这项技术；神经科学家担心，把深度思考外包给AI可能影响大脑神经连接的发育。

impact v./n. 影响、冲击
boycott v. 抵制
offload v. 外包、卸载
neural adj. 神经的

With the widespread use of artificial intelligence in schools and universities, there are concerns about its impact on learning and ultimately young brains. AI can be a great tool and can help stimulate critical thinking if used in the right way. But a recent study of students in China highlighted problems with offloading hard mental work to AI.

随着人工智能在学校和大学广泛使用，人们开始担心它对学习以及年轻大脑的影响。AI如果使用得当，可以帮助激发批判性思维；但一项针对中国学生的研究凸显了把费脑的思维劳动外包给AI所带来的问题。

widespread adj. 广泛的
ultimately adv. 最终
stimulate v. 激发
critical thinking 批判性思维
highlight v. 突出、强调

Published this year, the study of more than 26,000 students in grades 7 to 12 looked at how generative AI affected homework productivity and learning. It found AI use lifted homework scores by 18 per cent and improved completion times by 30 per cent. But it lowered monthly exam scores by 20 per cent within six months.

这项覆盖七至十二年级逾2.6万名学生的研究发现，使用生成式AI使作业分数提高18%、完成时间缩短30%，但六个月内月考分数下降了20%。

generative AI 生成式人工智能
productivity n. 效率
completion n. 完成
within six months 在六个月内

Learning and deep thinking physically alter the human brain, creating new connections between neurons. It is known as brain plasticity. Over time, critical thinking, learning, and other cognitive tasks help build robust nerve networks that underlie what is known as cognitive reserve.

学习和深度思考会从生理上改变人脑，在神经元之间建立新的连接，这被称为大脑可塑性。随着时间推移，批判性思维、学习及其他认知任务会建立稳健的神经网络，构成所谓的认知储备。

physically adv. 从生理上
alter v. 改变
neuron n. 神经元
brain plasticity 大脑可塑性
cognitive reserve 认知储备

They might read the AI-generated text and understand what it says, but they never went through the struggle of working through the ideas and how they might fit in the overall picture. Some young people are so worried about the impact artificial intelligence could have on their learning they have decided to boycott it.

他们或许能读懂AI生成的文本，却从未经历自己梳理思路、把想法放进整体图景的挣扎过程。一些年轻人担心人工智能对学习的影响，以致决定抵制它。

AI may drive a transformation in how educators teach and how student learning is assessed. It may be possible to adapt AI engines to encourage critical thinking and learning, so that the AI enables learning rather than replacing it. How learning is assessed will matter more than ever.

AI可能推动教学方式和学习评估发生变革。或许可以改造AI引擎来鼓励批判性思维与学习，让AI促成学习而不是取代学习；如何评估学习也将比以往更加重要。
