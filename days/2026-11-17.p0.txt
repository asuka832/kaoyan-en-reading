<section class="block"><p class="en">Proctors will check every pair of glasses and every watch worn by test-takers at Thursday&#x27;s national college entrance exam, Prime Minister Han Seong-sook said Tuesday, announcing tighter checks to prevent artificial intelligence-assisted cheating. Speaking at a Cabinet meeting two days before the College Scholastic Ability Test, or Suneung, is held nationwide, Han described AI-assisted cheating as a &quot;social evil&quot; and said the government would activate an interagency emergency response team.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">韩国总理韩圣淑周二宣布，将在周四的全国大学入学考试中检查所有考生佩戴的眼镜和手表，并收紧监考措施，以防止人工智能辅助作弊。她在大学修学能力考试（韩国称“修能”）全国开考两天前的内阁会议上发言，将人工智能辅助作弊称为“社会之恶”，并表示政府将启动跨部门应急响应小组。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>proctor</strong> n. 监考人员</div><div class="item"><strong>test-taker</strong> n. 考生</div><div class="item"><strong>entrance exam</strong> 入学考试、升学考试</div><div class="item"><strong>tighter checks</strong> 更严格的检查/抽查</div><div class="item"><strong>announce</strong> v. 宣布</div><div class="item"><strong>Cabinet</strong> n. 内阁</div><div class="item"><strong>nationwide</strong> adv./adj. 在全国范围内</div><div class="item"><strong>describe...as</strong> 把……称为</div><div class="item"><strong>social evil</strong> 社会之恶、社会毒瘤</div><div class="item"><strong>activate</strong> v. 启动、激活</div><div class="item"><strong>interagency</strong> adj. 跨部门的</div><div class="item"><strong>emergency response team</strong> 应急响应小组</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">&quot;All smart devices, including smartphones, will be switched off and collected, while proctors will inspect students&#x27; glasses and watches, as well as collars, sleeves and buttons,&quot; Han said. The government will deploy additional proctors and require test-takers to leave their bags in hallways or other areas outside exam rooms. Officials will periodically check whether electronic devices are operating inside the bags.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">韩圣淑表示：“所有智能设备，包括智能手机，都将被关闭并收缴；监考人员还将检查学生的眼镜和手表，以及衣领、袖口和纽扣。”政府将增派监考人员，并要求考生把包放在走廊或考场以外的其他区域。工作人员还会定期检查包内是否有电子设备在运行。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>smart device</strong> 智能设备</div><div class="item"><strong>switch off</strong> 关闭（电源）</div><div class="item"><strong>collect</strong> v. 收缴、收集（熟词僻义）</div><div class="item"><strong>inspect</strong> v. 检查、查看</div><div class="item"><strong>collar</strong> n. 衣领</div><div class="item"><strong>sleeve</strong> n. 袖子、袖口</div><div class="item"><strong>deploy</strong> v. 部署、增派</div><div class="item"><strong>hallway</strong> n. 走廊</div><div class="item"><strong>periodically</strong> adv. 定期地、不时地</div><div class="item"><strong>operate</strong> v. 运行、工作（熟词僻义）</div></div></div></div></section>
