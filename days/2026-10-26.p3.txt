<section class="block"><p class="en">So why is China taking the lead? &quot;The Chinese government has a much more paternalistic view of its role in society,&quot; says Sam Bresnick of Georgetown University. <section class="block"><p class="en">Despite the ban, people who turned to AI for companionship remain committed.
