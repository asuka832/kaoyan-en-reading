China's Huawei says AI chip demand outstrips supply as it steps up Nvidia challenge

华为称 AI 芯片供不应求，加码挑战英伟达

来源：Reuters · 2026-09-17 · 原文链接
先自己啃英文。中文译文、生词注释、文末复盘默认折叠，点一下才展开。

Huawei Technologies cannot produce enough artificial-intelligence computing equipment to meet demand in China and is limiting overseas sales, rotating chairman Eric Xu said on Thursday, as the Chinese tech giant steps up its challenge to Nvidia.

轮值董事长徐直军周四表示，华为无法生产足够的人工智能计算设备以满足中国国内需求，并正在限制海外销售；与此同时，这家中国科技巨头正加码挑战英伟达。

outstrip v. 超过、超越（需求 outstrips supply = 供不应求）
computing equipment 计算设备、算力设备
meet demand 满足需求
rotating chairman 轮值董事长
step up 加强、加码推进
challenge to 对……的挑战

Xu said testing of Huawei's Ascend 950DT AI chip had produced good results and the company was in extensive discussions with Chinese AI model developers. He expected many of them to begin training models on systems using the chip next year.

徐直军称，华为昇腾 950DT 人工智能芯片的测试效果良好，公司正与中国多家 AI 大模型开发商进行广泛洽谈。他预计，明年将有不少开发商开始在搭载该芯片的系统上训练模型。

testing n. 测试
produce good results 取得良好结果
extensive adj. 广泛的、深入的
discussion n. 讨论、洽谈
model developer 模型开发商
train models 训练模型

"Since we don't have enough capacity to even satisfy the demand in China, we don't have a plan to expand into the international market in a fully-fledged way," Xu told reporters at the company's Huawei Connect conference in Shanghai.

徐直军在上海举办的华为全联接大会上对记者说：“由于我们连中国国内的需求都无法满足，目前没有以全面方式拓展国际市场的计划。”

capacity n. 产能、能力
satisfy v. 满足
expand into 拓展进入
fully-fledged adj. 全面的、成熟完备的
conference n. 大会、会议

The comments underscore Huawei's growing role at the centre of China's drive to build a domestic AI computing industry, as US export controls restrict access to Nvidia's most advanced chips and semiconductor manufacturing equipment.

这些表态凸显华为在中国打造国产人工智能算力产业中日益核心的地位；与此同时，美国出口管制正限制中国获得英伟达最先进芯片及半导体制造设备。

underscore v. 强调、凸显
growing role 日益重要的角色
drive n. 推动、运动
domestic adj. 国内的、本国的
export controls 出口管制
restrict access to 限制获得……的渠道
semiconductor n. 半导体

"We cannot accept a destiny where we cannot control our fate being determined by others in terms of willingness to sell chips to China or not," he said. "No matter if it's for the Chinese government, industry in China, or for Huawei, it is certainly the way forward to try to push for full self-sufficiency for chips."

他说：“我们不能接受这样一种命运——在别人愿不愿意向中国卖芯片这一点上，我们无法掌控自己的命运。”“无论是对中国政府、对中国产业，还是对华为而言，推动芯片完全自给自足，无疑都是前进方向。”

destiny n. 命运
fate n. 命运、前途
be determined by 由……决定
willingness n. 意愿
the way forward 前进方向、出路
self-sufficiency n. 自给自足
push for 推动、争取

The company plans to have its Ascend 960DT chip ready in the first quarter of 2027, three quarters earlier than previously planned, and the 960PR ready in the third quarter, one quarter ahead of schedule, rotating chairman David Wang said.

轮值董事长汪涛表示，公司计划在 2027 年第一季度推出昇腾 960DT 芯片，比原计划提前三个季度；960PR 则定于第三季度就绪，比原计划提前一个季度。

have … ready 使……就绪、准备好
first / third quarter 第一 / 第三季度
previously adv. 先前、原先
ahead of schedule 提前于计划

The company is increasingly trying to compensate for limits on individual Chinese chips by connecting large numbers of processors so they can work together as one larger computing system. Huawei unveiled a new computing architecture designed eventually to allow as many as 1 million processors to operate together as a single system.

华为正越来越多地试图通过把大量处理器互联、使其作为一个更大的计算系统协同工作，来弥补单颗国产芯片的性能局限。公司公布了一种新的计算架构，最终目标是让多达 100 万颗处理器作为单一系统协同运转。

compensate for 弥补、补偿
individual adj. 单个的、个别的
processor n. 处理器
work together as 作为……协同工作
unveil v. 公布、亮相
architecture n. 架构
as many as 多达
operate together 协同运转
