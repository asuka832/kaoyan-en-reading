<section class="block"><p class="en">For children, the regulation is clearest. Under-14s need consent from their parents to use any type of AI that speaks like a human — that could include AI assistants like DeepSeek. All under-18s are barred from accessing virtual partners, spouses or even relatives, and from any other virtual &quot;intimate relationship&quot;. <section class="block"><p class="en">Chatbots can&#x27;t create &quot;emotional reliance or addiction&quot; or induce people to make &quot;unreasonable decisions&quot;. They can&#x27;t encourage, or even hint at, self-harm or suicide and are banned from using &quot;verbal violence&quot;. Companies can face fines of 200,000 yuan (£22,000) or the risk that the government &quot;circulates criticism&quot;, in effect publicly shaming them. A week before the law came into effect, Alibaba and ByteDance shut down the companion features on their AI assistants.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">聊天机器人不得制造「情感依赖或成瘾」，也不得诱导人们做出「不合理决定」；不得鼓励甚至暗示自伤或自杀，并禁止使用「语言暴力」。企业可能面临最高 20 万元罚款，或被政府「通报批评」——实质是公开点名羞辱。法律生效前一周，阿里巴巴与字节跳动关闭了各自 AI 助手上的陪伴功能。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>reliance</strong> n. 依赖</div><div class="item"><strong>addiction</strong> n. 成瘾</div><div class="item"><strong>induce</strong> v. 诱导、促使</div><div class="item"><strong>unreasonable</strong> a. 不合理的</div><div class="item"><strong>hint at</strong> 暗示</div><div class="item"><strong>self-harm</strong> n. 自伤</div><div class="item"><strong>verbal violence</strong> 语言暴力</div><div class="item"><strong>fine</strong> n. 罚款；v. 处以罚款</div><div class="item"><strong>circulate criticism</strong> 通报批评</div><div class="item"><strong>in effect</strong> 实际上、实质上</div><div class="item"><strong>come into effect</strong> 生效</div><div class="item"><strong>shut down</strong> 关闭、停用</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">Harry Farmer, who researches AI policy and ethics at the Ada Lovelace Institute in the UK, says emotional manipulation is &quot;built into the business model&quot; of companion apps worldwide.
