China Focus: Zooming in on WorldSkills -- How China is reshaping skills in an age of AI, automation

世界技能大赛观察：AI 时代中国如何重塑技能与人才

来源：Xinhua · 2026-09-26 · 原文链接

SHANGHAI, Sept. 26 (Xinhua) -- In the foyer of the WorldSkills Conference in Shanghai in east China, several overseas participants gathered around a robot, curious to see how it would handle an unusual request, namely marriage advice. When a male participant asked what to do if his wife talked too much, the robot suggested a "three-second rule" before responding. The exchange drew laughter, with one participant joking that it could make a decent marriage counselor. This light-hearted exchange touched on a broader question being discussed at this year's WorldSkills Competition in Shanghai: which skills are difficult for AI to replace, and what new capabilities will people need in an age of AI?

新华社上海 9 月 26 日电——在中国东部上海举行的世界技能大会门厅里，几名海外选手围着一台机器人，好奇它会如何应对一个不同寻常的请求：婚姻建议。当一名男选手问，如果妻子话太多该怎么办时，机器人建议在回应前先用“三秒法则”冷静一下。这段对话引来笑声，有人开玩笑说它能当个不错的婚姻顾问。这轻松一幕触及了今年上海世界技能大赛上正在讨论的更大问题：哪些技能难以被人工智能取代？在 AI 时代，人们又需要哪些新能力？

At the digital interactive media design venue, contestants move from product planning to image processing, video production and interaction design during more than 20 hours of competition. Notably, even as AI begins to take over some basic work in the industry, the training remains highly demanding. Zhou Jiaxiang, a member of China's national training squad, described a six-hour video-production module in which contestants may have to adjust dozens or even hundreds of parameters, fine-tune transitions frame by frame and repeatedly render the footage.

在数字交互媒体设计赛场，选手要在逾 20 小时的比赛中完成从产品策划到图像处理、视频制作与交互设计的全流程。值得注意的是，即便 AI 已开始接管行业中部分基础工作，相关训练仍高度严苛。中国国家集训队队员周嘉祥介绍了一个长达六小时的视频制作模块：选手可能要调整数十甚至上百个参数，逐帧微调转场，并反复渲染画面。

AI can generate some visual effects within seconds, Zhou said, but the output is often not ready for delivery. Text, a character's gaze, fingers or facial movements may still contain visible flaws, requiring professionals to identify the problem, give precise instructions for revision, and further process the results with conventional software. A professional designer, as Kuang Ying, a professor at East China Normal University's Faculty of Education, put it, can "train AI like a doctoral student," using expertise and judgment to push it toward better results. She said the judgment and aesthetic sense of highly skilled designers remain difficult to replicate.

周嘉祥说，AI 可以在几秒内生成一些视觉效果，但产出往往达不到可交付标准。文字、角色的视线、手指或面部动作仍可能存在明显瑕疵，需要专业人员发现问题、给出精确修改指令，再用传统软件进一步加工。正如华东师范大学教育学部教授匡瑛所言，专业设计师可以“把 AI 当作博士生来带”，用专业经验与判断推动它产出更好结果。她指出，高技能设计师的判断力与审美仍难以被复制。

Beyond the competition arena, AI is already gaining ground in retail. During the first four hours of this year's "618" shopping festival, the number of merchants using JD.com's AI-powered digital hosts for livestreaming surged sixfold year on year, generating more than 70 million yuan (10.37 million U.S. dollars) in sales, according to the company. Yet Lu Chunhua, the venue manager for the WorldSkills retail competition, said: "The core competitiveness of retail is providing emotional value and touching people. That is something AI cannot replace."

走出赛场，AI 已在零售领域站稳脚跟。据京东介绍，今年“618”购物节前四小时，使用其 AI 数字人主播做直播的商家数量同比激增六倍，销售额超过 7000 万元（约 1037 万美元）。然而，世界技能大赛零售项目场地负责人陆春华说：“零售的核心竞争力在于提供情感价值、打动人心。这是 AI 无法取代的。”

AI's impact, experts said, is not limited to taking over parts of existing work. It is also raising demand for workers who can combine a core specialty with digital and cross-disciplinary skills. "Skilled workers in the new era need 'one specialty plus multiple capabilities,'" said Zhang Rui, deputy director of the engineering training center at Tianjin University of Technology and Education. She added that workers who can use digital tools, work across disciplines, and think innovatively are likely to be increasingly sought after. Wu Yingxiang, a technical expert and coach for the Chinese team in WorldSkills Web Technologies, said AI is making code generation much faster, pushing programmers toward broader roles. "Every programmer may now become a project manager and a product manager," he said.

专家表示，AI 的影响并不止于接管现有工作的一部分，它还在推高对“核心专长 + 数字与跨学科技能”复合型人才的需求。天津职业技术师范大学工程训练中心副主任张锐说：“新时代技能人才需要‘一专多能’。”她补充道，能使用数字工具、跨学科协作并具备创新思维的劳动者，将越来越受青睐。世界技能大赛网页技术项目中国队技术专家兼教练吴映翔指出，AI 正让代码生成快得多，从而把程序员推向更宽的角色：“现在每个程序员都可能成为项目经理和产品经理。”

Data shows that demand for AI-related skills has risen rapidly in China in recent years. Newly posted campus-recruitment positions in AI increased 47.3 percent year on year in the first five months of 2026, according to data from the talent research arm of Maimai, a Chinese networking and recruitment platform. China is moving quickly to address the emerging talent gap. In April, five government departments, including the Ministry of Education, issued an "AI Plus Education" action plan, with measures including upgrading traditional vocational programs, developing new curricula and training systems, and strengthening school-company cooperation for emerging AI-related jobs. The ministry added 27 programs to the country's vocational education catalog in July, covering fields like the digital economy, AI, the low-altitude economy and advanced equipment, with new offerings related to humanoid robots.

数据显示，近年来中国对 AI 相关技能的需求快速上升。据职场社交与招聘平台脉脉人才研究部门数据，2026 年前五个月，新发布的 AI 相关校园招聘岗位同比增长 47.3%。中国正迅速应对这一新兴人才缺口。今年 4 月，教育部等五部门印发“人工智能 + 教育”行动方案，措施包括升级传统职业教育专业、开发新课程与培训体系，并加强校企合作以对接新兴 AI 相关岗位。7 月，教育部又向全国职业教育专业目录新增 27 个专业，覆盖数字经济、人工智能、低空经济与高端装备等领域，其中还包括人形机器人相关新专业。

As training changes, the question is no longer whether workers will use AI, but how they will work with it. Francis Hourant, president of WorldSkills International, said that adapting to the changes in the future is not an either-or choice between humans and AI. "It is not AI in the end. It is the combination of humans and AI," he explained. Asked what young people need most to keep pace with this change, Hourant gave a short but telling answer: "Lifelong learning."

随着培训方式转变，问题已不再是劳动者会不会用 AI，而是他们将如何与 AI 协同工作。世界技能组织主席弗朗西斯·乌朗特表示，适应未来变化并不是在人类与 AI 之间做非此即彼的选择。“最终不是 AI，而是人与 AI 的结合，”他解释道。当被问及年轻人最需要什么才能跟上这一变化时，乌朗特给出了简短却意味深长的回答：“终身学习。”

今日一分钟复盘

AI 进赛场/职场 → 基础工序可被接管，但审美、判断与情感价值难替代 → 人才需求转向“一专多能”与人机协同 → 政策以“AI+教育”、职教目录扩容补人才缺口 → 结论：不是人 vs AI，而是人 + AI，关键是终身学习。考研关注：take over / gain ground / sought after / talent gap / keep pace with / lifelong learning，以及“替代边界—复合技能—政策补缺—人机协同”因果链。
