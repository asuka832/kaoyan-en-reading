China nurtures emerging professions to fuel industrial upgrading

中国培育新兴职业：以人才供给支撑产业升级与 AI 转型

来源：Xinhua · 2026-09-21 · 原文链接

BEIJING, Sept. 21 (Xinhua) -- At an AI-focused training camp recently held by the Huawei ICT Academy at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), over 70 students underwent three days of integrated training and hands-on practice on AI fundamentals, AI operator development and open-source large models.

新华社北京 9 月 21 日电——近日，华为 ICT 学院在北京邮电大学举办人工智能专题训练营，70 余名学生接受了为期三天的综合培训与动手实践，内容涵盖人工智能基础、AI 算子开发与开源大模型。

undergo v. 经历、接受（训练/治疗等）
integrated adj. 综合的、一体化的
hands-on adj. 动手的、实践性的
fundamentals n. 基础、基本原理
operator n. （此处）算子；操作员
open-source adj. 开源的

This training camp was just one link in a joint talent-cultivation chain involving BUPT and Chinese tech giant Huawei, which has been deepening industry-education integration. Such initiatives provide students with opportunities to engage with cutting-edge technologies and gain hands-on experience via real-world projects, according to Gao Peng, deputy dean of the Ye Peida School of Innovation and Entrepreneurship at BUPT.

该训练营只是北邮与科技巨头华为共建人才培养链条中的一环；华为正不断深化产教融合。北邮叶培大创新创业学院副院长高鹏表示，此类举措让学生有机会接触前沿技术，并通过真实项目积累实践经验。

initiative n. 举措、倡议

cutting-edge adj. 前沿的、尖端的
deputy dean 副院长

Such AI-talent cultivation efforts echo China's recent announcement of 11 new occupations and 23 new work types, reflecting how AI-driven economic and industrial development is increasingly steering education and employment in new directions. Among these new work opportunities, five jobs related to the digital economy are highlights, including digital twin engineering technician, embodied intelligent robot application technician and industrial product digital modeler.

此类 AI 人才培养举措，呼应了中国近期公布的 11 个新职业与 23 个新工种，反映出人工智能驱动的经济与产业发展正日益把教育与就业引向新方向。在这些新机遇中，与数字经济相关的五个岗位尤为亮眼，包括数字孪生工程技术人员、具身智能机器人应用技术员与工业产品数字化建模师等。

echo v. 呼应、反映
occupation n. 职业
reflect v. 反映、体现
steer v. 引导、驾驭
digital twin 数字孪生
embodied intelligent robot 具身智能机器人
modeler n. 建模师

The emergence of new occupations mirrors the accelerated iteration of the country's industrial structures and expanding gaps for talents equipped with new skills in the job market, especially in sectors such as AI, embodied intelligent robots and modern services. The added value of core digital economy industries accounted for more than 10.5 percent of China's GDP, with the core AI industry exceeding 1.2 trillion yuan (about 177.8 billion U.S. dollars) in scale, the Digital China Development Report (2025) showed.

新职业的出现，映射出产业结构加快迭代，以及就业市场对具备新技能人才缺口不断扩大——尤其是在人工智能、具身智能机器人与现代服务业等领域。据《数字中国发展报告（2025）》，核心数字经济产业增加值占 GDP 比重超 10.5%，核心人工智能产业规模已超 1.2 万亿元（约 1778 亿美元）。

However, talent gaps continue to expand. An official from the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security said China is expected to face a sizable shortage of workers with digital skills for some time. McKinsey & Company also forecasts that China will require 6 million AI professionals by 2030, but could face a shortfall of 4 million.

然而，人才缺口仍在扩大。人力资源和社会保障部一位官员表示，预计中国在一段时间内仍将面临数字技能劳动力的较大短缺。麦肯锡公司也预测，到 2030 年中国需要 600 万名人工智能专业人才，但可能短缺 400 万。

sizable adj. 相当大的
shortage n. 短缺
forecast v./n. 预测
professional n. 专业人才
shortfall n. 缺口、差额

In April 2026, the Ministry of Education and four other departments issued an "AI + Education" action plan, proposing to "make AI a public fundamental course in colleges and universities, compile course textbooks by disciplines and majors, and ensure that all students acquire AI knowledge." The plan also promotes colleges and universities to optimize talent-training programs of traditional disciplines and offer interdisciplinary AI courses.

2026 年 4 月，教育部等五部门印发《“人工智能+教育”行动计划》，提出“推动人工智能成为高校公共基础课，分学科专业编写课程教材，确保全体学生掌握人工智能知识”。计划还推动高校优化传统学科专业的人才培养方案，并开设跨学科人工智能课程。

optimize v. 优化
interdisciplinary adj. 跨学科的

China has been dynamically updating its occupational classification catalog. Since 2019, the country has issued eight batches of 121 new occupations. Looking ahead, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security said it will formulate national standards for the new occupations to guide vocational education, skills training and talent assessment, providing strong talent support for high-quality economic and social development.

中国一直在动态更新职业分类目录。自 2019 年以来，已发布八批共 121 个新职业。人社部表示，下一步将制定新职业国家职业标准，用以指导职业教育、技能培训与人才评价，为经济社会高质量发展提供有力人才支撑。

vocational education 职业教育
assessment n. 评估、评价
high-quality development 高质量发展
