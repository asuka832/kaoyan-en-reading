China's AI chipmakers raise prices as high-bandwidth memory shortage bites

中国 AI 芯片厂商因高带宽内存短缺大幅提价

来源：Reuters · 2026-09-10 · 原文链接
先自己啃英文。中文译文、生词注释、文末复盘默认折叠，点一下才展开。

Chinese AI chipmakers including Huawei Technologies and Cambricon have sharply raised prices for current and next-generation AI processors, three people familiar with the matter said, as soaring costs for high-bandwidth memory squeeze efforts to build domestic alternatives to Nvidia.

知情人士称，包括华为与寒武纪在内的中国人工智能芯片厂商，已大幅上调现有及下一代 AI 处理器价格，原因是高带宽内存（HBM）成本飙升，挤压了打造英伟达国产替代方案的努力。

sharply adv. 急剧地、大幅地
next-generation adj. 下一代的
familiar with 熟悉、知情于
soaring adj. 飙升的、飞涨的
squeeze v. 挤压、使承压
domestic alternative 国产替代方案

Huawei has lifted the indicated price of its Ascend 950DT accelerator card, which integrates an AI processor with memory and other components, to above 250,000 yuan ($37,255), two people said. The increase represents a jump of 20% to 50% from prices quoted to customers just two months ago, depending on contract terms.

两人表示，华为已将昇腾 950DT 加速卡的报价抬至逾 25 万元人民币（约合 3.73 万美元）；该卡集成 AI 处理器、内存及其他组件。按合同条款不同，这一涨幅较两个月前对客户的报价高出约 20% 至 50%。

lift v. 提高（价格）
indicated price 指示价、报价
accelerator card 加速卡
integrate v. 集成、整合
quote v. 报价
depending on 取决于

Beijing-based Cambricon has also repriced its next-generation chip, tentatively called the 690, at 20% to 30% above levels it indicated two months ago, two sources said. Smaller rivals MetaX and Iluvatar CoreX have made similar increases, the sources said.

知情人士称，总部位于北京的寒武纪也已重新定价其暂称 690 的下一代芯片，较两个月前指示价高出约 20% 至 30%。规模较小的竞争对手墨芯（MetaX）与天数智芯（Iluvatar CoreX）亦有类似提价。

reprice v. 重新定价
tentatively adv. 暂定地、初步地
rival n. 竞争对手
similar adj. 类似的

The price hikes illustrate how a worldwide shortage of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) is reverberating through China's AI industry as Beijing presses companies to replace Nvidia products with home-grown alternatives.

这些涨价表明，在北京推动企业以国产方案替代英伟达产品之际，全球高带宽内存短缺正冲击中国人工智能产业。

price hike 涨价、提价
illustrate v. 说明、表明
shortage n. 短缺
reverberate v. 回荡、产生连锁影响
press v. 敦促、施压促使
home-grown adj. 本土的、国产的

HBM is a crucial component for AI computing, consisting of vertically stacked memory chips that allow processors to access vast amounts of data at high speeds. The advanced HBM market is dominated by South Korea's SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics, and U.S.-based Micron Technology.

高带宽内存是人工智能计算的关键组件，由垂直堆叠的存储芯片构成，使处理器能高速访问海量数据。先进 HBM 市场由韩国 SK 海力士、三星电子以及美国美光科技主导。

crucial adj. 关键的、至关重要的
consist of 由……组成
vertically stacked 垂直堆叠的
access v. 访问、获取
vast amounts of 大量的
be dominated by 由……主导

Since Washington tightened controls on exports of certain advanced HBM products to China in December 2024, Chinese chipmakers have increasingly relied on grey-market channels to secure supplies, the sources said. Such HBM typically costs several times what buyers outside China pay, they said.

知情人士称，自华盛顿于 2024 年 12 月收紧对华部分先进 HBM 产品出口管制以来，中国芯片厂商越来越依赖灰市渠道获取供应。这类 HBM 的价格通常是中国境外买家所付的数倍。

tighten controls 收紧管制
export n./v. 出口
rely on 依赖
grey-market adj. 灰市的（非正规但非黑市渠道）
secure supplies 确保供应、拿到货源
several times 数倍

The price hikes increase the cost of building AI computing capacity and highlight a growing bottleneck for Chinese chipmakers, who benefited from a void in China's $50 billion AI chip market after U.S. export controls restricted the country's access to Nvidia's most advanced processors.

提价抬高了建设人工智能算力的成本，也凸显中国芯片厂商面临日益加剧的瓶颈。此前美国出口管制限制中国获得英伟达最先进处理器后，这些厂商曾受益于中国约 500 亿美元 AI 芯片市场留下的空白。

computing capacity 算力、计算能力
highlight v. 凸显、强调
bottleneck n. 瓶颈
benefit from 受益于
void n. 空缺、空白
restrict v. 限制

今日一分钟复盘

HBM 全球短缺 + 美对华出口管制 → 灰市供货价数倍 → 华为昇腾 950DT、寒武纪 690 等国产 AI 卡提价约两成至五成 → 算力建设成本上升，国产替代既有窗口也有瓶颈。考研关注：squeeze / reverberate / bottleneck / void / grey-market，以及“管制—短缺—提价—算力成本”因果链。
