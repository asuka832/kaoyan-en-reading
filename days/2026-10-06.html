Researchers develop high-efficiency NEV wireless charging system

科研人员研发高效新能源汽车无线充电系统

来源：Xinhua（新华社）· 2026-10-05 · 原文链接
先自己啃英文。中文译文、生词注释、文末复盘默认折叠，点一下才展开。

GUANGZHOU, Oct. 5 (Xinhua) -- Researchers from Chinese and British institutions have developed a wireless charging system for new energy vehicles (NEVs) with conversion efficiency exceeding 98 percent.

新华社广州10月5日电——中英两国科研机构的研究人员研发出一套新能源汽车（NEV）无线充电系统，其转换效率超过 98%。

institution n. 机构（科研/教育机构）
wireless adj. 无线的
new energy vehicle (NEV) 新能源汽车
conversion efficiency 转换效率
exceed v. 超过、超出（exceeding 现在分词作后置定语）

The system could support the large-scale use of autonomous vehicles and has potential in sectors such as rail transit, industrial robots and low-altitude aircraft.

该系统有望支撑自动驾驶汽车的规模化应用，并在轨道交通、工业机器人和低空飞行器等领域具有应用潜力。

large-scale adj. 大规模的
autonomous adj. 自主的；autonomous vehicle 自动驾驶汽车
have potential in 在……方面有潜力
sector n. 领域、行业、部门
rail transit 轨道交通
low-altitude adj. 低空的

A fully automated charging solution that requires no manual intervention is seen as key to the commercial rollout of autonomous vehicles. Inductive power transfer is widely regarded as an ideal route to such charging.

一种无需人工干预的全自动充电方案，被视为自动驾驶汽车实现商业化推广的关键。感应式电能传输被普遍认为是实现这种充电的理想途径。

automated adj. 自动化的
manual adj. 人工的、手动的
intervention n. 干预、介入
be seen as key to 被视为……的关键（key to 中 to 是介词）
rollout n. 推出、铺开
inductive adj. 感应的、电感的
be regarded as 被认为是……（= be seen as）
route to 通往……的途径

But mainstream ferrite-core solutions face challenges in efficiency, power density and thermal stability. Improving power density and thermal stability while maintaining high efficiency has been a core bottleneck for wireless charging technology.

但主流的铁氧体磁芯方案在效率、功率密度和热稳定性方面面临挑战。如何在保持高效率的同时提升功率密度与热稳定性，一直是无线充电技术的核心瓶颈。

mainstream adj. 主流的
ferrite n. 铁氧体
density n. 密度；power density 功率密度
thermal adj. 热的、热量的
stability n. 稳定性
while maintaining ... 在保持……的同时（while + doing 作状语）
bottleneck n. 瓶颈
动名词短语作主语 Improving ... while maintaining ... 整体作主语，谓语为 has been

Researchers from the Dongguan Institute of Materials Science and Technology in south China's Guangdong Province, under the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), worked with the City University of Hong Kong, the University of Cambridge and the Institute of Physics under the CAS to develop a 20 kW-class wireless charging system for NEVs based on nanocrystalline alloys, achieving conversion efficiency of 98.51 percent.

中国科学院下属、位于广东的东莞材料科学与技术研究所的研究人员，与香港城市大学、剑桥大学及中国科学院物理研究所合作，研发出一套基于纳米晶合金的 20 千瓦级新能源汽车无线充电系统，转换效率达到 98.51%。

under prep. 隶属于、在……下属
work with ... to do 与……合作做某事
-class ……级（20 kW-class 20 千瓦级）
based on 基于
nanocrystalline adj. 纳米晶的
alloy n. 合金
achieving ... 现在分词作结果状语，表示“从而达到……”

Their study, recently published in Nature Communications, describes a magnetic core structure resembling an overpass that can meet the long-term high-temperature requirements of wireless charging.

他们的研究成果近期发表于《自然·通讯》，描述了一种形似立交桥的磁芯结构，能够满足无线充电长期高温工况的要求。

recently published in ... 过去分词短语作插入语，修饰 study
magnetic adj. 磁的、磁性的
core n. 芯、核心
resemble v. 像、类似（resembling 作后置定语）
overpass n. 立交桥、天桥
meet the requirements of 满足……的要求
long-term adj. 长期的

The magnetic core strip produced by the team measures 440 mm long, 330 mm wide and 4 mm thick. Its thickness is only half that of traditional ferrite solutions, yet it can carry higher power density.

该团队制备的磁芯带材长 440 毫米、宽 330 毫米、厚 4 毫米。其厚度仅为传统铁氧体方案的一半，却能承载更高的功率密度。

strip n. 条、带（材）
measure v. 尺寸为……（measure + 数值 + long/wide/thick）
thickness n. 厚度
half that of ... 是……的一半（that 指代 thickness，避免重复）
yet conj. 然而、却

The system's performance is expected to be further optimized and charging efficiency improved. The Dongguan Institute of Materials Science and Technology is also pushing forward its industrial application.

该系统的性能有望进一步优化，充电效率也将进一步提升。东莞材料科学与技术研究所也在推进其产业化应用。

be expected to 有望、预计会
optimize v. 优化
charging efficiency improved 省略结构：= charging efficiency (is expected to be) improved
push forward 推进、推动
industrial application 产业化应用

According to the institute, the new system can be applied not only to autonomous vehicles but also extended to rail transit, industrial automated guided vehicles and low-altitude aircraft.

据该研究所介绍，这一新系统不仅可用于自动驾驶汽车，还可拓展至轨道交通、工业自动导引车和低空飞行器等领域。

according to 据……所说
be applied to 被应用于……
not only ... but also ... 不仅……而且……（连接两个并列谓语）
extend to 延伸、拓展到
automated guided vehicle (AGV) 自动导引车

In September, China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and eight other government departments announced the five-year plan for the intelligent connected NEV industry from 2026 to 2030. The plan proposes that by 2030, vehicles with autonomous driving functions will achieve large-scale application in China, with highly automated driving in scenarios such as highways, urban expressways and some urban roads.

9 月，中国工业和信息化部等九部门发布了 2026 至 2030 年智能网联新能源汽车产业五年规划。规划提出，到 2030 年，具备自动驾驶功能的汽车将在中国实现规模化应用，并在高速公路、城市快速路及部分城市道路等场景实现高度自动驾驶。

ministry n. （政府的）部
announce v. 宣布、发布
intelligent connected 智能网联的
propose that ... 提出……（宾语从句）
by 2030 到 2030 年（常与将来时/完成时连用）
with + n. + 介词短语 独立主格式的 with 结构，补充说明
scenario n. 场景
expressway n. 快速路、高速公路

这是一篇典型的科技新闻（说明文）：成果先行——中英团队研发转换效率超 98% 的新能源汽车无线充电系统 → 意义：支撑自动驾驶规模化，可拓展到轨道交通、机器人、低空飞行器 → 背景：全自动充电是自动驾驶商业化的关键，铁氧体方案存在效率/功率密度/热稳定性瓶颈 → 突破细节：纳米晶合金、立交桥式磁芯、厚度减半功率更高、效率 98.51% → 展望：继续优化与产业化 → 政策背景：2030 年自动驾驶规模化应用。考研关注：be seen/regarded as / key to / bottleneck / while maintaining / resemble / half that of / be expected to（含省略）/ not only ... but also / propose that，以及“成果—意义—难点—突破—展望”的科技文结构，阅读题常考“研究的意义”和“现有方案的不足”。
