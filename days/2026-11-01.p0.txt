<section class="block"><p class="en">Newport has established a cluster of semiconductor companies, with IQE in production since 1988 and the wider cluster is growing. An independent report for CSconnected, produced by Cardiff University&#x27;s Welsh Economy Research Unit, found the south Wales compound semiconductor sector supported 3,140 jobs across Wales in 2025, up from 2,748 a year earlier, a 14% increase. Of those, 1,914 were direct jobs in the industry, concentrated around Newport and the wider M4 corridor. The report said average salaries in the sector were about £66,000.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">纽波特已形成半导体企业集群：IQE 自 1988 年起投产，更广泛的集群仍在扩张。卡迪夫大学威尔士经济研究部为 CSconnected 撰写的独立报告发现，南威尔士化合物半导体产业在 2025 年支撑了全威尔士 3,140 个岗位，较前一年的 2,748 个增长 14%。其中 1,914 个为行业直接就业，集中在纽波特及更广阔的 M4 走廊一带。报告称，该行业平均年薪约 6.6 万英镑。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>establish</strong> v. 建立、形成</div><div class="item"><strong>cluster</strong> n. 集群、聚集地</div><div class="item"><strong>in production</strong> 投产、在生产中</div><div class="item"><strong>wider</strong> a. 更广泛的</div><div class="item"><strong>independent report</strong> 独立报告</div><div class="item"><strong>produce</strong> v. 撰写、制作（报告）</div><div class="item"><strong>compound semiconductor</strong> 化合物半导体</div><div class="item"><strong>sector</strong> n. 行业、部门</div><div class="item"><strong>support jobs</strong> 支撑/带来就业岗位</div><div class="item"><strong>up from</strong> 较……上升</div><div class="item"><strong>direct jobs</strong> 直接就业岗位</div><div class="item"><strong>concentrate around</strong> 集中在……一带</div><div class="item"><strong>corridor</strong> n. 走廊（交通/产业带）</div><div class="item"><strong>average salary</strong> 平均薪资</div></div></div></div></section>
