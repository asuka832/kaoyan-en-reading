<section class="block"><p class="en">Eradicating those errors is likely to be challenging. &quot;As things stand, I think the fundamental way these systems function makes it quite hard to imagine how they could become 100% reliable,&quot; says François Yvon. This is because probability is a crucial part of the way AI translates each word rather than another and what is probably correct is not the same as what is correct. Especially in the realm of something as subtle and surprising as language.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">根除这些错误很可能颇具挑战。「就目前而言，我认为这些系统的根本运作方式，使人很难想象它们如何能达到 100% 可靠，」François Yvon 说。因为概率是 AI 在每个词上做选择的关键部分——「很可能正确」并不等于「正确」。尤其在语言这般微妙又出人意料的领域。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>eradicate</strong> v. 根除、消灭</div><div class="item"><strong>challenging</strong> a. 具有挑战性的</div><div class="item"><strong>as things stand</strong> 就目前情况而言</div><div class="item"><strong>fundamental</strong> a. 根本的</div><div class="item"><strong>function</strong> v. 运作；n. 功能</div><div class="item"><strong>reliable</strong> a. 可靠的</div><div class="item"><strong>probability</strong> n. 概率</div><div class="item"><strong>realm</strong> n. 领域、范围</div><div class="item"><strong>subtle</strong> a. 微妙的</div><div class="item"><strong>surprising</strong> a. 出人意料的</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">He thinks a new generation of tech will be an improvement. &quot;We will see translation systems that are increasingly capable of evaluating what they do and correcting themselves using different AI tools simultaneously: one to do the translation, a second to criticize it and a third to produce a new version, for example.&quot; But perhaps the big moment will be when AI can show doubt. &quot;The real breakthrough won&#x27;t come until these systems are not merely capable of translating but also of telling us when they&#x27;re not sure,&quot; Yvon says.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">他认为新一代技术会有所改进。「我们将看到翻译系统越来越能评估自身产出，并用不同 AI 工具同时自我纠正：例如一个负责翻译，第二个批评它，第三个再出新版本。」但真正的大时刻，或许是 AI 能够表达怀疑之时。「真正的突破要等到这些系统不仅能翻译，还能告诉我们它何时不确定，」Yvon 说。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>generation</strong> n. 一代（技术/人）</div><div class="item"><strong>improvement</strong> n. 改进</div><div class="item"><strong>evaluate</strong> v. 评估</div><div class="item"><strong>simultaneously</strong> ad. 同时地</div><div class="item"><strong>criticize</strong> v. 批评、挑错</div><div class="item"><strong>version</strong> n. 版本</div><div class="item"><strong>breakthrough</strong> n. 突破</div><div class="item"><strong>not merely ... but also</strong> 不仅……而且</div><div class="item"><strong>doubt</strong> n. 怀疑；v. 怀疑</div><div class="item"><strong>not sure</strong> 不确定</div></div></div></div></section>
