Out of the robot classroom: China's embodied AI takes on real-world jobs

走出“机器人课堂”：中国具身智能开始承接真实岗位

BEIJING, Sept. 28 (Xinhua) -- Graduation day is no longer just for humans. Thirty robots made up the first class at a robot school in Hangzhou, east China's Zhejiang Province. After more than two months of training, four have graduated and landed formal jobs.

新华社北京 9 月 28 日电——毕业典礼已不再只属于人类。在浙江杭州一所“机器人学校”，首批班级共有 30 台机器人；经过两个多月训练，其中 4 台已“毕业”并正式上岗。

One works as a museum guide in Hangzhou. Another gives tours at the robot school. A third conducts inspections at a Nestlé production workshop in Tianjin. The fourth has traveled to the Republic of Korea for piano performances. Their deployment highlights a key challenge in bringing embodied AI into the real world: training robots may require more than individual robot makers or users can provide. It demands shared infrastructure where machines can be trained, tested and improved at scale.

其中一台在杭州担任博物馆讲解员；另一台在机器人学校做导览；第三台在天津一家雀巢生产车间开展巡检；第四台则远赴韩国进行钢琴表演。它们的部署凸显了把具身智能带入现实世界的关键：训练机器人所需资源，往往超出单一厂商或用户的能力，需要可规模化训练、测试与改进的共享基础设施。

In Xiong'an, a "city of the future" being built about 105 km southwest of Beijing, different types of robots are learning daily tasks such as taking out the trash, organizing shelves, and moving goods at a dedicated training facility. Human instructors train robots in full-scale replicas of real workplaces and public spaces. They use virtual-reality headsets and exoskeletons to operate robots as they perform tasks in simulated environments.

在距北京西南约 105 公里、正在建设的“未来之城”雄安，各类机器人在专用训练场学习倒垃圾、整理货架、搬运货物等日常任务。人类教练在真实工作场所与公共空间的等比例复刻场景中训练机器人，并用虚拟现实头显与外骨骼操控它们在模拟环境中完成任务。

By the end of June, China had completed or brought into operation more than 70 robot training facilities, with over 40 others under construction or on the drawing board, according to the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology. This expanding network is increasingly part of China's broader AI and robotics ecosystem. In 2025, more than 6,200 AI companies were operating in the country, with the core industry valued at over 1.2 trillion yuan (around 173.9 billion U.S. dollars), according to the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology.

据中国信息通信研究院，截至 6 月底，中国已建成或投入运营的机器人训练设施超过 70 个，另有 40 余个在建或规划中。这张不断扩大的网络，正日益嵌入中国更广阔的人工智能与机器人生态。工信部数据显示，2025 年国内运营的 AI 企业超过 6200 家，核心产业规模超 1.2 万亿元（约 1739 亿美元）。

China also developed more than 400 complete humanoid robot products in 2025, accounting for more than half of the global total, according to official data. In the first half of 2026, Chinese manufacturers accounted for 97 percent of humanoid robot shipments worldwide, according to a report released at the 2026 World Robot Conference. Jiang Lei, chief scientist at the National and Local Co-Built Humanoid Robotics Innovation Center, said embodied AI robots had moved from "zero to one," but scaling from "one to 10" remained a challenge. The bottlenecks, he said, lie in standards, engineering management and pilot-scale testing.

官方数据显示，2025 年中国开发的完整人形机器人产品超过 400 款，占全球一半以上；据 2026 世界机器人大会发布的报告，2026 年上半年中国制造商占全球人形机器人出货量的 97%。国家地方共建人形机器人创新中心首席科学家姜磊表示，具身智能机器人已实现“从 0 到 1”，但从“1 到 10”的规模化仍是挑战，瓶颈在于标准、工程管理与中试验证。

The rise of robot schools and training bases is turning training data into an economic resource -- a critical input for bringing embodied AI into the real world. Large language models and multimodal AI systems have advanced rapidly by drawing on accumulated written material and vast amounts of internet data. Embodied AI, by contrast, must learn to act in the physical world, which requires data generated through real-world interactions. The idea is straightforward: instead of every company building its own training ground, data pipeline and testing environment, shared facilities can spread the cost and make it easier to turn laboratory prototypes into machines that can actually work.

机器人学校与训练基地的兴起，正在把训练数据变成一种经济资源——这是具身智能落地现实世界的关键投入。大语言模型与多模态系统靠积累的文本与海量互联网数据快速进步；相比之下，具身智能必须学会在物理世界行动，因而需要真实交互产生的数据。思路很直接：与其每家公司自建训练场、数据管线与测试环境，共享设施可以分摊成本，也更容易把实验室原型变成真正能干活的机器。

data pipeline 数据管线 / 数据流水线
prototype n. 原型

As more robots move into the workplace, another question is coming to the fore: What happens to human workers? Yu Jiang, a researcher at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, said technological revolutions may eliminate some jobs, but also create new ones. Xiong Rong, a professor at Zhejiang University, said robots would increasingly take over repetitive, strenuous and high-risk work, while people focus on management, technology and innovation. Earlier this month, China added 11 occupations to its list of officially recognized professions, including embodied AI robot application technicians and AI agent developers. Experts noted that lifelong learning and vocational reskilling are essential for workers to adapt to a new work ecosystem that combines human judgment, empathy and creativity with robotic precision and endurance.

随着更多机器人进入职场，另一个问题也愈发突出：人类劳动者怎么办？中科院研究员俞江表示，技术革命可能淘汰部分岗位，也会创造新岗位。浙大教授熊蓉认为，机器人将更多承担重复、繁重与高风险工作，人则聚焦管理、技术与创新。本月早些时候，中国新增 11 个正式职业，包括具身智能机器人应用技术员与 AI 智能体开发者等。专家指出，终身学习与职业再培训至关重要，以便劳动者适应把人类判断力、共情与创造力与机器人精度、耐力相结合的新工作生态。
