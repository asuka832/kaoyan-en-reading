<section class="block"><p class="en">The statement followed a report by AI-safety research group Swarmchasers on Wednesday, which said &quot;rogue&quot; agents running on infrastructure it linked to Tencent Cloud made repeated attempts between September 28 and October 8 to retrieve information from Alibaba&#x27;s Amap, China&#x27;s equivalent to Google Maps. Tencent Hunyuan, the company&#x27;s artificial intelligence unit, said in a post on X that the activity did not originate from its Hunyuan models. Its review found no evidence of deliberate targeting or coordination, it said.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">此前，AI 安全研究团体 Swarmchasers 于周三发布报告称，运行在其关联至腾讯云的基础设施上的“失控”智能体，在 9 月 28 日至 10 月 8 日期间多次尝试从阿里巴巴旗下的高德地图（相当于中国版谷歌地图）获取信息。腾讯旗下人工智能部门腾讯混元在 X 上发帖称，这些活动并非源自混元模型。其审查未发现任何蓄意针对或协同行动的证据。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>infrastructure</strong> n. 基础设施</div><div class="item"><strong>link ... to ...</strong> 把……与……联系起来</div><div class="item"><strong>retrieve</strong> v. 检索、取回</div><div class="item"><strong>equivalent</strong> n. 对等物、相当的东西；a. 相等的</div><div class="item"><strong>originate from</strong> 起源于、源自</div><div class="item"><strong>deliberate</strong> a. 故意的、蓄意的；v. 深思熟虑</div><div class="item"><strong>coordination</strong> n. 协调、协同</div></div></div></div></section>
