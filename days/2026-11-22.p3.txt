<section class="block"><p class="en">But London&#x27;s AI safety ambitions face a number of obstacles. Gina Neff, professor of responsible AI at Queen Mary University of London, says one of the biggest challenges for researchers is gaining access to cutting-edge AI models before they are released to the public. &quot;It shouldn&#x27;t just be a few people in northern California who set the terms of the future for the rest of us,&quot; she says. She points to the UK&#x27;s track record of developing regulatory &quot;sandboxes&quot; that allow researchers and policymakers to test emerging technologies in controlled environments before they are deployed more widely.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">然而，伦敦在人工智能安全方面的雄心也面临诸多障碍。伦敦玛丽女王大学负责任人工智能教授吉娜·内夫（Gina Neff）说，研究人员面临的最大挑战之一，是在尖端人工智能模型向公众发布之前获得使用权限。“不应只由北加州的少数人来为其余所有人设定未来的规则，”她说。她提到英国在建立监管“沙盒”方面的既往成绩，这类沙盒允许研究人员和政策制定者在受控环境中测试新兴技术，然后再更广泛地部署。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>ambition</strong> n. 雄心、抱负</div><div class="item"><strong>face a number of obstacles</strong> 面临诸多障碍</div><div class="item"><strong>responsible AI</strong> 负责任的人工智能</div><div class="item"><strong>challenge</strong> n. 挑战</div><div class="item"><strong>gain access to</strong> 获得……的使用权/准入</div><div class="item"><strong>cutting-edge</strong> adj. 尖端的、前沿的</div><div class="item"><strong>be released to the public</strong> 向公众发布</div><div class="item"><strong>set the terms</strong> 设定规则、设定条件</div><div class="item"><strong>the rest of us</strong> 我们其余的人</div><div class="item"><strong>point to</strong> 指出、提及</div><div class="item"><strong>track record</strong> 既往成绩、过往记录</div><div class="item"><strong>regulatory sandbox</strong> 监管沙盒</div><div class="item"><strong>emerging technologies</strong> 新兴技术</div><div class="item"><strong>controlled environment</strong> 受控环境</div><div class="item"><strong>deploy</strong> v. 部署、投入使用</div><div class="item"><strong>more widely</strong> 更广泛地</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">Despite the challenges, many of London&#x27;s leading universities are also keen to keep pace - and responsibly. At Imperial College London, president Hugh Brady says &quot;AI is rapidly permeating every aspect of what we do. It&#x27;s embedded in our education programmes, driving and accelerating much of our research, and at the heart of many of our start-ups.&quot; But while he recognises AI&#x27;s enormous potential, he is also clear about the risks. &quot;The train has left the station,&quot; he says. &quot;All of this has to happen at greater speed than ever before.&quot;</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">尽管面临这些挑战，伦敦的许多顶尖大学仍希望与时俱进——而且要以负责任的方式。伦敦帝国理工学院校长休·布雷迪（Hugh Brady）说：“人工智能正在迅速渗透到我们工作的方方面面。它嵌入我们的教育项目，驱动并加速着我们的大量研究，也是许多初创企业的核心。”不过，尽管他承认人工智能潜力巨大，对风险也态度明确。“列车已经离站，”他说，“这一切都必须以前所未有的更快速度推进。”</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>despite</strong> prep. 尽管</div><div class="item"><strong>leading universities</strong> 顶尖大学</div><div class="item"><strong>be keen to</strong> 渴望、热衷于</div><div class="item"><strong>keep pace</strong> 跟上步伐、与时俱进</div><div class="item"><strong>responsibly</strong> adv. 负责任地</div><div class="item"><strong>permeate</strong> v. 渗透、遍及</div><div class="item"><strong>every aspect of</strong> ……的方方面面</div><div class="item"><strong>be embedded in</strong> 嵌入于</div><div class="item"><strong>education programme</strong> 教育项目</div><div class="item"><strong>accelerate</strong> v. 加速</div><div class="item"><strong>at the heart of</strong> 处于……的核心</div><div class="item"><strong>start-up</strong> n. 初创企业</div><div class="item"><strong>recognise</strong> v. 承认、认识到</div><div class="item"><strong>enormous potential</strong> 巨大潜力</div><div class="item"><strong>be clear about</strong> 对……态度明确</div><div class="item"><strong>The train has left the station</strong> 列车已经离站（比喻大势已成、无法回头）</div><div class="item"><strong>at greater speed than ever before</strong> 以前所未有的更快速度</div></div></div></div></section>

<h2>复盘</h2>
<p><strong>主旨：</strong>文章写伦敦正迅速成为欧洲人工智能安全中心：DeepMind、Anthropic 及约 50 家安全机构聚集于此，政府追加数亿英镑投入；汉娜·弗赖伊等对其“向善的伦理科技”路径表示乐观。同时，文章指出获取前沿模型等现实障碍，并以帝国理工校长“列车已经离站”收束，强调须在认清风险的同时加速、负责任地推进。</p>
<p><strong>结构：</strong>开篇指出伦敦早在人工智能成为头条常客之前，就已悄然打造安全中心，生态系统聚焦“造福而非危害” → 欧洲领先枢纽，DeepMind 与 Anthropic 落户，约 50 家机构专注安全 → 介绍 AISI、Apollo Research、LISA 及国王十字“人工智能走廊” → 警告升温并发出能否在全球真正发挥作用的设问，同时给出数亿英镑新投资 → 埃尔斯顿称伦敦不仅是参赛者，更有几分“裁判”意味，清晰边界有助于吸引人才 → 弗赖伊对比旧金山“竞次式超级资本主义”，称伦敦走伦理向善之路 → 内夫指出障碍：发布前难以获得尖端模型，并提及英国监管沙盒传统 → 帝国理工等高校将人工智能嵌入教育与研究，布雷迪在承认风险的同时以“列车已经离站”强调必须加速。</p>
<p><strong>考研词汇与搭配：</strong>long before、regular fixture、shape itself as、rather than、cause harm、be described as、be home to、establish a major base、according to、up to、state-backed、assess、potential for、act against、be set to、relocate、ramp up、genuinely、make a difference、on the global stage、ranging from ... to ...、beyond human control、not just ... but also、something of a、attract talent、remain optimistic about、punch above one's weight、on a different path from、race to the bottom、instead、for good、on our doorstep、face a number of obstacles、gain access to、cutting-edge、set the terms、track record、regulatory sandbox、emerging technologies、deploy、despite、be keen to、keep pace、permeate、be embedded in、at the heart of、recognise、enormous potential、be clear about、The train has left the station、at greater speed than ever before。熟词僻义：fixture（常客、固定出现的事物）、hub（中心、枢纽）、player（参赛者）、interest（利益）、worth（价值……的）、for good（向善的，而非“永远”）。</p>
<p><strong>阅读技巧：</strong>标题中的 urgent race 提示“紧迫”与“竞赛”两条线，阅读时应同时抓住伦敦的雄心与尚存的障碍。开篇 quietly、long before 与后文 ramp up、obstacles、The train has left the station 形成时间与程度对照。注意几组对比与隐喻：player vs referee（参赛者与裁判）；race-to-the-bottom hyper-capitalism vs ethical technology for good（竞次式超级资本主义 vs 向善的伦理科技）；set the terms of the future（谁来为未来设定规则）。第 4 段修辞问句 will these efforts genuinely make a difference on the global stage 易出主旨或态度题，后文投资与机构是部分回应，并非已经给出肯定答案。第 6 段弗赖伊 remain optimistic，第 7 段内夫提出挑战，第 8 段布雷迪既承认 enormous potential 又 be clear about the risks，属于平衡写法，不宜把全文简化为对伦敦模式的全盘赞美。习语 punch above its weight 意为“发挥超出自身体量的作用”；for good 在 ethical technology for good 中是“向善/有益”，不是“永远”。专有名词需对应：AISI、Apollo Research、LISA、King's Cross、Hannah Fry、Gina Neff、Imperial College London、Hugh Brady、DeepMind、Anthropic。</p>
