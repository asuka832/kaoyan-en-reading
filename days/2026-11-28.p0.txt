<section class="block"><p class="en">The developer has typically released Grand Theft Auto on PC long after its initial console launch – 19 months later in the case of GTA 5. This time it may not have that luxury – in part because of an apparent leak of files from an Xbox development build of the game, but also due to the rapid advance of AI coding platforms. These tools are being used to recreate or adapt existing games, sometimes within days, taking away the control developers have over their products.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">该开发商通常会在主机版首次发售后很久才推出 PC 版——以《侠盗猎车手 5》为例，间隔长达 19 个月。这一次，它未必还能享有这种从容——部分是因为据称有一份 Xbox 开发版文件泄露，同时也因为人工智能编程平台发展迅速。这些工具正被用来重制或改编现有游戏，有时只需几天，从而削弱了开发商对自己产品的掌控。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>typically</strong> adv. 通常、一般</div><div class="item"><strong>release</strong> v. 发布、推出</div><div class="item"><strong>long after</strong> 在……之后很久</div><div class="item"><strong>initial</strong> adj. 最初的、首次的</div><div class="item"><strong>in the case of</strong> 就……而言、以……为例</div><div class="item"><strong>luxury</strong> n. 从容、可奢侈享有的条件（熟词僻义：不只是“奢侈品”）</div><div class="item"><strong>in part because of</strong> 部分是因为</div><div class="item"><strong>apparent</strong> adj. 表面上的、所谓的（语气保留）</div><div class="item"><strong>leak</strong> n. 泄露</div><div class="item"><strong>development build</strong> 开发版本、开发构建</div><div class="item"><strong>due to</strong> 由于、因为</div><div class="item"><strong>rapid advance</strong> 迅速发展、快速进步</div><div class="item"><strong>coding platforms</strong> 编程平台</div><div class="item"><strong>recreate</strong> v. 重制、再现</div><div class="item"><strong>adapt</strong> v. 改编、适配</div><div class="item"><strong>existing</strong> adj. 现有的</div><div class="item"><strong>within days</strong> 在几天之内</div><div class="item"><strong>take away</strong> 夺走、削弱</div><div class="item"><strong>control over</strong> 对……的掌控</div></div></div></div></section>
