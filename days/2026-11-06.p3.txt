<section class="block"><p class="en">Addressing privacy concerns, the university said: &quot;Without authorization, our smart classroom system does not collect biometric information such as facial data or conduct facial recognition in teaching applications.&quot; It added that the system no longer collects head-up rate data. The university pledged to respect and protect students&#x27; privacy while using digital technologies to improve learning.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">就隐私担忧，校方表示：「未经授权，我校智慧教室系统不会在教学应用中采集人脸等生物识别信息，也不会进行人脸识别。」并补充称系统已不再采集抬头率数据。校方承诺在用数字技术改善学习的同时，尊重并保护学生隐私。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>address concerns</strong> 回应/处理担忧</div><div class="item"><strong>authorization</strong> n. 授权</div><div class="item"><strong>biometric information</strong> 生物识别信息</div><div class="item"><strong>facial data</strong> 人脸数据</div><div class="item"><strong>conduct</strong> v. 实施、进行</div><div class="item"><strong>teaching applications</strong> 教学应用场景</div><div class="item"><strong>no longer</strong> 不再</div><div class="item"><strong>pledge</strong> v. 承诺、保证</div><div class="item"><strong>respect and protect</strong> 尊重并保护</div><div class="item"><strong>digital technologies</strong> 数字技术</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">Gao Hang, vice-dean of the School of Education at Renmin University of China, said using head-up rates to judge students&#x27; engagement was &quot;one-sided&quot; and lacked academic support. Looking down, for instance, does not necessarily mean a student is not learning, as they may be consulting materials or taking notes, he said. &quot;Does a student have to stare at the teacher to be learning?&quot; Gao asked. He warned that relying on such indicators could lead universities to prioritize formalistic measures over assessing what students actually learn and internalize.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">中国人民大学教育学院副院长高杭表示，用抬头率判断学生投入度「片面」，且缺乏学理支撑。例如低头并不必然等于没在学——学生可能在查阅资料或记笔记。「学生非得盯着老师才算在学习吗？」高杭问道。他警告，依赖此类指标可能让高校优先追求形式化手段，而忽视评估学生真正学到并内化了什么。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>vice-dean</strong> 副院长</div><div class="item"><strong>judge</strong> v. 评判、判断</div><div class="item"><strong>one-sided</strong> a. 片面的</div><div class="item"><strong>lack academic support</strong> 缺乏学理/学术支撑</div><div class="item"><strong>for instance</strong> 例如</div><div class="item"><strong>not necessarily</strong> 不一定、未必</div><div class="item"><strong>consult materials</strong> 查阅资料</div><div class="item"><strong>take notes</strong> 记笔记</div><div class="item"><strong>stare at</strong> 盯着看</div><div class="item"><strong>rely on</strong> 依赖</div><div class="item"><strong>indicator</strong> n. 指标</div><div class="item"><strong>prioritize A over B</strong> 把 A 置于 B 之上；优先 A 而非 B</div><div class="item"><strong>formalistic measures</strong> 形式化举措</div><div class="item"><strong>internalize</strong> v. 内化</div></div></div></div></section>
<section class="block"><p class="en">AI can improve education in many ways, including boosting classroom interaction, enabling real-time quizzes and polls, and recommending reading materials. However, in this case, the technology is being used mainly for management and surveillance, Gao said. Li, a graduate student in Beijing, also criticized the system as &quot;formalistic&quot;. &quot;We were never asked for consent, yet our privacy is fully exposed,&quot; she said, arguing that the system creates an appearance of effective management without genuinely improving teaching or learning quality. &quot;At university, if the content is truly interesting or useful, students will naturally look up,&quot; she added.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">高杭指出，人工智能本可多方面改善教育，例如增强课堂互动、实现实时测验与投票、推荐阅读材料；但在本案中，技术主要用于管理与监控。北京一名研究生李某也批评该系统「形式主义」。「从未征求过我们同意，隐私却被完全暴露，」她说，并认为系统只是营造出有效管理的表象，并未真正提升教与学的质量。「在大学里，内容真有趣或有用，学生自然会抬头，」她补充道。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>boost</strong> v. 增强、提升</div><div class="item"><strong>classroom interaction</strong> 课堂互动</div><div class="item"><strong>real-time</strong> a. 实时的</div><div class="item"><strong>quiz / poll</strong> 测验 / 投票</div><div class="item"><strong>recommend</strong> v. 推荐</div><div class="item"><strong>reading materials</strong> 阅读材料</div><div class="item"><strong>in this case</strong> 在本案/此种情形下</div><div class="item"><strong>mainly</strong> ad. 主要地</div><div class="item"><strong>management and surveillance</strong> 管理与监控</div><div class="item"><strong>graduate student</strong> 研究生</div><div class="item"><strong>criticize ... as</strong> 把……批评为……</div><div class="item"><strong>formalistic</strong> a. 形式主义的</div><div class="item"><strong>consent</strong> n. 同意、知情同意</div><div class="item"><strong>be fully exposed</strong> 被完全暴露</div><div class="item"><strong>argue that</strong> 主张、认为</div><div class="item"><strong>appearance of</strong> ……的表象/样子</div><div class="item"><strong>effective management</strong> 有效管理</div><div class="item"><strong>genuinely</strong> ad. 真正地</div><div class="item"><strong>teaching or learning quality</strong> 教或学的质量</div><div class="item"><strong>naturally</strong> ad. 自然地</div><div class="item"><strong>look up</strong> 抬头；查阅</div></div></div></div></section>

<h2>复盘</h2>
<p><strong>主旨：</strong>南大否认智慧教室监控「抬头率」与人脸生物信息，回应网传截图与平时成绩挂钩传言；争议背后是 AI 课堂系统的隐私边界与教育价值——专家批评抬头率片面、形式主义，技术更宜用于互动与学习支持，而非管理监控。</p>
<p><strong>结构：</strong>总起否认（抬头率/人脸）→ 抬头率定义 → 争议起因（出勤周通知+截图+平时成绩传言）→ 项目投资与招标功能（人脸检测/识别/抬头率）→ 学生未事先征求、不适感 → 校方声明（改造目的+政府采购）→ 隐私回应（未授权不采集、已停采抬头率）→ 高杭：片面/缺乏学理/形式化 → 专家与学生：AI 应用错位+知情同意缺失。</p>
