China rising as global robotics powerhouse, industry leader says

业界领袖称：中国正崛起为全球机器人强国

来源：Xinhua · 2026-09-25 · 原文链接
先自己啃英文。中文译文、生词注释、文末复盘默认折叠，点一下才展开。

BERLIN, Sept. 24 (Xinhua) -- China is emerging as a global powerhouse in robotics, driven by the rapid adoption of robots across industries, a large and increasingly diverse domestic market and a national strategy supporting the development of robotics and artificial intelligence, said Susanne Bieller, Secretary General of the International Federation of Robotics (IFR).

新华社柏林 9 月 24 日电——国际机器人联合会（IFR）秘书长苏珊娜·比勒（Susanne Bieller）表示，中国正崛起为全球机器人领域强国，动力来自各行业对机器人的快速采用、规模庞大且日益多元的国内市场，以及支持机器人与人工智能发展的国家战略。

"From our last visits to China, it was really impressive to see the speed of adoption and the interest across a broad range of industries, but also among people," Bieller told Xinhua in an exclusive interview on Tuesday, coinciding with the release of the IFR's latest World Robotics report. The IFR, based in Frankfurt, Germany, represents robotics manufacturers and research institutes worldwide and publishes the World Robotics report annually, tracking global trends in industrial robot adoption.

比勒周二在接受新华社专访时说：“从我们最近几次访华看，机器人在各行业乃至普通民众中的采用速度与兴趣都令人印象深刻。”专访恰逢 IFR 最新《世界机器人报告》发布。总部位于德国法兰克福的 IFR 代表全球机器人制造商与研究机构，每年发布该报告，追踪工业机器人采用的全球趋势。

According to the IFR's newly released World Robotics 2026 report, China installed 354,000 industrial robots in 2025, up 20 percent from the previous year and accounting for 59 percent of global installations. The figure was nearly 60,000 units higher than the previous annual record, further consolidating China's position as the world's largest market for industrial robots. Its vast manufacturing base continues to generate strong demand for robots across a wide range of applications.

据 IFR 最新发布的《世界机器人报告 2026》，中国 2025 年安装工业机器人 35.4 万台，同比增 20%，占全球安装量的 59%。该数字比此前年度纪录高出近 6 万台，进一步巩固了中国作为全球最大工业机器人市场的地位。其庞大的制造业基础继续在广泛应用场景中催生强劲需求。

Bieller said several factors were driving demand for robots in China, including industrial modernization, demographic changes and the country's industrial policy and national strategy for robotics. "There are certain drivers for the demand for robots in China. First of all, the industrialization of the industry and the need for more technology to replace or encounter the demographic shift the country is facing in the years to come," she said. "There is also the strong industrial policy and the national strategy for robotics, bringing robotics into the market in China."

比勒表示，驱动中国机器人需求的因素包括工业现代化、人口结构变化，以及国家产业政策与机器人战略。“中国机器人需求有明确驱动。首先是产业工业化，以及用更多技术来应对未来数年人口结构转变的需要，”她说。“此外还有强有力的产业政策与国家机器人战略，正在把机器人引入中国市场。”

According to Bieller, the IFR sees China's 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-2030) as placing greater emphasis on technological self-reliance, the modernization of traditional industries and the application of artificial intelligence in the physical world. The Chinese industrial robot market also has considerable room for further growth. IFR projections indicate that the market could maintain average annual growth of around 5 percent to 10 percent through 2029.

比勒称，IFR 认为中国“十五五”规划（2026—2030）更加强调科技自立自强、传统产业现代化，以及人工智能在物理世界中的应用。中国工业机器人市场仍有可观增长空间。IFR 预测显示，至 2029 年该市场或可保持约 5% 至 10% 的年均增长。

China's domestic robot suppliers have also strengthened their position in the market. Their installations rose 15 percent in 2025 to 195,000 units. According to the latest IFR data, domestic suppliers accounted for about 55 percent of the Chinese market in 2025. "Chinese companies have grown in competitiveness over the last decade, and the domestic share has increased dramatically," Bieller said. She noted that the growth was not confined to a small number of industries, with "huge opportunities" in sectors that have not invested as heavily in automation as automotive.

中国本土机器人供应商也在强化市场地位。2025 年其安装量同比增长 15%，达 19.5 万台；IFR 最新数据显示，本土供应商约占 2025 年中国市场的 55%。比勒说：“过去十年中国企业竞争力提升，国内份额大幅增加。”她指出，增长并不局限于少数行业，在自动化投入不及汽车业的领域仍有“巨大机会”。

She cited logistics as one area with strong potential and said vehicles beyond passenger cars, as well as food, textiles and paper, could also see further growth in robot adoption. "Chinese companies have learned to develop robots that are just fit for the purpose," Bieller said, calling this a strength of Chinese suppliers in developing solutions tailored to specific markets and applications. During a visit to Shenzhen in April, Bieller visited electric vehicle manufacturer XPeng's factory and saw robots being used in vehicle production. She said XPeng was also developing quadruped and humanoid robots — "a customer of robots for their electric vehicle factory, but at the same time they are developing their own."

她举例物流是潜力较大的领域，并认为乘用车以外的车辆，以及食品、纺织与造纸等行业，机器人采用仍可继续增长。“中国企业已学会开发‘恰合用途’的机器人，”比勒说，并称这是本土供应商针对特定市场与应用定制方案的优势。今年 4 月她在深圳参观小鹏汽车工厂时，看到机器人用于整车生产；她还表示小鹏同时在开发四足与人形机器人——“既是电动车工厂的机器人用户，又在研发自己的产品。”
