<section class="block"><p class="en">&quot;Il pleut des cordes,&quot; say the French when it&#x27;s raining hard. &quot;It&#x27;s raining ropes,&quot; is the direct translation. It&#x27;s the kind of idiom which can trip up an AI translator. &quot;You can imagine the dilemma this throws AI into,&quot; says François Yvon, a research director specialised in automatic translation with the French National Centre for Scientific Research CNRS. He says a well-trained system will know that ropes are not literally falling from the sky and use the English phrase &quot;it&#x27;s raining cats and dogs&quot;. &quot;But is it going to find the equivalent when it&#x27;s translating into Indonesian?&quot; asks Yvon.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">法语里形容下大雨会说「Il pleut des cordes」。直译是「天上下绳子」，这类习语很容易绊倒 AI 翻译。「你可以想象这会让 AI 陷入怎样的两难，」法国国家科学研究中心（CNRS）专门研究机器翻译的研究主任 François Yvon 说。他指出，训练良好的系统会明白绳子并非真的从天上掉下来，并改用英语习语「it&#x27;s raining cats and dogs」。「可是译成印尼语时，它还能找到对应说法吗？」Yvon 问道。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>idiom</strong> n. 习语、成语</div><div class="item"><strong>trip up</strong> 绊倒、难住</div><div class="item"><strong>dilemma</strong> n. 两难、困境</div><div class="item"><strong>throw ... into</strong> 使……陷入</div><div class="item"><strong>specialised in</strong> 专攻、专长于</div><div class="item"><strong>automatic translation</strong> 机器翻译/自动翻译</div><div class="item"><strong>literally</strong> ad. 字面地、确实地</div><div class="item"><strong>equivalent</strong> n. 对等说法；a. 对等的</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">A comment on the weather is pretty low-risk, but more and more translation is being done by machines, and on topics with higher stakes than the weather. In April, Google said people are now translating one trillion words a month with Google Translate. That doesn&#x27;t count the others: DeepL, Microsoft, Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, not to mention made-to-measure corporate translation systems. Direct sales of machine translation (i.e. outside general AI subscriptions) were worth an estimated $1.4bn (£1bn) last year.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">评论天气风险很低，但越来越多的翻译正由机器完成，而且话题的利害远高于天气。今年四月，谷歌称人们如今每月用谷歌翻译处理一万亿个词。这还不包括 DeepL、微软、Claude、ChatGPT、Gemini，更不用说量身定制的企业翻译系统。机器翻译的直接销售额（即排除通用 AI 订阅以外）去年估计达 14 亿美元（约 10 亿英镑）。</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>low-risk</strong> a. 低风险的</div><div class="item"><strong>stakes</strong> n. 利害、赌注（复数）</div><div class="item"><strong>trillion</strong> n. 万亿</div><div class="item"><strong>not to mention</strong> 更不用说</div><div class="item"><strong>made-to-measure</strong> a. 量身定制的</div><div class="item"><strong>corporate</strong> a. 企业的、公司的</div><div class="item"><strong>subscription</strong> n. 订阅</div><div class="item"><strong>estimated</strong> a. 估计的；v. 估计</div></div></div></div></section>

<section class="block"><p class="en">&quot;For plenty of use cases, raw AI translation is now the right call: multiplying your costs by 100 to catch the last few percent of errors isn&#x27;t rational if you&#x27;re translating internal documents or product listings,&quot; says Jonathan Zimmermann, founder of OKA (Open Knowledge Association), a Swiss non-profit that translates Wikipedia articles into other languages. &quot;For an encyclopedia it&#x27;s different. Wikipedia runs on trust. A wrong fact can sit there for years and get cited by journalists and AI systems,&quot; he says.</p><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示中文" data-hide="隐藏中文">显示中文</button><div class="panel-body"><p class="zh">「对许多使用场景来说，直接用原始 AI 译文现在就是正确选择：若你在译内部文件或商品列表，为抓最后几个百分点的错误而把成本放大一百倍并不理性，」瑞士非营利组织 OKA（开放知识协会）创始人 Jonathan Zimmermann 说——该组织把维基百科条目译成其他语言。「百科全书则不同。维基百科靠信任运转。一个错误事实可以躺在那里好几年，被记者和 AI 系统引用。」</p></div></div><div class="panel"><button class="toggle" data-show="显示生词" data-hide="隐藏生词">显示生词</button><div class="panel-body"><div class="notes"><div class="item"><strong>use case</strong> n. 使用场景/用例</div><div class="item"><strong>raw</strong> a. 原始的、未经加工的</div><div class="item"><strong>the right call</strong> 正确的决定</div><div class="item"><strong>multiply</strong> v. 使成倍增加</div><div class="item"><strong>rational</strong> a. 理性的、合理的</div><div class="item"><strong>internal</strong> a. 内部的</div><div class="item"><strong>product listing</strong> 商品列表/商品页</div><div class="item"><strong>encyclopedia</strong> n. 百科全书</div><div class="item"><strong>run on</strong> 依靠……运转</div><div class="item"><strong>cite</strong> v. 引用</div></div></div></div></section>
