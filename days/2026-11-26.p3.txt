<section class="block"><p class="en">Speaking to the Today programme, on BBC Radio 4, Prof Sir Martin Sweeting, founder and executive chairman of Surrey Satellite Technology Ltd, said while the idea of data centres in space seemed &quot;technically feasible&quot;, whether it &quot;makes sense at scale yet&quot; still needed to be analysed. Being able to get the data from the network of satellites, he added, would depend on being able to use laser links from the ground to them. &quot;That has not yet been perfected to the level at which it would be able to support the huge amounts of data that are likely to be transferred backwards and forwards,&quot; The orbiting data centre concept is also being explored by other firms - including Google and Blue Origin - and is being pitched as a greener alternative to traditional centres, which require enormous amounts of power to run and water for cooling. An expert previously told the BBC that launching hardware into orbit remains expensive and that a growing quantity of space debris puts the physical hardware at risk.</p><div class="panel"><button <h2>复盘</h2>
<p><strong>主旨：</strong>美国联邦通信委员会批准 SpaceX 的 Starmind 计划，允许向近地轨道发射多达一百万颗卫星，以阳光供电、在太空处理人工智能数据；公司称这是应对地面数据中心阻力、满足超出现有地面能力之算力需求的高效方案，但竞争对手、天文学家与工程专家对其可行性、观测影响和碎片风险提出质疑。</p>
<p><strong>结构：</strong>首段点出“首例批准”→ 介绍阳光供电、处理人工智能数据并回传，以及 FCC 对“超级智能经济”的表述 → 亚马逊批评“雄心而非计划”，天文学家担心夜空观测 → 地面数据中心遭反对并被指影响中期选举，SpaceX 主张轨道方案更经济节能 → 卡尔与马斯克的正面表态 → 许可时间表（2033 年启动、2035 年完成 10%、2038 年完成 50%）及与星链的接力传输 → 斯威廷强调规模可行性与激光链路尚未成熟 → 美国天文学会表示失望，并带出谷歌、蓝色起源的同类设想以及费用与太空碎片风险。</p>
<p><strong>考研词汇与搭配：</strong>give sb the go-ahead、launch、of its kind、power、process data、known as、booming、however、rival firm、criticise ... as、lofty、ambition、rather than、the move、significantly、undermine、opposition to、a factor in、orbital、meet the demand for、growing demand、computing power、beyond、terrestrial、capability、in a statement、bold、frontier、computing infrastructure、ingenuity、reinforce、prosperity、asset、under、licence、deployment、no later than、be required to、via、low-Earth orbit、altitude、ranging between、relay、while、technically、feasible、make sense、at scale、depend on、perfect、to the level at which、be likely to、transfer、meanwhile、disheartened、dismayed、proposed、constellation、decades of、as well as、explore、pitch ... as、alternative to、enormous amounts of、remain、space debris、put ... at risk。熟词僻义：power（供电、驱动）、terrestrial（地面的）、perfect（完善）、licence（许可证）、asset（财富、资产）。</p>
<p><strong>阅读技巧：</strong>标题中的 approves、a million、data satellites 已点出“监管放行 + 超大规模 + 用途是算力而非通信”。注意对比：FCC / Carr / Musk 的 booming、bold new frontier、ingenuity，对亚马逊的 lofty ambition rather than a real plan、Sweeting 的 technically feasible 但未必 makes sense at scale yet，以及 AAS 的 disheartened and dismayed。数字要记清：a million、10,000（现有星链）、500-2,000km、November 2033 / 2035 / 2038。licence 条件下的 must begin、will be required to have deployed 是典型的法规语气。结尾的 greener alternative 与 expensive、space debris 形成对“把数据中心送上天是否更绿、更可行”的保留态度，态度题不宜只看成正面放行新闻。</p>
