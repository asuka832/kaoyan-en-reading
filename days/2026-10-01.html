As AI reshapes jobs, China's universities race to patch up traditional majors

人工智能重塑就业，中国高校用“微专业”加速补齐传统专业

BEIJING, Sept. 12 (Xinhua) -- Artificial intelligence (AI) is making an unexpected appearance in classrooms at some Chinese universities.

新华社北京 9 月 12 日电——人工智能（AI）正意外地出现在部分中国高校的课堂里。

At China Agricultural University, students from various academic backgrounds, including veterinary medicine, plant protection, sociology and accounting, are learning how to apply AI to their fields through "micro-majors" -- short, focused and flexible programs designed to complement their primary majors.

在中国农业大学，来自兽医、植保、社会学与会计等不同学科背景的学生，正通过“微专业”——短小、聚焦且灵活、用以补充主修专业的项目——学习如何把 AI 应用到各自领域。

As AI and other emerging technologies reshape industries, the skills employers seek are also changing, with demand growing for people who can combine expertise in a particular field with new technologies. A recent report by the Chinese career platform Maimai showed that AI-related job postings in China surged 8.7-fold year on year from January to April this year.

随着 AI 及其他新兴技术重塑产业，雇主所需技能也在变化：既懂某一领域专长、又能结合新技术的人才需求不断增长。中国职场平台“脉脉”近日报告显示，今年 1 至 4 月，国内 AI 相关岗位发布量同比激增 8.7 倍。

Yet traditional university majors can be slow to adapt. A new major typically takes four to five years to go from planning and approval to producing its first graduates, while technological advances in fields such as AI, integrated circuits, new energy and biomedicine are progressing on a quarterly or even monthly basis, said Zhang Duanhong, director of the education policy research center at Tongji University.

然而传统大学专业调整往往较慢。同济大学教育政策研究中心主任张端鸿表示，一个新专业从规划审批到产出首批毕业生通常要四到五年，而 AI、集成电路、新能源与生物医药等领域的技术进步却按季度甚至按月推进。

Micro-majors offer a more agile response. In March 2025, the Ministry of Education launched a nationwide initiative to enhance students' employment skills, under which universities were encouraged to offer 1,000 micro-majors or clusters of specialized courses, along with 1,000 vocational skills training courses. By May this year, 1,433 universities across China had offered nearly 10,000 micro-majors, attracting about 300,000 students.

微专业提供了更敏捷的应对。2025 年 3 月，教育部启动全国性提升学生就业技能行动，鼓励高校开设 1000 个微专业或特色课程集群，以及 1000 门职业技能培训课程。截至今年 5 月，全国已有 1433 所高校开设近 1 万个微专业，吸引约 30 万名学生。

Typically comprising three to eight core courses, the programs fit around students' regular studies, with classes held in the evenings, on weekends or during school breaks, and offered both online and in person. "Micro-majors are more flexible than traditional majors and allow universities to update what they teach more quickly as new technologies and industries emerge," said Wu Wei, deputy dean of the College of Geoinformatics at Zhejiang University of Technology.

这类项目通常由三到八门核心课组成，可嵌入学生日常学习安排：晚上、周末或假期开课，线上线下皆有。浙江工业大学地理信息学院副院长吴伟说：“微专业比传统专业更灵活，能让高校随新技术与新产业出现更快更新教学内容。”

Anhui Polytechnic University has introduced an AI micro-major tailored to emerging industries in the region, with companies providing real-world problems for students to work on. Of the 40 graduates from its first two cohorts, 85 percent went on to jobs in related fields. For education expert Zhang Duanhong, what matters is not how many micro-majors universities offer, but whether they are "relevant, practical and high-quality."

安徽工程大学开设了面向本地区新兴产业的 AI 微专业，由企业提供真实问题供学生攻关。前两届 40 名毕业生中，85% 进入相关领域就业。教育专家张端鸿认为，关键不在高校开了多少微专业，而在于它们是否“对口、实用、高质量”。
