<section class="block"><p class="en">Data centres are crucial to the AI boom. The vast warehouse-style buildings house the advanced computer chips and servers that are needed to run AI programmes such as Claude and ChatGPT. As of April there were about 3,000 data centres across the US, with another 1,500 in various stages of approval and construction, according to the Pew Research Centre.
